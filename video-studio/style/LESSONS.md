## assets
- [L2] Talking-head / on-camera people: (1) make each character once as a Higgsfield Soul (soul_2) image: that is her identity; (2) for each scene, edit that Soul image with GPT Image 2.6 on Higgsfield (gpt_image_2_6) to add only what the scene needs (holding the real product, the room, the outfit), keeping the same face; (3) lock that edited image as start_image and generate the take natively on Seedance 2.5 at 720p (the line in the prompt, plus one short voice sample per character as the voice reference so she sounds the same in every scene). Never a separate lip-sync pass (no Sync / lipsync model / talking-photo tool over a clip or still): it makes the people look stiff and AI. Casual, natural phone-video movement.  (MUST)

## text
- [L1] No disclaimer-type text on screen in any generated image, clip or edit: no 'AI illustration' / 'AI-generated' labels, no footnotes or footnote marks (*, †), no 'FDA' / 'not evaluated' / 'results vary' lines, no photo-credit captions. Disclosures go in the platform AI label and the post description, never in the video.  (MUST, checked)
