## Patterns across the winners
1. **Promise the result in the first second.** Every skin tag winner (#7, #182, #78, #156) opens on the tag drying
   and falling off or the before/after, not on a question (#349).
2. **One named mechanism, said the same way every time**: soaks in → ricinoleic acid → cuts off the blood supply →
   dries out → falls off (skin tags); "soaks in vs sits on top" (wrinkles). Show it with the #156/#189 3D animation.
3. **Iterate winners by swapping only the angle.** #304 and #305 copy #160 and #79 shot for shot with a new angle;
   #450 puts #316's angle into #107's format. New ideas are "winner format × winner angle".
4. **The offer image wins hooks**: #181 H2 ("50% OFF + A FREE BOTTLE"). Current offer copy: *Buy 1 Get 1 Free +
   30 nights or your money back*.
5. **Faces that convert get reused**: #303's presenter, the 50-year-old woman at the vanity (#160).
6. **Remakes ("Duplicate" briefs, 57 launched)** copy a proven outside ad closely; most of what they earn comes from
   following the reference exactly (see the review notes).
