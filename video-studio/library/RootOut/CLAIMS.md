## 🟢 FACT (keep current)
- 11 ml amber bottle, brush in the cap (nail-polish style), leak-proof seal; night cover dots; single-use mini files.
- Routine: File (after a warm shower, dry, 2 strokes, once or twice a week, bin the file) · Soak (brush on at bedtime,
  1 minute) · Cover (night dot, peel in the morning). "Never oil a wet wart." Keep going 14 days after it's gone.
- 1 bottle = 30 nights. Hands: often 1–2 bottles; feet and corns: 2; two people: 3.
- "0 acid", "No freezing, no pads, no acid", "Nothing to wear by day".
- Ingredients (adult): tea tree 25%, distilled lemon 8%, eucalyptus 5%, propolis 5%, green-tea catechins 1%, menthol 0.5%,
  thuja leaf oil 0.3% in sunflower/castor/light carrier oils + vitamin E. Label actives: Thuja occidentalis 6X,
  Phytolacca decandra 12C (HPUS). Kids 4+: tea tree 15%, distilled lemon 5%, propolis 5%, green-tea catechins 1%.
- "Natural oil" (on the label band). Smell: eucalyptus, "a scent and a tingle you can feel".
- Offer: $44 / $74 / $88, buy 2 get 1 free, free US shipping, 90-day money-back, no subscription.
- Not for: genital warts, moles, a spot that bleeds or changes colour, dry cracked heels, under 4s, bee allergy (propolis).
  Ask a doctor first: diabetic/numb/poor-circulation feet, pregnant, immunosuppressed.
