for people (and parents of kids) who have frozen the same wart again and again.

## Type
- **Headlines:** Newsreader (serif) SemiBold 600, tight (letter-spacing −0.025em, line-height 1.05). Italic Newsreader
  is used for pull quotes and the product name in "Freezing and acid vs *RootOut*".
- **Body / UI / captions:** Inter (400–700). Announcement bar: Inter Bold 12, slightly tracked, white on navy.
- **Wordmark:** the text "WilloMD" in Inter ExtraBold 800, letter-spacing −0.045em, navy. The site has **no logo image**:
  the box and bottle print "WilloMD" small in Inter above a large "RootOut™" (Inter Bold, navy). Ask for vector files.
- Both fonts are Google Fonts (OFL), free to use in renders.

## Look of the brand's own images
- Clean, bright, clinical-friendly: white and ice-blue backgrounds, soft shadows, rounded white tiles with navy line icons
  (snowflake = no freezing, flask = no acid, drop = no tears).
- Mechanism visuals: glossy 3D skin cross-section blocks (pink skin, yellow hard-skin layer, red root channel), amber oil
  glowing as it travels down. Split screen: FREEZING (left) vs ROOTOUT OIL (right), navy pill labels top, caption pill bottom.
- How-to: macro overhead of hands on a sole over a white towel; step pill at top ("1 File", "2 Brush", "3 Cover").
- Lifestyle: warm bedside lamp, night, cosy bedroom/bathroom; the bottle always on the nightstand/counter.
- Home "real results" photos are phone-shot style (sofa, bed, kitchen table, bath mat) but **AI-made** (see DO_NOT_USE).
- Nearly all imagery reads as AI/3D renders. Real product photos and real customer footage: not seen yet.

## Voice
Plain, short, second person, a little wry. Short declaratives, often two-line contrast headlines:
"Same Wart. / A Different Way In." · "The Bump Is Just the Tip. / Here's What's Under It." ·
"You didn't fail the freezing. It never got past the hard skin." · "You're Going to Bed Anyway. Give the Root 8 Hours."
Quotes real Amazon 1★ reviews of competitors (freeze kits, corn pads, roller-ball oils) as the enemy's own words.
Numbers as proof devices: "2 brush strokes · 8 hours under a dot · 0 acid · 14-day Root Rule", "We read 9,545 wart stories."
