## Patterns across the swipes (what I saw)
- **Kids:** every Amoils swipe shows kids' faces. The RootOut scripts never do (feet, hands, sleeves), so shot plans
  must build the same warmth from hands, voice and the parent's face.
- **Captions:** all swipes burn in captions (white rounded box, mid-frame, 2–6 words); the RootOut scripts say
  "On screen = hook + end card only". Decide with the client before editing; captions are standard for these formats.
- **Pace:** the pest/pet swipes cut every 1.4–2.3 s with VFX; the Amoils kid UGC holds one take for 10–35 s. Our long
  scripts (105–235 s) sit closer to the PestLab pace, so plan 1.5–3 s inserts under the talking parent.
- **Mechanism picture:** Amoils #004/#039/#023 already use a pink skin cross-section with roots, and a cream-on-armor
  macro. Our side cut + dandelion must look clearly different (our colours, our drops, no copied frames).
