## What makes it work
1. **The rename hook.** It tells you the thing you've been calling harmless has another name, then proves it with
   close-ups you recognise. Set 20's "you think it's a callus, look for tiny black dots" is the same move.
2. **Real, slightly gross close-ups carry the belief.** Every symptom line gets its own real skin shot, about 2 s
   each, with a slow push-in. Nothing is explained in the abstract.
3. **Your fix is the cause.** "Creams or powders… only make it worse" = our pumice and tweezers "made it worse".
4. **It spreads.** A fast montage of three body parts = our sister's toe + the study.
5. **One hidden mechanism with one metaphor image** (bulletproof vest) = our dandelion. Theirs is a still image with
   arrows; ours can be real dandelion footage.
6. **One ingredient, shown physically** (dropper, oil on skin) before the 3D kill shot.
7. **Pace.** A new picture every ~2 s, captions on every line, the voice never stops. The talking face is barely there:
   two on-camera moments, both different men; the rest is VO over inserts.
8. **Rapid proof stack at the end** (five faces in three seconds) then the offer.

framing ("I've seen clients"); showing a competitor's product (Desenex) by brand; kid faces (never, our rule).
