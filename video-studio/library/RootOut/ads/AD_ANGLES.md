Not tested winners: no past ads or performance data yet. These are the angles, hooks and beats the PDP is built on, so

## Audience (as the site writes it)
- People who have **frozen the same wart again and again** (clinic or home kit) and it came back.
- **Parents** of kids with hand/finger/plantar warts who fight bedtime or cried at the clinic ("No tears").
- **On-their-feet workers** with corns (nurses on 12-hour shifts, work shoes), runners, swimmers (pads slide off).
- Adults with plantar warts who avoid going barefoot, or hide a knuckle wart in photos.

## Beat skeleton (what the PDP's order implies)
1. Hook: freezing again / the bump is just the tip (0–3 s).
2. Problem: the root sits under hard skin, so it comes back.
3. Mechanism: File · Soak · Cover, oil soaks down (cross-section).
4. Routine proof: 2 minutes at bedtime, nothing to wear by day, kids OK (with sign-off).
5. What to expect: dries and darkens in days, hands weeks 2–4, feet 4–8, plus 14 days (timeline claims: CAUTION).
6. Offer + CTA: Buy 2 get 1 free, 90-day money-back, "Start my 60 nights".
