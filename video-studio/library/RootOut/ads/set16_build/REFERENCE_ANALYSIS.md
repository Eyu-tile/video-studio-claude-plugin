## What Set 16 takes from it (within the client's script and lessons)
1. **Mom takes become hand-held arm's-length selfies** (not propped on the table): close framing, natural drift, she
   glances down at his foot / at him and back to the lens on her own lines, small shrugs, a laugh on "Yep!".
2. **Natural delivery**: line in the prompt + a short Cora voice sample + a real-selfie motion reference (house L2),
   never locked to the exact VO file (that is what made her stiff in v1).
3. **His answers stay real but off-face** (L10): voice from just out of frame, his foot/hand reacting in her lap.
4. **❌/✅ captions** on the tried list (❌ sticky pads, ❌ freeze kit, ❌ apple cider vinegar) and on the timeline
   (✅ after a week, ✅ about two weeks, ✅ hasn't come back): the swipe's turning point, inside our word-by-word captions.
5. **Hook split-screen**: 0–2.5 s mom + a simple foot drawing with the wart marked (code-made), like the swipe's opener.
6. **Less polish on the mom**: slight under-exposure, phone noise, no music under her first lines (bed comes in after the hook).
7. Keep from the script: the b-roll per phrase (client lesson L2/L3), the dandelion + four plants, the offer and end card
   (the swipe has none; our script adds them).
