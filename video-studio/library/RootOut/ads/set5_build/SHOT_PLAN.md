## What makes Set 5 different from Set 1 (not a re-skin)
- **Diary device, not a confession:** every night opens on a big hand-lettered stamp "NIGHT 1 / 3 / 7 / 10 / 14" and a
  crayon X landing on the paper calendar taped by the bed (the swipe's "HOUR 12 / BY HOUR 72" stamps, in our look).
  The stamp is part of the scene (crayon on paper), so the "no captions" rule still holds.
- **Phone-on-the-nightstand framing:** one fixed low angle, lamp left, calendar right. Each night changes her top, his
  pajamas, the picture book, so the time jump reads without text.
- **Same foot, five states** (rough → dots paler → flatter → smooth), built from one locked foot frame so it is the same wart.
- **Real proof inserts:** phone screenshots of real pages (RootOut PDP, AAD) slide in for 1.5–2 s as a "she looked it up" beat.
- **New inside view:** Set 1's 3D skin block is reused only as a style reference; the drops, the door, the 8x gauge and the
  9 pm → 7 am clock are new clips that follow Set 5's own lines (Set 1 ran 9 pm → 5 am).

## Cast (locked once, reused in every shot)
- **Mom:** early-to-mid 30s, soft, tired-but-warm, no makeup look, hair down; talks low (he's falling asleep). Five tops,
  one per night. Voice: ElevenLabs v4 preset, a *different* voice from Set 1's Emily (pick from 3 samples).
- **Son:** ~6, only feet and hands. Plantar wart on the ball of the left foot. Pajamas change per night.
- **Room:** kid's bedroom at night, warm bedside lamp, nightstand with phone POV, paper calendar taped to the wall, picture books.
- **Testimonial moms:** 3 new faces + 3 new voices (none from Set 1).
