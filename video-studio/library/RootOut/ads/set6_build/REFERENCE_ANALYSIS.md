## Changes worth making (in order of impact)
1. **Pace up to ~3 w/s**: play her takes at 1.08–1.10x (pitch kept, lips stay in sync) and trim pauses inside a take to ≤ 0.25 s (Set 5 and Set 18 clients both asked for this).
2. **Break every on-camera hold over ~3.5 s** with a cutaway on its words, so she returns on the turns only:
   "nobody had to hold anybody down" → hands holding the knee/ankle (reuse a backup clinic clip, greyed) ·
   "It's a kids' formula, 4 and up" → bottle label close-up (real PDP photo) · "No freezing, no acid, no blister" → three quick struck-out shots ·
   "If your kid's limping… hard spot under his foot" → the limp + heel spot (new angles) · "four plants" → the four plants on the counter (library clip d87a55aa: real bottle + lemon, tea tree, pokeweed, white cedar on a counter).
3. **Acted reaction shots of our mom** (same identity, no lip-sync, silent Seedance 4 s each, ~28 cr): at night checking his foot with a phone light; head in hands at the counter after the freeze came back; sitting in the car outside the clinic; scrolling her phone in bed (for "I looked it up"). These carry the low moments the way the reference does.
4. Optional: a small "About 2 weeks" chip on "In about two weeks, it was gone" (the reference's "2 DAYS" move) — only if the client clears the claim on screen.
Keep: our captions (L5), no kid faces (L10), real bottle (L1), voice + music only (L7), no disclaimer text (L1).
