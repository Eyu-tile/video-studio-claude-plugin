## What makes it work
1. **The trial is told by the captions, not by faces.** "Person 1 / 2 / 3" is said and captioned. Every result is a nail close-up, so the viewer checks her own feet against each one.
2. **Losers first, then a long, concrete win.** The two failures take one line each. The winner gets six shots, each tied to a word (fading, softening, breaking down, new nail). The proof is the picture changing.
3. **The question turns the result into the mechanism.** "So what made it work where every other solution failed?" is the hinge, and the 3D animation answers it in one picture ("it hides underneath").
4. **Constant motion and macro texture.** A cut every 2.9 s, almost all extreme close-ups of the problem. Nothing static is held for more than about 3 s except the 3D animation.
5. **The guarantee has a confident twist** ("you won't need it"), and the ending moves from the problem to an identity (sandals, no more hiding your feet).
