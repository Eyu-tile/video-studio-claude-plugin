# Set 20 b-roll sources (real footage, free licences)

Search 22 Nov 2026. Files live in `video-studio/projects/rootout-set20/assets/broll/<beat>/`; JSON copy: `assets/broll/broll.json`; raw download log: `assets/broll/_ledger.jsonl`. Every file was looked at (videos: 4-6 frames each; photos: full contact sheet). Grades: **A** clean, usable as is; **B** usable part (see "usable"); rejects were deleted and are listed at the end.

Sources reached: Wikimedia Commons (images + webm), Mixkit, Coverr. Archive.org had nothing relevant (searched dandelion, weeding, pumice, callus, cryotherapy, wart, barefoot, limping). Pexels, Pixabay, Openverse and YouTube were blocked from this machine.

**Licence notes.** (1) Mixkit now ships most videos under the *Mixkit Restricted License*, which forbids advertising and company social posts; only items whose page says "Free" (Mixkit Stock Video Free License) were downloaded, which ruled out every Mixkit dandelion, pedicure, foot-massage, liquid-nitrogen and kids-on-grass clip. (2) Coverr: only items with `isPremium:false` and `isAiGenerated:false` were taken (Coverr also mixes AI clips into search). (3) Wikimedia CC BY / CC BY-SA need the credit line (in the description or an end credit); CC BY-SA also means any edited version of that still is shared under BY-SA. (4) Wart photos are amateur/medical photos: no brands visible, no blood; they are adult feet, not the boy's, so they work as "a close-up like this" inserts, not as his foot.
