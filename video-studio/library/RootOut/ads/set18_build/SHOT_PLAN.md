## Routing rule (per beat)
1. **Real footage first** (Mixkit free licence, Internet Archive, Openverse, Wikimedia CC): see `projects/rootout-set18/raw/stock/STOCK.md`.
2. **Real screenshots** where a fact is said, framed as a phone browser card (`make_web_cards.py` from Set 1).
3. **Code graphics** (free): citation cards, half-circle, calendar, side-cut skin, end card.
4. **Higgsfield** only for what can't be real: the dad on camera, kids' feet with warts, the RootOut bottle in hand.

## How it stays different from Set 1
Set 1 = tired mom, warm lamp bedroom, emotional. Set 18 = deadpan dad, **cool night kitchen** (fridge light + one pendant
lamp, blue-grey), dry comedy, hard cuts on the punchline, a held 1 s beat of silence after each joke. Recurring
**Cheerio** motif (ruler in row 4, eaten in row 19). Real **web proof cards** carry the numbers (PubMed, AAD), not
an AI doctor. New cast, new house, new graphics palette (navy/ice side cut, no Amoils-like 3D).

## Cast (locked once, reused in every shot)
- **Dad:** late 30s, gray T-shirt, reading glasses pushed up on his head, short beard, deadpan. Phone selfie at arm's length.
- **Son (~8):** shorts, feet/legs/hands only. One rough wart with black dots on the **left heel**, later a second beside it.
- **Daughter (~6):** pink pajamas, glitter polish; small hand with one spot **on a finger** (VO wins over the row 5 visual).
- **Doctor:** chest down only, white coat, no name or badge.
- **Places:** night kitchen (Cheerio bowl), couch edge, exam room, backyard at dusk / sunny, daughter's bedroom, bath mat.
