## #1 Duct tape (weak / mixed evidence)

| screenshot | source · URL | verbatim quote |
|---|---|---|
| `duct_aafp_little_evidence.png` | American Family Physician (AAFP), "Treatment of Nongenital Cutaneous Warts", 2011 · https://www.aafp.org/pubs/afp/issues/2011/0801/p288.html | "**Duct tape is a popular remedy for nongenital cutaneous warts, but there is little documented evidence of its effectiveness.** One flawed RCT including 61 patients showed positive response to application of duct tape compared with cryotherapy. ## "Roots"
No source checked (AAD, NHS, Cleveland Clinic, MedlinePlus, AAFP, CFP/PMC) says warts have roots; none of these
pages uses the word for warts. Nothing was captured for it. Do **not** put a source card next to "the root" lines:
the dandelion/root is the ad's metaphor, not a cited fact. (Several dermatology sources say the opposite, that warts
have no roots, so a "doctors confirm roots" card would be false.)
