## What makes it different from Set 1 (not a reskin)
- **Format:** a TikTok green-screen ranking video. The mom is generated on a flat green wall, keyed in ffmpeg, and
  composited over a **code-made animated foot cut-away** (hard cracked top band, grey wart, black dots, long white root,
  depth ruler, 4 pin slots). Every pin drop, the bump turning into three, the R.I.P. tombstone, the 9 pm → 5 am clock, and
  the "smooth skin, only the gold pin left" ending are drawn in code, frame-exact on the VO and free.
- **New cast and voice:** a wry, late-30s mom in a plain sweater (not Set 1's tired bedroom mom), a new ElevenLabs voice,
  and new rooms (TikTok desk setup, couch, bathroom counter, fridge).
- **Proof layer:** real web screenshots (AAD / NHS / PubMed-style lines, new sentences, not the Set 1 ones) slide in
  over each rank, framed as phone captures, and real CC footage is used wherever the internet has it (dandelion, duct tape,
  cryotherapy, pads). See `WEB_SOURCES.md` and `BROLL_PICKS.md`.
- **Clay drops:** the four plants as tiny clay drops with faces (brief), done in Seedance 3D claymation over our cut-away look.
