What changes from v1

One cut-away animation held for long stretchesA new picture at least every 3 s. Each shot shows the words being said. The 3D skin animation appears in short 1–3 s bursts only, mixed with real footage and b-roll.
Mom keyed out of a green screen (rough edges)No cut-outs. The mom is filmed whole inside Higgsfield in a real home (kitchen, car, sofa), and appears in short beats like a UGC host.
Product sometimes small, covered or croppedEvery product shot is made in Higgsfield with the real bottle as reference. Label always facing camera, cap and base in frame, hands never over the label. Any frame that breaks this gets re-rolled.
2:30–2:37 end card cut off the bottle's capNew CTA: the real product page on a phone, a three-bottle shot, the offer circled like the reference, then a clean end card made in Higgsfield with the whole bottle and wide margins.
Glitches (edge fringing, keyed hair, zoomed-in takes)Every clip comes from Higgsfield at one size, with no compositing. Each clip is checked frame by frame before the edit, and every talking clip is checked word for word against the script.

How the reference is cut (SoFyre #035, measured)

Pace

  • 44 shots in 78.6 s, median 1.6 s
  • Hard cuts only, many with a slow push-in or pull-out
  • The first cut lands at 1.4 s

Structure

  • A hook banner sits on screen for the first 9 s
  • Each rank opens on a big number and name card
  • Every line is shown literally (“bad air freshener” = mint in a jar)

Mechanism and proof

  • One or two short “inside the body” 3D shots per rank
  • Product held, turned and placed, always fully in view
  • Ends on the real checkout page with the offer circled

Rules for every frame

top UI
buttons
caption + CTA bar

Text and headlines

  • All titles, rank cards, source cards and captions sit inside the safe zone: clear of the top bar, the right-hand buttons and the bottom 20%
  • Captions: 2–4 words, lower middle (as in the reference)
  • Hook banner and rank cards: upper third
  • Disclaimers (†, *) small, above the caption line

Product

  • The real RootOut bottle in every product shot (front + duo images as reference)
  • Label front-on and readable, never mirrored, nothing covering it
  • Whole bottle in frame, cap included, with margin on every side

Kids and claims

  • No child faces: feet, hands, sleeves, backs of heads only
  • Paper-towel test stays labelled “AI placeholder · proof needed”
  • Source cards quote the page unchanged, with no logos and nothing implying endorsement
