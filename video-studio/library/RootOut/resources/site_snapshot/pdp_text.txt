FAQ
How do I use it?
File the hard skin once or twice a week, brush it on at bedtime, cover it with a dot, and peel it in the morning. Two minutes.
Why a brush-on oil?
A thin oil soaks down once the hard skin is filed; cream sits on top. The brush puts it on the spot, not the skin around it.
Will it work if freezing didn't?
Freezing burns the top. The root sits under a hard layer of skin, so it grows back. The oil goes on after you file that layer down, and soaks to the root every night.
Does it work on corns?
Yes. A corn has a hard core under a cap of hard skin; pads take the cap and leave the core. File the cap, brush the oil on and cover it. It's for corns, seed corns and calluses with a hard centre, not dry, cracked heels.
How many bottles do I need?
Hands: often 1–2 bottles. Feet and corns: 2, because of the 14 extra days. Two people, or a wart and a corn: get 3.
Is it safe for my kid?
The Kids 4+ oil has no acid, no freezing, no menthol, no eucalyptus and no thuja oil. Patch-test on the inner arm first. Not for under 4.
Who shouldn't use it?
Not for genital warts, moles, or a spot that bleeds or changes colour: see a doctor. Diabetic, numb or poor-circulation feet, pregnant or immunosuppressed: ask your doctor first. Skip it with a bee allergy (it has propolis). Not for dry, cracked heels.
