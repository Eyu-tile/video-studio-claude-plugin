## The one-line brief
A belief-change ad, not a feature list: **hook in ≤2 s (≤1.5 s on Meta) → one old→new belief shift → one proof (an app screen that matches the spoken metric, the product, a testimonial or a graph) by ~5 s → CTA.**

## Structure template
| Beat | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook / scroll stopper | 0–2 s | Hook text on frame 1 (static, large, high contrast, 1–3 words in red/blue). The first frame alone explains the concept. |
| Problem / old belief | 2–6 s | Relatable pain: plateau, scale lies, belly fat 40+, exhausted despite sleep, data overload. |
| Mechanism / new belief | ~5–15 s | Pod: hand + foot sensors → fat/muscle/hydration/segmental trends. Band: wearable + app → interpreted sleep/stress/recovery, Prof AI. |
| Proof | by 5 s, reinforced | The **matching** app screen, product close-up, before/after trend, social proof. |
| Offer / CTA | last 3–5 s | UGC: spoken CTA + native caption. Polished: CTA card (small logo → headline → subhead → button). |

## Hooks that won (Notion winner library, by spend)
- **Fake apology / reverse psychology**: "We apologize to anyone who bought the Hume Health Pod at 40% off, because we lied." ($498k, ROAS 1.52); "To the 520 men over 40 who bought the HealthPod yesterday…" (hook 31.9%, hold 33.2%); "To the 1000 Ozempic users who bought the body pod yesterday, we're sorry." (ROAS 2.27).
- **Age reversal / identity**: "Here's how I got younger getting older…" (hook rate **54%**).
- **Story / podcast**: "Craziest story from our customers." (hook 47.6%); "A 41-year-old man just thought he needed more coffee."
- **Hidden risk**: "How to destroy visceral belly fat when you're over 50" (hook 46.5%).
- **Enemy / industry**: "They charge $200 for a DEXA scan that takes 5 minutes, just because they can."
- Winner medians (video): Pod hook 40% / hold 28% / ROAS 1.47; Band hook 39% / hold 26% / ROAS 1.58. 46 winners total, $4.23M recorded spend. Formats: mostly UGC testimonial/UGC, then podcast style, explainer, social-proof statics.
- Approved hook styles: shock/disbelief, fake apology, direct callout, before/after implication, desire ("younger", "stronger", "energy").

## Visual language
- A visual change every 1–3 s: zooms (subtle, handheld feel), crop jumps, b-roll, app overlays, body render, product close-ups. Intentional, not patterned.
- Lo-fi UGC stays raw and native (TikTok font, no shadows, iOS emojis). Polished = Red Hat Display / Canela with clean hierarchy.
- Colors: Hume Blue **#1882FF** / **#31ABE8**, black, white. Red or blue on 1–3 key words only; the rest of the caption is white.
- ≤2 text styles per video. Generous margins on headlines.
- Captions: balanced two lines, never crossing a cut, mobile-readable, inside safe zones.

## Audio
- Music: native and emotional, cut to its rhythm, always under the VO.
- VO: Fish Audio. Clear pauses at sentence ends. Use punctuation for emphasis. Test "Hume Pod / Hume Band 2.0 / Prof AI" pronunciation first.

## Deliverables
First review: a 9:16 full edit + a render of all hooks (≥3 brief hooks). Finals after approval: 1:1, 9:16, 16:9 by default (4:5 when the task Size field asks). **16:9 house method:** the approved 4:5 (or 9:16) centred at full height, over the same video enlarged to fill the frame, blurred and slightly darkened; every graphic stays inside the centre column (producer, Muscle Imbalance 16:9). Hume Ad Namer file names, no spaces, same base name across revisions.

## Measured targets (166 previously shipped Hume ads, medians; see `02_previous_ads/README.md`)
| Lever | Target | Range seen (p25–p75) |
|---|---|---|
| Length | ~70 s for the main 9:16 cut | 49–90 s |
| First cut | by **~1.8 s** | 1.2–2.8 s |
| Average shot | **~2.0 s** (median shot 1.8 s) | 1.8–2.4 s |
| Cut rate | **~4.8 cuts / 10 s** | 4.0–5.5 |
| VO | **~220 wpm**, wall-to-wall (talk ratio 0.94) | 198–240 wpm |
| Cut placement | **~80 % of cuts on a word start** | 71–87 % |
| SFX | whoosh/hit on **~55–60 % of cuts** | 45–70 % |
| Captions | 1–3 word cards at **~67 % of frame height**, line height **~3 % of frame** (≈58 px on 1920); white pill/black text or white with outline; key word flips to a red (problem) / green (proof) / blue (brand) pill | y 0.64–0.72 |
| Headline | top banner in a coloured box for the first ~7–10 s (93 % of ads) | |
| Music | bed ~120 BPM under the VO | 112–129 BPM |
| Loudness | **−19 LUFS integrated**, LRA ~3 LU; keep true peak ≤ −1 dBTP (a quarter of past ads hit 0 dBTP, so don't copy that) | −20 to −16 LUFS |

Do not copy the claims: 100 of the 166 ads contain red-line wording (DEXA equivalence, disease, heart attack, "medical-grade"). See `02_previous_ads/COMPLIANCE.md`.
