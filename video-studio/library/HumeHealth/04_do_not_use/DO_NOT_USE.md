# Hume Health: DO NOT USE (preliminary)

Status: **seeded from the Notion SOP and Proof & Claims Bank only.** The client's own do-not-use guideline (usually guideline videos on Drive) has not been shared yet. When it arrives, it will be transcribed here and these rules updated.

## Hard claim red lines
1. Never say Hume products **diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease**. (Restricted)
2. Never say Hume Band **predicts heart attacks or acute medical events**. (Rejected / "Do not say")
3. Never present Hume Band as a **medical device or clinical diagnostic tool**. (Restricted)
4. **No DEXA replacement or equivalence claim.** Pod = convenient at-home *trend* tracking between scans.
5. **Accuracy or competitor-comparison claims** need approved substantiation. (High risk)
6. Don't position Pod as diagnostic. Use trend and composition language.
7. Biological/metabolic age and stress-aging language only where substantiated. Don't overclaim.
8. GLP-1 / Ozempic creative: monitoring fat and muscle trends only, with **no treatment claims**.
9. In-review claims: verify before paid use: "45+ body metrics", "no required monthly subscription", "HSA/FSA eligible where applicable", Band sleep/stress/recovery claims.
10. Claim grammar: the product *detects / tracks / reveals / surfaces*; the user *prevents / protects / decides / acts*.

## Visual and product rules
- Use the correct hardware generation (Hume Pod vs Hume Band 2.0). Never mix Pod and Band in one concept unless the brief says ecosystem.
- **The app screen must match the spoken metric.** Never show a generic dashboard as a stand-in for the metric being discussed.
- No logo-first or slow intros. No animated-in scroll-stopper text.
- No pretty b-roll that proves nothing. No over-produced TikTok-style UGC.
- Text and faces never outside the safe zones. Never crop the forehead, eyes or mouth.
- No more than two text styles. No shadows on organic/lo-fi captions. iOS emojis only.
- Gradients only for tests and backgrounds.
- AI tool name: always "Prof AI".
