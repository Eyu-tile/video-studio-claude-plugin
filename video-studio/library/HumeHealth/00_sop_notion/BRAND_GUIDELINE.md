# Hume "Creative Concept Guideline" (brand kit on Drive)

Source: Drive folder "Creative Guideline" (1-8G-9yj5k77daUSQchrSs70raw9aRGHe): `Creative Guideline _ Hume.pdf` (9 image-only pages, read visually 2026-11-12) plus the font and logo files in the same folder.

## Core idea
"Hume isn't just about data; it's about the biological truth. We believe health isn't a destination, it's a high-performance craft. We bridge the gap between clinical precision and the luxury of self-knowledge." Tagline: **Data-led. Human-proven.**
Brand personality: elite, professional ("for people who take themselves seriously"). Precise, smart, balanced, aspirational, personal, active.

## Visual language
- **UI as art:** take the data visualisations from the Hume app and treat them like gallery prints, with glows, blurs and elegant gradients (e.g. Biological Age card, "35 Years Young", "LOW RISK" chips).
- **Close-ups of the devices.**
- **Candid elite:** behind-the-scenes shots of athletes or high performers in "rest mode", not just "action mode".
- **NO bright-lit gym photos.**

## Typography
- **Primary font, headlines/text: Red Hat Display** (Regular / Medium / Bold). OFL licence, files in the Drive folder.
- **Secondary font, highlights: Canela Text** (Thin / Light, italic for the emphasised word: "Built by *scientist*. Proven by *champions*."). Note: the Drive copies are **Trial** fonts and are not licensed for published ads; ask the client for licensed files before using Canela.
- **TikTok Display** (Regular / Medium / Bold) is in the same folder for native UGC captions.

## Colours
- Primary: Hume Blue **#1882FF**, light blue **#31ABE8**, deep navy (~#0F1E3D), black, white.
- Secondary: slate blue-grey, mid blue, dark blue, pale cream, light grey.
- Gradients: sage→cream, blue→pale blue, navy→slate, pale blue, light grey. "We need to make use of sophisticated gradients for high-def quality."

## Logotype
- Primary logo "✕ HumeHealth™" (general assets). Logo mark "✕" (social media). Secondary "✕ HumePod™" (Body Pod assets) and "✕ BAND" (Band assets).
- SVGs (white and black versions) are in the Drive folder `Logos`.

## Notes for editors
- Use the product logo that matches the product: ✕ BAND for Band 2.0 ads, ✕ HumePod for Pod ads.
- The guideline's own example statics include claims ("Caught silent risks before they became symptoms", "saved me $1,200+ a year on DEXA scans"). These conflict with the Claims Bank red lines; check `../04_do_not_use/DO_NOT_USE.md` before reusing any copy.
