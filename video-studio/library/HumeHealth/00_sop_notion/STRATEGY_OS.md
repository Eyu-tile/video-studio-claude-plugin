## Competitors and alternatives
- DEXA scans: authoritative but expensive and infrequent. Position Pod as at-home trend visibility *between* checks.
- Bathroom scale: cheap but blind to fat/muscle/water. Core belief shift: "weight isn't the whole story".
- Oura / Whoop-style wearables: subscription fatigue and metric overload. Band = interpretation + value.
- Cheap smart scales: foot-only, met with accuracy skepticism. Explain the hand-and-foot mechanism.
