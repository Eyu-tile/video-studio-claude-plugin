**Department:** Creative / Marketing
**Owner:** Creative Strategist 
**Ops Partner:** EF (Marketing Coordinator)
---

## Purpose
Ensure in-house editors and designers receive **clear, complete, product-specific briefs** to reduce revisions, queue congestion, and delays.
---

## Scope
Applies to all **in-house creative production** (UGC edits, statics, polished assets) for:
- **Hume Band**
- **Hume Body Pod**
---

## Tools
- Google Docs (briefs)
- Trello (task management)
- Google Drive (asset storage)
- Band Creative Sheet
- Body Pod Creative Sheet
---

## Roles & Responsibilities
- **Creative Strategist**
  - Creates & owns brief quality
  - Separates Band vs Pod briefs
  - Defines success criteria & references
- **EF**
  - Assigns briefs in Trello based on workload
  - Tracks progress & status
  - Logs approved assets into sheets
- **Editors / Designers**
  - Execute strictly against brief
  - Flag blockers early in Trello
- **Ani / Jeff**
  - Final approval & feedback in Trello
---

## Procedure

### 1. Brief Creation (Critical Fix Area)
Each brief **must be its own document** and **product-specific**.
**Required sections (non-negotiable):**
1. **Product** (Band or Body Pod — one only)
1. **Objective** (net-new test, iteration, or support)
1. **Creative Type**
  - UGC edit
  - Static
  - Polished / high-production
1. **Primary Angle**
1. **Hook direction**
1. **B-roll guidance**
  - Explicit examples (e.g. app screens, lifestyle, talking head)
1. **Reference links**
1. **Do / Don’t**
1. **Delivery priority** (High / Medium / Low)
1. **Deadline**
> 🔴 If B-roll guidance is missing → revisions increase → queue slows.
---

### 2. Assignment & Execution
1. EF assigns brief in **Trello** based on editor/designer availability.
1. Editor completes asset.
1. Editor moves card to **“Approval”** column.
---

### 3. Feedback & Revision
- Feedback happens **only in Trello**
- Must be:
  - Specific
  - Actionable
  - Consolidated (avoid drip feedback)
---

### 4. Approval & Handoff
1. Approved asset is marked final in Trello.
1. EF logs asset in:
  - Band or Body Pod Creative Sheet
1. Media buyers pull from sheet.
---

## Known Failure Points (Now Solved)
- ❌ Combined Band + Pod briefs
- ❌ Vague B-roll guidance
- ❌ Missing priorities
- ❌ Editors guessing intent
