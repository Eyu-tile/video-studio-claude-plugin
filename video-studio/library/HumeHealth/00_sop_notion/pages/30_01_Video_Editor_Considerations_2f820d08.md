### Main Editing Errors to Improve
**Voice Pauses & Sentence Endings**
There needs to be a clear pause when a sentence ends.
For example, “this is the Hume Health Pod” should **sound finished**, not like the phrase continues into the next line. The pacing of the voiceover is key for clarity and impact.
- ▸ **ELEVEN LABS GUIDE**
  - **Use Commas**: Add commas to break up phrases or clauses and make speech flow more naturally. Example: “I love, you know, walking in the park every morning.”
  - **Add Periods for Pauses**: Use periods to give your voice a natural pause between thoughts or sentences. Example: “I need to go. I’ll be back soon.”
  - **Use Exclamation Marks**: Exclamation marks give emphasis and energy. Example: “That’s amazing!”
  - **Add Ellipses (Three Dots)**: Ellipses indicate a pause, hesitation, or unfinished thoughts, making speech feel more conversational. Example: “I was thinking... maybe we could go for dinner?”
  - **Incorporate Dashes (–)**: This introduce sudden changes in thought or to add an element of surprise or emphasis. Example: “I was just about to leave – oh, wait!”
  - **Add Question Marks**: Question marks give the voice curiosity or an inquisitive tone. Example: “What do you think about that?”
  - **Use Parentheses for Side Thoughts**: Parentheses are helpful for adding additional thoughts or commentary, making the voice feel more personal. Example: “I didn’t know (but I was excited) that they were coming.”
  - **Add Em Dash: **The em dash can emphasize certain ideas or add emphasis between clauses. Example: “The food was great – but the company was even better.”
  - **Add Emphasis (Capitalized Words)**: Occasionally capitalizing certain words for emphasis can add a more informal, organic touch. Example: “That was the BEST decision ever.”

**Caption & Visual Editing Consistency**
When editing, it’s very important to consider the following:
- **Emoji usage:** Always use **iOS emojis**, keep them **small**, and place them intentionally according to the text.
