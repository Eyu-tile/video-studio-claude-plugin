# Hume Editing Bar
Hume creative is belief-change first, not feature-first.
Good Hume edits:
- Hook in the first 2 seconds.
- Make one belief shift clear.
- Use one main proof point or metric.
- Use app UI, body map, product, testimonial, or graph when it proves the claim.
- Match the app screen to the actual spoken metric: biological age gets the biological-age screen; Prof AI insights get the relevant Prof AI screen. Ask for missing proof assets rather than substituting a generic dashboard.
- Confirm the correct hardware generation. Use Hume Pod and Hume Band 2.0 in copy as appropriate; call the AI Prof AI.
- Keep Pod, Band, and ecosystem concepts separate unless the brief says otherwise.
Avoid:
- Feature dumping.
- Logo-first or slow intros.
- Pretty b-roll that does not prove anything.
- Over-produced TikTok-style UGC.
- Unsupported medical claims.
Claim rule:
- Product can detect, track, reveal, surface, and help users see patterns.
- User prevents, protects, decides, and acts based on the data.

# Editing Rules

## Hook
- Hook text appears immediately.
- First frame must make the concept clear.
- Avoid hook text that animates in too slowly.

## Pacing
- Visual change every 1-3 seconds.
- Use zooms, crop jumps, b-roll, app UI, product close-ups, and proof overlays intentionally.
- Music supports the edit; it does not overpower the VO.

## Captions
- Caption starts and finishes on the same clip.
- Text is short, balanced, typo-free, and mobile-readable.
- Use red or blue only to highlight 1-3 important words.

## Safe Zones
- Text stays inside platform safe zones.
- Faces stay fully visible.
- Preview on mobile scale before export.

## Style
- Use no more than two text styles per video.
- Lo-fi UGC stays native and organic.
- Polished concepts can use CTA cards and cleaner hierarchy.

## Voiceover
- Sentence endings need clear pauses.
- Use Fish Audio instead of ElevenLabs for new voiceovers and VO revisions.
- Use punctuation to control phrasing and emphasis. Generate a short test before the full script and check Hume Pod, Hume Band 2.0 and Prof AI pronunciation.
- Choose a library voice or clone an approved creator recording. Save the voice reference and downloaded VO with the task assets for revisions.

# Manage Your Work in Notion

## Where to work
Open your personal editor Office, then the assigned task in [[page:e3984a1d-d85a-4f23-bd41-aaeaaf982508]]. These are production tasks. The [[page:46af4e5c-0f6e-47e4-98c5-641463f24e11]] holds brief planning and source context; its older status labels do not define your production workflow.
If your Office is missing or shows the wrong tasks, ask Poorvi / assigned ops to check your invite, permissions and Video Editor filter. Do not create a separate task database.
Do not add, rename or remove database status options yourself. Ask Poorvi / assigned ops to make workflow changes and check the linked Office filters and automations; changing status options has previously broken task visibility.
When handing work to a reviewer or VA, confirm the task’s Assignee reflects the next person responsible and mention them in the handoff comment. Keep the Video Editor role property populated so editor attribution is retained. If the next owner is unclear, ask the assigned strategist / ops before changing it.

## Before you start
The strategist / ops should populate the task. Check:
- Task Overview / File ID and the approved script or Source Brief.
- Video Editor, Assignee, Creative Strategist, Creative Director and Virtual Assistant where relevant. A name in the title does not replace the role properties.
- Product and exact generation. The database label “Band” must be clarified in the brief as Hume Band 2.0 or the explicitly requested generation.
- Priority, Due Date, Size, Creative Style, required hooks and variations.
- Raw footage link, proof assets, references and offer / CTA.
- One consolidated feedback owner and the final approver.
Ask for missing information in task comments and mention the responsible person. Confirm your draft ETA with a date, time and timezone. Do not remove or silently move an assigned deadline.

## Status flow
Use these exact labels from the production database. Status supports multiple selections; remove stale editing labels as work moves forward. Keep other relevant workflow labels only when they still apply.
Status | When to use / next owner
Ready for Editor | Brief and assets are ready, editor assigned. Editor confirms pickup and ETA.
Edit In-Progress | Editor has started the draft or an agreed revision.
Needs Feedback - CS | Self-QA complete; accessible [Frame.io](http://Frame.io) share link posted in the task; strategist mentioned for review.
Needs Feedback - CD | Ready for creative director review when required by the task. Mention the named director.
Edit In-Revision | Changes requested. Editor acknowledges consolidated feedback and gives a revision ETA.
Edit Needs Resize | Main direction approved; remaining sizes or variations need finishing.
VA to upload | Required approvals recorded; all requested exports and project handoff complete; final delivery links posted; assigned VA mentioned.
Blocked / Missing files/footage | Work cannot continue. Explain what is missing, who can unblock it and the deadline impact.
Moved to Sheet | Ops / VA has completed the downstream tracker handoff. Editor does not use this to imply an ad is live.
“READY TO UPLOAD TO META” is older SOP wording. The current production handoff label is “VA to upload.” The production database has no “Approved” status; record explicit approval in the task comments before handoff.

## Draft, feedback and final delivery
1. Confirm pickup in the Notion task and set Edit In-Progress.
1. Edit 9:16 first unless the brief specifies another starting size.
1. Self-QA. For first review, deliver two renders: one containing all requested hooks / scroll stoppers, and one full body edit with a selected hook.
1. Upload to [Frame.io](http://Frame.io) using the agreed base file name. Stack revisions on the same asset; keep old versions.
1. Use [Frame.io](http://Frame.io)’s **Share** option. Open the share link and confirm it shows the correct creative, current version and reviewer access. Do not paste the browser’s internal project URL.
1. Post the draft share link in the Notion task comments, state what is included and mention the reviewer. Set Needs Feedback - CS or Needs Feedback - CD according to the task’s review order.
1. Apply consolidated feedback. If comments conflict, ask the feedback owner to resolve them before editing. Keep timestamped edit notes in [Frame.io](http://Frame.io) and decisions / ETA in Notion.
1. After approval of the main direction, finish all requested hooks, variations and sizes. Default final sizes are 1:1, 9:16 and 16:9 unless the task explicitly requests otherwise. Reframe and QA each size.
1. Upload approved final exports and transferable project/source files to the assigned delivery folder. For CapCut or software without transferable projects, retain the editable project locally and document that location and retention arrangement in the task.
1. Record approver, approval date, final folder link, delivered hooks / sizes and project-file location in a final handoff comment. Use the existing final-delivery field if provided by the task template; otherwise keep the final link clearly labelled in that comment. Update Access Link to the accessible review / delivery link as appropriate, then set VA to upload and mention the assigned VA.
Do not overwrite the master briefs tracker’s Final link with a draft. That field is used for the actual script brief page URL.

# File Naming
Use [Hume Ad Namer](https://hume-ad-namer.netlify.app/) and its [Help Guide](https://hume-ad-namer.netlify.app/help). No spaces.
Current structure:
`FORMAT_PRODUCT_PARENTANGLE_SUBANGLE_HOOK_ASSIGNEE_EDITOR_TAG_DDMMYY_YYYYWeek##_RATIO`
Product codes: POD, BAND, or PODBAND for an explicitly briefed bundle. Hook: 2-5 words in CamelCase. Optional editor / tag fields follow the naming tool.
Use a distinct final name for each ratio and hook variation. Keep the same base name across revisions of that exact creative so [Frame.io](http://Frame.io) versions remain together. Confirm naming changes with the task owner if an assignment changes.
Older names and examples in linked SOPs are historical. Use the current naming tool for new work.

# First Assignment
Isabel / the assigned strategist should select one low-risk edit with:
- One approved brief, one product and a clear product generation.
- Accessible raw assets, app proof, references and delivery folders.
- Named editor, strategist, feedback owner and final approver.
- Due Date, agreed draft ETA with timezone, required hooks and final Size values.
First review: one 9:16 full edit plus one render of all requested hooks / scroll stoppers. Use at least three brief-provided hook options; if fewer are supplied, ask the strategist before inventing new copy.
Finish the other requested sizes after the main direction is approved. Default final delivery is 1:1, 9:16 and 16:9 unless explicitly briefed otherwise.
Practice the whole handoff: Notion pickup, status update, [Frame.io](http://Frame.io) share link, reviewer mention, revision, recorded approval and final delivery.
