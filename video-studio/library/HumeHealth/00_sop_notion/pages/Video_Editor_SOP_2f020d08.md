## What You’re Editing
We create **short-form performance videos** for paid social (Meta, TikTok, YouTube Shorts).
Typical formats:
- UGC-style talking head
- Voiceover + b-roll
- Podcast clips
- AI / hybrid UGC
- High-end videos
**Core rule:**
> If it doesn’t hook in the first 2 seconds, it doesn’t ship.

## Platforms & Specs
- ▸ Aspect Ratios
  - **9:16** → Primary (Meta, TikTok)
  - **4:5** → Feed
  - **16:9** → Youtube
- ▸ Length
  - Depending of the brief
- ▸ Safe Zones
  Please take in consideration the safe zones. Our videos are viewed on mobile with UI overlays (captions, CTAs, platform buttons). If text or faces sit outside the safe zone, **performance drops.**
  [IMAGE: image.png] attachment:2a61735f-2d91-4894-86f3-30deb4e3c6e4:image.png

### Text & Captions
  - All text **must live inside the safe zone**
  - Avoid placing captions:
    - Too low (covered by captions/CTA buttons)
    - Too high (covered by username or platform UI)
  - Always preview on **mobile scale** before exporting

### Faces & Subjects
  - Faces must remain **fully visible at all times**
  - Eyes should sit roughly in the **upper-middle** of the frame
  - Never crop:
    - Forehead
    - Eyes
    - Mouth

- Visual change every **1–3 seconds**
- Never let a static frame sit too long
Examples of **visual change**:
- Zoom in / out
- Crop jump
- Cut to b‑roll
- App chart overlay
- Body render / product close-up
  - Please, take in consideration that you **DON’T** have to overuse them.

### Captions
- ▸ **CAPTIONS STYLE**
  Take in consideration when we’re doing a native TikTok video captions must feel organic (at least the concept is different than a storytelling)
  [IMAGE: Screenshot 2026-01-22 at 6.05.44 PM.png] attachment:50a29afc-1ba4-4efc-95a2-db6b7221b3e3:Screenshot_2026-01-22_at_6.05.44_PM.png
  Color is a **tool to control attention**, not decoration.
  When creating **hook captions**, we intentionally use color to **direct the viewer’s eyes to what matters most in the first seconds**.

### Approved Use of Color
  - **Red / Blue** are our primary attention colors
  - Use them when something:
    - **Requires urgency**
    - Signals a **problem**
    - Highlights a **warning, mistake, or consequence**
    - Emphasizes what the viewer needs to notice immediately
  Examples of what to color:
  - Problem words
  - Negative states (pain, risk, mistake)
  - Key trigger phrases (“STOP”, “WARNING”, “DON’T”, “THIS IS WHY”)

### How to Apply Color
  - Color **only 1–3 words**, never the full sentence
  - The rest of the text should remain **white**
  - High contrast is mandatory (must be readable on mobile)
  **Correct:**
  > “This is why your **METABOLISM** is failing”
  [IMAGE: Screenshot 2026-01-22 at 6.12.19 PM.png] attachment:0d6a856b-7a92-4d63-9b95-dc7e7c97549e:Screenshot_2026-01-22_at_6.12.19_PM.png
  [IMAGE: Screenshot 2026-01-22 at 6.12.36 PM.png] attachment:3d8bdb92-171a-4d5f-9827-2e2e79864f12:Screenshot_2026-01-22_at_6.12.36_PM.png
- ▸ **RULES**
  - **Each caption must start and finish on the same clip**
    - Never allow text to begin on one clip and finish on another
  - Prefer **balanced line lengths** for readability
  **Bad:**
  > I always struggle with dark
spots
  **Good:**
  > I always struggle 
with dark spots
  - Keep captions short, punchy, and easy to read on mobile

### Hook Playbook
Every video must start with a **hook** in the first 2 seconds.
Approved hook styles:
- Shock / disbelief
- Fake apology
- Direct callout
- Before/after implication
- Desire-based ("younger", "stronger", "energy")
Rules:
- Hook text appears immediately
- High contrast color
- Large font size

## Quality Checklist
Before uploading, do your own** QA**. And check if:
- [ ] The deliverable follows all the notes that the brief provided
- [ ] No typo’s in any of the captions
- [ ] Voiceover is correct and easy to follow
- [ ] The hook is attention grabbing
- [ ] Use of color on headline or impactful words
- [ ] The pacing is correct, as per the target audience
- [ ] The video has a CTA
- [ ] The video speaks to a specific audience we want to target
- [ ] The video is backed by research
- [ ] The text is easy to read
- [ ] The UI does not cover the text or any important parts of the video
- [ ] Video is requested in sizes 1080x1350 & 1080x1920
- [ ] The file naming convention it's the correct one
