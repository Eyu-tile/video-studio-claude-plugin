# Hume Creative Strategy OS
(page id 39d20d08-9385-8112-b225-efa682f5ea92)

[page] Hume Creative Strategy OS
This is the non-destructive working build of the Hume Health Creative Strategy OS. It is designed as an operating system for decisions, handoffs, tests, learnings, and ad-to-landing-page congruence across Hume Pod, Hume Band 2.0, the combined ecosystem, and future products.

## Current Creative Priorities
- Product priority: Hume Pod and Hume Band 2.0 remain separate strategy tracks, with Pod + Band ecosystem concepts handled as their own product lane.
- Channel priority: Meta, TikTok, YouTube, CTV, CRM, and CRO landing pages are seeded first because they carry the clearest current workflows.
- Business objective: turn customer insight into belief-change briefs, production-ready assets, live tests, learnings, and next actions.
- Top gap: existing source material is strong but spread across Notion pages, Google Sheets, decks, trackers, and reporting notes.
- Decision rule: every creative record must name product, persona, old belief, new belief, proof, channel, landing page, and next action.

## Audit Summary
What works well:
- Existing Growth Team SOP OS has strong operating principles and cadence.
- Creative Strategy SOP already frames Hume creative as belief-change first.
- Revenue Optimization SOP clearly defines ad-to-page promise matching and CRO decision rules.
- Product SOP and creator guides contain strong product handling, guardrails, and proof language.
- Paid reporting contains useful market/channel learnings and landing-page signals.
What is too static or link-heavy:
- The Growth Hub acts mainly as a link index across sheets, decks, Drive folders, and pages.
- Weekly reporting captures learnings in long notes, but not as structured, reusable records.
- Creative assets, hooks, claims, landing pages, and tests are not consistently linked as one workflow.
Duplication and consolidation opportunities:
- Existing Creative Brief Pipeline and Growth Asset and Proof Bank should be referenced or migrated later, but not overwritten.
- Product proof, claims, and compliance guardrails are duplicated across creator guides, product SOPs, and performance docs.
- Weekly reporting, test outcomes, and creative learnings should roll into a Performance and Learning Log.
Missing for creative strategy:
- A master Customer Insight Bank.
- A belief-change Angle Library with testing status and performance rollups.
- A dedicated Hook Library.
- A real Ad-to-Landing-Page Congruence review system.
- Product, role, channel, and workflow dashboards.
- Governance, ownership, review cadence, and archive strategy.

## Relation Map
Core relation logic:
- Products connect to personas, insights, angles, claims, offers, campaigns, briefs, assets, tests, and landing pages.
- Insights connect to personas, angles, briefs, and learnings.
- Personas connect to insights, angles, briefs, assets, tests, and landing pages.
- Angles connect to insights, personas, hooks, claims, briefs, assets, tests, landing pages, and learnings.
- Briefs connect to campaigns, products, channels, personas, angles, hooks, insights, claims, offers, landing pages, assets, and tests.
- Assets connect to briefs, creators, angles, hooks, campaigns, landing pages, tests, learnings, and congruence reviews.
- Tests connect to briefs, assets, landing pages, offers, campaigns, and learnings.
- Landing pages connect to products, personas, angles, assets, tests, and congruence reviews.

## Workflow Map
Insight to Brief: insight collected -> persona -> angle -> hypothesis -> brief -> asset -> test -> learning -> angle and insight update.
Winner Iteration: winning asset -> diagnose variable -> preserve winning element -> change one variable -> iteration brief -> variants -> learning.
Cross-Channel Adaptation: winning concept -> extract belief and mechanism -> assess channel fit -> native brief -> new asset -> channel learning.
Ad-to-LP Congruence: strong ad engagement + weak post-click -> congruence review -> mismatch diagnosis -> ad/page/CRO recommendation -> result.
Weekly Review: performance review -> classify winners and losses -> capture missing learnings -> decide priorities -> assign owners.
Claim Approval: proposed claim -> evidence -> compliance review -> approved/restricted/rejected -> usage guidance.

## Governance
- Every strategic database includes owner/review/status fields where supported.
- Claims require approval status, evidence source, risk level, usage limits, and review date.
- Weekly reviews classify winners, losses, learning debt, congruence gaps, and next-week owners.
- Monthly strategy review should refresh angle saturation, persona coverage, and channel playbooks.
- Quarterly taxonomy review should archive stale angles without deleting historical learnings.

## Migration Recommendations
- Keep existing Growth SOP OS as the broader growth source of truth.
- Keep existing Creative Brief Pipeline and Growth Asset and Proof Bank intact until this OS is validated.
- Later migrate active briefs/assets into this OS if the team agrees on schema ownership.
- Convert useful weekly reporting notes into Learning Log records rather than leaving them as buried text.
- Convert high-signal Google Sheets trackers into either imports or linked source references.
