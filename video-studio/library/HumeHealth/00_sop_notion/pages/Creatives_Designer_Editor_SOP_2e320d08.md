### IMPORTANT INFORMATION:
1. When doing caption we want to make sure that each caption start and finish on the same clip, we can't have text that start on one clip and finish in another one.
We also prefer captions that both lines are the same length, for example:
”I always struggle with dark.   vs   “I always struggle with
                  spots”                                         dark spots”
1. Never use animated text on scroll stopper like the image below, the first frame should give a clear idea of what the video is going to be and help to stop the hook.
