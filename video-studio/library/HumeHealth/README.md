# Hume Health: product training workspace

Brand: **Hume Health**. Two separate product tracks, **Hume Pod** (body composition) and **Hume Band 2.0** (wearable), plus an explicitly briefed **Pod + Band ecosystem** lane. Keep them separate in every brief, edit and file name (POD / BAND / PODBAND).

## Read order
0. **`EDITING_RULES.md`: client-confirmed MUST rules. Rule 1: every spoken metric gets its matching app screen with a red mark. STRICT: the product is always fully in frame in every size, and product-use shots start at the moment of use. Apply to every ad.**
0b. **`comments/README.md`: the client comments gallery. Every review round per project (note, fix, lesson) and the lessons checklist to run before planning and before every delivery. Log each new round there before changing anything.**
1. `AD_STYLE_GUIDE.md`: house style (v0 from the SOP; measured numbers come later).
2. `04_do_not_use/DO_NOT_USE.md`: claim red lines and banned visuals (preliminary).
3. `00_sop_notion/SOP_DIGEST.md`: the full editor SOP: editing bar, captions, VO, brand, sizes, naming, workflow, QA.
4. `00_sop_notion/STRATEGY_OS.md`: angles, personas, hooks, competitors, channel playbooks, landing pages.
5. `00_sop_notion/WINNER_AD_LIBRARY.md`: 46 winners with spend, ROAS, hook and hold rate, hook lines and insight notes.
6. `00_sop_notion/SOURCE_LINKS.md`: Drive, Figma and Frame.io sources and their access status.

## Current product facts (from the SOP, 2026)
- **Hume Pod**: at-home, hand-and-foot (8-sensor) multi-frequency BIA; segmental body fat, muscle, hydration, trends. "Know what your body is made of, not just what you weigh." HSA/FSA eligible where applicable (in review). "45+ body metrics" (in review). Not diagnostic; **not a DEXA equivalent**.
- **Hume Band 2.0**: wrist wearable + Hume app; sleep, stress, recovery, activity trends; interpreted insights from **Prof AI**. "No required monthly subscription for core use" (in review). **Not a medical device; no heart-event prediction.**
- Brand: Hume Blue #1882FF / #31ABE8, black, white; Red Hat Display, Canela, TikTok Display.
- Workflow: Notion production tasks, Frame.io review, Drive assets, Fish Audio VO, Hume Ad Namer naming.

Sources: public Notion SOP "Video Editor Day 1 Onboarding SOP" and the pages and databases it links to (crawled 2026-11-12). Credentials in the source were deliberately not copied.
