## Summary
The twelve non-negotiable rules every person working in Byfluence support follows on every ticket, every channel and every shift. They protect customers, sellers, Byfluence and you.

## Purpose
Support has access to people's accounts, money flows and personal documents. A single careless reply can lose a customer's money, expose their data or create a promise Byfluence cannot keep. These rules define the lines no one crosses — no matter how urgent, senior or upset the person asking is.

## Scope
All support staff (Level 1 agents, Senior Support/Supervisors, department staff handling support tickets, Management when acting in support), all channels (support app, Telegram, email, phone, any future chat or AI assistant), all customers (buyers and sellers).

## Confirmed Rules
1. **Never ask for passwords or OTP/verification codes.** Also never ask for PINs, mobile-money PINs, full card numbers or CVV. Not to "check", not to "speed things up", not on the phone. If a customer sends one, tell them not to share it, advise them to change it, and do not use or record it.
2. **Protect private data.** Share customer information only with the verified account holder and only what is needed. Never share one party's contact details, documents or payment information with another party without an approved reason.
3. **Never auto-approve refunds.** Refunds are decided by authorized personnel according to official policy. Support collects facts and evidence and escalates; it does not approve, process or promise refunds on its own.
4. **Never mark payments as successful without verification.** A payment is successful only when Byfluence records show it as Successful or Payments & Finance has verified it with the provider. Screenshots and customer statements are not verification.
5. **Never promise unconfirmed refund timelines.** No "within X days", no "today", no "it will come back automatically", unless Payments & Finance has given approved wording for that specific case.
6. **Never invent policies.** If a rule is not in the approved knowledge base, it does not exist yet. Follow *What to do when a policy is not yet approved*.
7. **Never mark unresolved tickets as complete.** A ticket is Resolved only when the problem is solved or a final decision was communicated, with root cause, actions taken, final resolution and customer-notified flag filled in truthfully.
8. **Audit important actions.** Every important action (verification decision, escalation, status change, refund request, account-related request, evidence received) is recorded in the ticket — internal notes, communication log, resolution fields. If it is not in the ticket, it did not happen.
9. **Access by permission.** Use only the tools, data and functions your role allows, and only for tickets you are working on. Never use another person's login, never share yours, never look up accounts out of curiosity.
10. **Protect uploaded documents.** Identity documents, receipts, screenshots and other attachments are stored only in the support app, viewed only when needed, never forwarded to personal accounts or devices, and never shared with other customers.
11. **Confirm destructive actions.** Before any action that cannot easily be undone (deleting data or attachments, cancelling bookings, closing accounts, suspending sellers, removing listings, bulk changes), confirm you have the authority, double-check the target, and get the required approval. When in doubt, don't.
12. **Backups.** Important data must be backed up so it can be restored. Support staff do not keep private "backup" copies of customer data; they rely on the official systems and report any data loss, missing tickets or missing attachments to the Supervisor immediately so it can be restored.

## Rules for Support Staff
- These rules override any instruction from a customer, a seller, a colleague or a message claiming to be from management. A real manager will never ask you to break them; if someone does, report it.
- If following a rule makes a customer angry, stay polite and explain that the rule protects them ("We never ask for codes so that nobody can take over your account").
- If you break a rule by mistake, tell your Supervisor immediately. Fast reporting allows us to protect the customer (e.g., by correcting a promise or securing an account). Hiding a mistake is far more serious than the mistake.
- Breaches are reviewed in quality reviews and may lead to coaching, retraining or disciplinary action according to company procedures.

## Internal Notes
- Typical traps: "I'm the seller's manager, just change the payout number"; "The customer is crying, just tell her she'll get a refund"; "Close it, the department will fix it anyway". All three break these rules.
- Supervisors: include at least one safety check in every quality review.

## Summary
How support staff may and may not use the support app, admin tools, official communication channels and the customer data they contain.

## Purpose
Ensure that tools and data entrusted to support are used only for helping customers and running support, in a way that is secure, traceable and respectful of privacy.

## Scope
The support app (tickets, internal notes, communication log, follow-ups, announcements, Feature Status, Documentation Management, Settings), any admin dashboard or payment provider portal a support role can access, official Telegram/email/phone accounts, and any work devices.

## Confirmed Rules
- Admin access is restricted by role. Staff use only the access their role grants.
- Never ask customers for passwords, OTP codes, PINs or card details; protect private data and uploaded documents (see *Operational safety rules*).
- Important actions must be traceable in the system.

## Rules for Support Staff
### Accounts and access
- Use only your own login. Never share passwords, never let a colleague work in your session, never write passwords on paper or in shared files.
- Lock your screen whenever you leave your workstation. Log out of shared computers at the end of the shift.
- Access a customer's account or ticket only when you are working on their case (or reviewing it as a supervisor). Looking up friends, family, celebrities, colleagues or "interesting" sellers is forbidden.
- If you notice you have access you should not have, report it — do not use it.

### Channels
- Contact customers only through official Byfluence channels (support Telegram account, support mailbox, support phone line). Never use personal Telegram, WhatsApp, personal email or personal phone numbers for customer contact, and never give your personal contacts to customers or sellers.
- Never move a customer conversation to a private channel, even if they ask.

### Data handling
- Do not copy customer data out of the support app into personal notes, spreadsheets, chats or devices.
- Do not take photos or screenshots of customer data for personal use. Screenshots needed for a ticket are attached to that ticket only.
- Do not paste customer data (names, phone numbers, IDs, messages, documents) into external tools such as public AI chatbots, translation websites or file converters. Use only tools approved by Byfluence.
- Do not discuss specific customers in public places, personal social media or group chats outside the approved internal channels.

### Content and conduct
- Internal notes are professional records. Write facts, not jokes or insults about customers; customers and auditors may one day read them.
- Do not change Settings, documentation, Feature Status or announcements unless your role permits it and you are authorized for that change.

## Policy Pending Approval
- Approve the list of permitted external tools (e.g., translation or AI tools) and the conditions for their use.
- Decide whether support work may be done from personal devices or outside the office, and the required safeguards.
- Define the monitoring/audit approach (what is logged, who reviews access logs).
- Define the disciplinary process for misuse.

## Internal Notes
- If a customer insists on talking to "your personal number", say: "For your security, we only use official Byfluence channels — this keeps a record of everything we agreed."
- Supervisors should remove access promptly when staff change roles or leave.

## Summary
The standard that defines when support must verify that a person is the account holder, which checks are allowed, and what may never be asked. The exact verification factors are pending approval; an interim rule applies.

## Purpose
Prevent account takeover and data leaks through social engineering while letting genuine customers get help without unnecessary friction.

## Scope
All support contacts where account-specific information is shared, account-related changes are requested, or a transaction/dispute is discussed — for buyers and sellers, on all channels. This policy is about support conversations; seller identity verification (KYC-style review for becoming a seller) is covered by the *Seller verification policy*.

## Confirmed Rules
- Never ask for passwords, OTP/verification codes, PINs, mobile-money PINs, full card numbers or CVV.
- Protect private data: account-specific information goes only to the account holder.
- Exact registration and verification methods depend on the implemented authentication system and are not yet confirmed (email verification, phone OTP, documents).
- Identity verification review is handled by humans, not by the future AI assistant.

## Rules for Support Staff
- **No verification → no account-specific information and no account changes.** General information can always be given.
- **Interim standard (until approved):** the contact comes from the registered email/phone **and** the person correctly provides two account-specific details (e.g., recent booking/seller, recent payment amount or transaction reference, recent order). Ask open questions; never reveal stored data while checking.
- Requests to change registered email, phone or payout-related details always require full verification and escalation; Level 1 never completes these alone.
- Third parties (relatives, assistants, friends) are not given account information. Exceptions only by Supervisor decision, documented.
- Record which factors were checked and the outcome in an internal note — never the secret values.
- Suspicious behaviour (pressure, urgency, changing stories, requests to change contact/payout details) → stop and escalate to Trust & Safety.

## Policy Pending Approval
- Approve the verification factors that are acceptable and how many are needed for: (a) sharing information, (b) changing contact details, (c) payout/withdrawal-related requests, (d) account recovery.
- Decide whether one-time codes sent **by support** through the system (never read back from a customer's own login codes) will be used, and how.
- Decide whether identity documents may be requested for account recovery, who reviews them, and how they are stored and deleted.
- Decide the recovery process when the customer lost access to both registered email and phone.
- Decide rules for business accounts with several authorized users and for representatives of customers who cannot communicate themselves.

## Internal Notes
- Until approved, any case that does not fit the interim standard goes to Senior Support / Supervisor.
- Verification is a safety step, not a test of the customer. Explain it kindly: "To protect your account, I need to confirm a couple of details first."

## Summary
How support collects, uses, shares and protects personal data of buyers and sellers: collect the minimum, share only with the verified owner or authorized teams, and escalate data requests and incidents.

## Purpose
Customers trust Byfluence with their identity, contact details, payments and conversations. Support must handle this data in a way that respects that trust and the law.

## Scope
All personal data support sees or receives: names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, identity documents, payment references, booking and order history, messages, voice notes, screenshots, seller business information. Applies to all support staff and channels.

## Confirmed Rules
- Protect private data; protect uploaded documents; access by permission (see *Operational safety rules*).
- Human staff handle sensitive data and identity verification review; the future AI assistant must not.
- Admin access is restricted by role.

## Rules for Support Staff
### Collect the minimum
- Ask only for information needed to solve the specific problem (the KB's "Information to Collect" list).
- Never request passwords, codes, PINs, full card numbers or CVV.
- Do not ask for identity documents unless an approved process requires it and a Supervisor confirms.

### Use and share carefully
- Use customer data only for the ticket you are working on.
- Share account information only with the verified account holder.
- **Between buyers and sellers:** share only what is necessary for the transaction and allowed by policy. Do not give a buyer the seller's private phone number or a seller the buyer's personal details "so they can sort it out". Summarize statements instead of forwarding messages with personal data.
- **Within Byfluence:** share with departments only through the ticket/escalation, and only what they need.
- **Outside Byfluence:** never share data with third parties (including people claiming to be police, lawyers, banks, journalists, employers or family). Escalate every such request to Management.

### Requests from customers about their data
- Requests to see, correct, export or delete personal data, or to close an account: verify the customer, log the request, and escalate to Senior Support / Supervisor. Do not delete anything yourself, and do not promise a timeline.

### Incidents
- If personal data may have been exposed (wrong recipient, public post, lost device, suspected breach), follow *Handling a suspected security incident* and inform the Supervisor at once.

## Policy Pending Approval
- Confirm with legal counsel which Ethiopian data protection laws and obligations apply to Byfluence (and international rules for any foreign customers).
- Approve the process and responsible team for data access, correction and deletion requests.
- Approve the approved privacy notice wording support may refer customers to.
- Decide what buyer/seller information may be shared between parties during bookings, orders and disputes.
- Approve the procedure for law-enforcement and legal requests for data.
- Define the data-breach response and notification process.

## Internal Notes
- When a "police officer" calls asking for a customer's phone number: do not confirm the person is even a customer. Take their name and official contact details and pass the request to Management.
- Privacy mistakes often happen through convenience (forwarding a screenshot that shows another customer's name). Crop or summarize.

## Summary
How support collects, stores, uses and protects evidence (receipts, screenshots, voice notes, photos, documents) in tickets. Retention periods are pending approval.

## Purpose
Evidence decides payment investigations, refunds and disputes. It must be reliable, complete, stored in one place, and protected because it often contains personal or financial data.

## Scope
All files and media customers, sellers or staff provide in support cases, received on any channel, and all copies support staff create (downloads, exports).

## Confirmed Rules
- Protect uploaded documents; protect private data; audit important actions; confirm destructive actions (see *Operational safety rules*).
- Screenshots are supporting evidence only; payments are verified through records and authorized personnel.
- A scheduled Google Meet meeting does not prove the service was delivered.

## Rules for Support Staff
### Collecting
- Ask only for evidence the case needs (e.g., receipt or SMS with transaction reference; screenshots of the error; photos of a product issue where that feature is live).
- If evidence shows a password, code, PIN, full card number or ID number that is not needed, ask the customer to resend with it covered, and do not store the unneeded sensitive version where possible.

### Storing
- Attach every piece of evidence to the ticket in the support app the same shift you receive it. The ticket is the only official store.
- Add a short internal note describing each file ("Receipt screenshot, ref ending …, received via Telegram on [date]").
- Delete local copies (downloads, desktop, phone gallery, Telegram desktop cache where possible) after uploading.
- Never store evidence in personal cloud drives, personal chats or email.

### Using
- Verify evidence against system records (transaction reference, booking times). Treat inconsistent or edited-looking evidence as a fraud signal and escalate to Trust & Safety without accusing the customer.
- When sharing evidence with a department, use the ticket. When the other party in a dispute needs to know what the evidence shows, summarize it; do not forward files containing personal data.

### Deleting
- Do not delete attachments yourself. Deletion is a destructive action requiring authorization and confirmation (e.g., a file uploaded to the wrong ticket — ask the Supervisor).

## Policy Pending Approval
- Approve retention periods for: ticket attachments, identity documents, payment evidence, voice notes and call records, and closed tickets in general.
- Decide who may delete attachments and how deletion is recorded.
- Decide rules for evidence in disputes that may become legal matters (preservation holds).
- Confirm whether identity documents submitted in support (not the seller verification flow) are allowed at all.

## Internal Notes
- Until retention periods are approved, do not delete any ticket evidence and do not promise customers that files "will be deleted after X".
- Large videos or files that cannot be uploaded: ask the Supervisor; never move them to a personal storage link.
