# Byfluence tone of voice

## Summary
How Byfluence Support sounds: warm, respectful, clear, honest and calm — one consistent voice in Amharic and English, adjusted in tone to the situation.

## Overview
Byfluence exists so that anyone can start and grow an online business. Our customers include first-time sellers, busy professionals offering consultations, and buyers paying online for the first time. Many contact us when they are worried about money or a missed session. Our voice must make them feel that a capable, respectful person is taking care of them.

Our voice follows the four Byfluence experience principles:

| Principle | What it means in our messages |
|---|---|
| **Simplicity** | Short sentences, plain words, one request at a time, clear next step |
| **Trust** | Honest about what we know and don't know; no promises we cannot keep; we protect the customer's data |
| **Opportunity** | Encouraging, especially to new sellers; we help them succeed, not just fix errors |
| **Accountability** | We own the case ("I will check this"), give a case number and follow up |

**Voice** (who we are) stays the same. **Tone** (how we say it) changes with the situation:

| Situation | Tone |
|---|---|
| Simple question | Friendly, efficient |
| Worried about money | Calm, reassuring, precise |
| Angry complaint | Patient, serious, no defensiveness |
| Security incident | Urgent, clear, protective |
| Bad news | Respectful, direct, kind |
| New seller asking for help | Encouraging, supportive |

## Guidelines
- **Be warm, not casual.** Greet by name, thank them, use please. In Amharic always use the respectful form (እርስዎ).
- **Be clear, not cold.** Get to the point in the first lines; avoid long introductions.
- **Be honest, not alarming.** Say what we are doing and what we don't know yet. Never guess.
- **Be confident, not arrogant.** "I will check this for you" — not "That's impossible" or "You must have done something wrong".
- **Be human, not robotic.** Adapt templates to what the customer actually said; mention their specific booking or problem.
- **Be calm.** Our calm reduces their stress. No capital letters, no exclamation marks in serious cases.
- **Be consistent.** Use the KB and templates as the base so every agent gives the same answer.
- **Speak as one company.** "We" for Byfluence; "I" for what you personally will do. Never "that's another department's problem".

## Examples
### Simple question — friendly and efficient
**EN:** "Hello Bethlehem, thank you for your question! To book a consultation, open the consultant's listing, choose an available date and time, and complete the payment. Once the payment is successful, your booking is confirmed. Is there anything else I can help you with?"

**AM:** "ሰላም ቤተልሔም፣ ስለ ጥያቄዎ እናመሰግናለን! ምክክር ለማስያዝ የአማካሪውን ገጽ ይክፈቱ፣ የሚመችዎትን ቀን እና ሰዓት ይምረጡ፣ ከዚያም ክፍያውን ያጠናቅቁ። ክፍያው በተሳካ ሁኔታ ሲፈጸም ቀጠሮዎ ይረጋገጣል። ሌላ ልረዳዎት የምችለው ነገር አለ?"

### Worried about money — calm and precise
**EN:** "Hello Dawit, I understand how worrying it is to see money leave your account without a confirmation. I am checking your payment now. Please send me the transaction reference from your SMS. Your case number is {{ticket_id}}."

**AM:** "ሰላም ዳዊት፣ ማረጋገጫ ሳይደርስዎት ገንዘብ ከሂሳብዎ ሲቀነስ ምን ያህል እንደሚያሳስብ ይገባኛል። ክፍያዎን አሁን እያጣራሁ ነው። እባክዎ ከኤስኤምኤሱ ላይ ያለውን የግብይት መለያ ቁጥር ይላኩልኝ። የጉዳይ ቁጥርዎ {{ticket_id}} ነው።"

### New seller — encouraging
**EN:** "Congratulations on submitting your seller application, Hana! Your application is now being reviewed. While you wait, you can prepare a clear description and photos for your first listing so you are ready to start as soon as you are approved."

**AM:** "ሐና፣ የሻጭ ማመልከቻዎን ስላስገቡ እንኳን ደስ አለዎት! ማመልከቻዎ አሁን በግምገማ ላይ ነው። እስከዚያው ለመጀመሪያ ዝርዝርዎ ግልጽ መግለጫ እና ፎቶዎችን ቢያዘጋጁ፣ ልክ እንደጸደቀ ወዲያውኑ ለመጀመር ዝግጁ ይሆናሉ።"

## Do and Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "I will check this for you." | "I don't know." (and stop there) |
| "Thank you for your patience." | "As I already told you…" |
| "I understand this is frustrating." | "Calm down." |
| Use the customer's name | "Dear customer" in a Telegram chat |
| "Your request will be reviewed." | "You will definitely get your money back." |
| One clear next step | Three paragraphs of explanation before the point |
| Respectful Amharic (እርስዎ, ይላኩልኝ) | Informal Amharic (አንተ/አንቺ, ላክልኝ) |

## Internal Notes
- When unsure about tone, read your message as if you were the customer's parent receiving it. Would it feel respectful and clear?
- Tone is assessed in quality reviews under "Communication".

## Summary
Practical rules for writing messages customers understand the first time — especially on small phone screens and in their second language.

## Overview
Most Byfluence customers read our messages on a phone, often between other tasks, sometimes on slow connections, and sometimes in their second language. Every unclear message costs another round of questions and delays the solution. A clear message gets an answer in one reply.

## Guidelines
1. **Lead with the point.** First line: what this message is about (answer, update, or request).
2. **One idea per sentence.** Split long sentences. Aim for sentences a customer can read in one breath.
3. **Number your requests.** If you need information, list it 1, 2, 3 — maximum three or four items. Customers answer numbered lists completely.
4. **Ask only what you need.** Check what the customer already sent before asking.
5. **Use the customer's words.** If they said "money cut", don't reply about "payment authorization holds".
6. **Plain words, not jargon.** "Your payment is still being checked", not "the transaction is pending provider callback".
7. **Be specific.** "the transaction reference in the SMS from your bank" instead of "payment details".
8. **Say what happens next.** Every message ends with the next step and who does it.
9. **Short paragraphs.** Two or three lines each; white space between them. Long Ethiopic-script blocks are especially hard to read on phones.
10. **Format for the channel.** Telegram: a few short lines. Email: greeting, short paragraphs, numbered list, signature.
11. **Check before sending:** names, amounts, dates, case number, no leftover `{{placeholders}}`, no sensitive data requested.

## Examples
### Requesting information — before and after
**Unclear (EN):** "Dear customer, regarding your issue we need some more details in order to proceed with the investigation of the transaction such as the information about the payment and also the booking and anything else that may help."

**Clear (EN):** "Hello Meron, I'm sorry for the trouble. To check your payment, please send me:
1. The transaction reference from your payment SMS
2. The date and time you paid
3. The consultant's name
Your case number is {{ticket_id}}."

**Clear (AM):** "ሰላም ሜሮን፣ ስለተፈጠረው ችግር ይቅርታ። ክፍያዎን ለማጣራት እባክዎ የሚከተሉትን ይላኩልኝ፦
1. ከክፍያ ኤስኤምኤስዎ ላይ ያለውን የግብይት መለያ ቁጥር
2. የከፈሉበትን ቀን እና ሰዓት
3. የአማካሪውን ስም
የጉዳይ ቁጥርዎ {{ticket_id}} ነው።"

### Status update — before and after
**Unclear (EN):** "We are still working on it."

**Clear (EN):** "Hello Yonas, an update on case {{ticket_id}}: our payments team is verifying your transaction with the payment provider. There is nothing you need to do now. I will contact you again by {{follow_up_time}}."

**Clear (AM):** "ሰላም ዮናስ፣ ስለ ጉዳይ ቁጥር {{ticket_id}} መረጃ፦ የክፍያ ቡድናችን ግብይትዎን ከክፍያ አቅራቢው ጋር እያጣራ ነው። አሁን ማድረግ የሚጠበቅብዎት ምንም ነገር የለም። እስከ {{follow_up_time}} ድረስ በድጋሚ አገኝዎታለሁ።"

## Do and Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Numbered list of what you need | A paragraph hiding three questions |
| "Please send the reference from your SMS" | "Please provide relevant documentation" |
| End with the next step | End with "Regards" and no next step |
| Short lines on Telegram | One long block of text |
| Re-read before sending | Send a template with `{{customer_name}}` still in it |

## Internal Notes
- Test: could the customer reply to your message without asking "what do you mean?" If not, rewrite.
- Clear messages reduce ticket handling time more than typing speed does.

## Summary
Ready-to-use empathy phrases in English and natural Amharic for the most common support situations — acknowledging feelings, apologizing, reassuring without promising, and thanking.

## Overview
Empathy means showing the customer that you understand how the situation affects them — before you jump to solutions. It is not about agreeing that Byfluence or the seller was wrong, and never about promising an outcome. Use one empathy phrase per message, matched to the situation; overdoing it sounds fake.

## Guidelines
- Acknowledge the **specific** situation ("a missed session", "money deducted"), not just "your issue".
- Apologize for the **experience** ("I'm sorry you have had this trouble") — you can always do that, even before you know who caused it.
- Reassure with **actions**, not outcomes: "I am checking this now" instead of "Everything will be fine".
- Thank customers for patience, details and for reporting problems.
- In Amharic, keep the respectful form and avoid word-for-word translations of English idioms.

## Examples
### Acknowledging feelings
| Situation | EN | AM |
|---|---|---|
| General frustration | I understand how frustrating this is. | ይህ ምን ያህል እንደሚያበሳጭ ይገባኛል። |
| Worry about money | I understand that you are worried about your payment. | ስለ ክፍያዎ እንደተጨነቁ ይገባኛል። |
| Missed session | I'm sorry you missed your session — I know you were looking forward to it. | ክፍለ-ጊዜዎ ስላመለጠዎት አዝናለሁ፤ በጉጉት እንደጠበቁት እረዳለሁ። |
| Repeated contact | I'm sorry you had to contact us again about this. | በዚህ ጉዳይ ድጋሚ ማግኘት ስላስፈለገዎት ይቅርታ እንጠይቃለን። |
| Seller worried about earnings | I understand how important your earnings are for your business. | ገቢዎ ለሥራዎ ምን ያህል አስፈላጊ እንደሆነ እረዳለሁ። |
| Security worry | I understand this is alarming. Let's secure your account first. | ይህ እንደሚያስደነግጥ ይገባኛል። በመጀመሪያ አካውንትዎን እንጠብቅ። |

| Waiting for information | As soon as I have an answer, I will contact you. | መልስ እንዳገኘሁ ወዲያውኑ አገኝዎታለሁ። |

## Do and Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "I'm sorry you had this experience." | "We are not responsible for that." |
| "I understand you are worried about your payment." | "I understand" repeated in every sentence |
| "I am checking this now." | "Don't worry, you'll get your money back." |
| One sincere empathy phrase, then action | Three apologies and no action |
| Natural Amharic phrasing | Literal translation ("ሁኔታዎን እሰማለሁ" for "I hear you") |

## Internal Notes
- Empathy is not admission of fault. "I'm sorry this happened" is safe; "This is our fault, we will pay you back" is not, unless confirmed.

# Phrases to never use (promises, blame, jargon)

## Summary
The phrases that must never appear in Byfluence support messages — unauthorized promises, blame, security risks, jargon and dismissive language — with safe alternatives in English and Amharic.

## Overview
Most serious support mistakes are single sentences: a promise of a refund, a guess about a timeline, blaming a seller, or asking for a code. These phrases create financial liability, break customer trust, or put accounts at risk. Quality reviews treat promise and security phrases as critical findings.

## Guidelines
- **No promises** of outcomes, refunds, approvals, compensation, timelines or features not confirmed live.
- **No blame** of the customer, the seller, payment providers, colleagues or other departments.
- **No security risks**: never ask for passwords, codes, PINs or card numbers; never offer personal contacts.
- **No jargon or internal language**: "ticket", "L2", "callback", "API", "backend".
- **No dismissive language** that tells the customer their feelings or problem don't matter.
- **No speculation** presented as fact.

## Examples
### Promises
| Never say | Say instead (EN) | Say instead (AM) |
|---|---|---|
| "You will get a full refund." | "Your refund request has been received and will be reviewed." | "የገንዘብ ተመላሽ ጥያቄዎ ደርሶናል፤ ይገመገማል።" |
| "The money will be back in your account soon." | "Our payments team is verifying the transaction, and I will update you." | "የክፍያ ቡድናችን ግብይቱን እያጣራ ነው፤ መረጃ እሰጥዎታለሁ።" |
| "It will be fixed today." | "Our team is working on it, and I will update you as soon as I have news." | "ቡድናችን እየሠራበት ነው፤ አዲስ መረጃ እንዳገኘሁ አሳውቅዎታለሁ።" |
| "Your seller account will be approved." | "Your application is being reviewed." | "ማመልከቻዎ በግምገማ ላይ ነው።" |
| "Delivery is available in Addis." (unless Feature Status says live) | "Let me check whether this service is currently available." | "ይህ አገልግሎት አሁን መኖሩን ላረጋግጥልዎት።" |
| "I guarantee…" / "100%…" | "I will do everything I can to help." | "ለመርዳት የምችለውን ሁሉ አደርጋለሁ።" |

### Blame
| Never say | Say instead (EN) | Say instead (AM) |
|---|---|---|
| "You entered the wrong details." | "It looks like the details didn't match; let's check them together." | "መረጃው ያልተዛመደ ይመስላል፤ አብረን እናጣራው።" |
| "The seller is a scammer." | "We have received your report and it will be reviewed." | "ሪፖርትዎ ደርሶናል፤ ይገመገማል።" |
| "Chapa/the bank is broken." | "We are checking the payment with the provider." | "ክፍያውን ከአቅራቢው ጋር እያጣራን ነው።" |
| "That's the other department's fault." | "I am following this up with our team." | "ጉዳዩን ከቡድናችን ጋር እየተከታተልኩ ነው።" |

### Security

## Do and Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Describe actions you are taking | Predict outcomes |
| Use "the payment confirmation", "your case" | Use "callback", "ticket", "L2/L3" |
| Neutral words about sellers and providers | Labels such as "scammer", "broken", "useless" |
| Warn about sharing codes | Ask for codes, even "just this once" |

## Internal Notes
- If you already sent a forbidden promise, tell your Supervisor immediately. A quick, honest correction message is far better than letting the customer rely on it.
- The customer-facing word for a ticket is "case" (ጉዳይ) and the number is the "case number" (የጉዳይ ቁጥር).
