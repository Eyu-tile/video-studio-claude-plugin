# Introduction to Byfluence
id: trn-01
number: 1
duration: 45

## Learning Objectives
- Explain in simple Amharic and English what Byfluence is, who it serves and why it exists.
- Describe Byfluence's role as a marketplace facilitator and what that means for who is responsible for a product or service.
- Explain at a high level how Byfluence earns money without quoting any unconfirmed fee or commission rate.
- Distinguish the MVP (live) capabilities from planned expansion areas, and check the Feature Status page before confirming any feature to a customer.
- Connect the four Byfluence experience principles (Simplicity, Trust, Opportunity, Accountability) to everyday support behaviour.
- Recognise a "Policy Pending Approval" marker and know the safe default action when you see one.

## Lesson

### What Byfluence is
Byfluence (byfluence.com) is an **Ethiopian multi-vendor online marketplace**. "Multi-vendor" means many independent sellers offer their products and services in one place, and buyers can discover, compare, book and pay for them on one platform. The primary customer language is **Amharic**; English is secondary.

Byfluence is at the **MVP stage** (Minimum Viable Product). Some capabilities are live today and many more are being developed. As a support agent you will often be the first person to explain this difference to a customer — so you must know it well and check it every time.

### The mission: opportunity without big starting capital
The mission behind Byfluence is that **anyone should be able to start and grow an online business, even without significant starting capital**. Building your own website, payment system and booking tool is expensive. Byfluence provides shared marketplace infrastructure — listings, payments, bookings and trust — so an entrepreneur can focus on what they sell.

This matters for support because many of our sellers are first-time online entrepreneurs. They may be unfamiliar with verification, listings or withdrawals. Patience and clear step-by-step guidance are part of the mission, not an extra.

### The facilitator role
Byfluence is a **marketplace facilitator**. It is **not** the owner or provider of most of the products or services listed. Sellers are responsible for their offerings, subject to the marketplace rules and to any fulfillment responsibility Byfluence explicitly takes on.

| Area | Who is responsible | What support does |
|---|---|---|
| Content and quality of a consultation | The seller (consultant) | Collect facts, contact the seller side through the proper process, escalate disputes |
| The listing description, price and availability | The seller, within marketplace rules | Guide the seller; report misleading listings to Trust & Safety |
| The platform: accounts, booking flow, payment recording | Byfluence | Troubleshoot, escalate bugs to Technical, payment issues to Payments & Finance |
| Refund and dispute decisions | Authorized Byfluence personnel, under official policy | Never decide; collect evidence and route |
| Delivery of physical goods | Not yet defined (planned feature) | Check Feature Status; do not promise |

> [!WARNING] "Facilitator" is never an excuse to say "that is not our problem." A customer who paid through Byfluence trusts Byfluence. You explain the roles politely and still own the case until it is handed to the right team.

### How Byfluence earns money
Byfluence earns revenue mainly through **commissions or fees on marketplace transactions**. That is all that is confirmed. **Commission rates are not confirmed.** If a seller asks "what percentage do you take?", you do not guess, estimate or repeat a number you heard from a colleague. You check the Policies section and Announcements; if nothing is published, you tell the seller the information will be shared through official channels and record the question in a ticket so management sees the demand.

### What is live and what is planned
The business overview separates MVP capabilities from expansion areas. The **Feature Status** page in the support app is the single source of truth — it can change at any time, so check it rather than memorising this table.

| Offering | Stage (at time of writing) | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer accounts & registration | MVP / live | Support fully |
| Seller application, onboarding & identity verification | MVP / live | Support fully; required documents not yet confirmed |
| One-on-one consultations | MVP / live | Core of most tickets today |
| Group consultations | MVP / live | Capacity, cancellation and refund rules pending |
| Google Meet integration | MVP / live | Confirm how links are generated before explaining it |
| Seller earnings & withdrawals | MVP / live | Payout methods and timelines pending |
| Physical products & Addis Ababa delivery | Planned | Do not tell customers it is available |
| Inventory-free selling (local dropshipping) | Planned | Terms and eligibility not defined |
| Digital products | Planned | Access and refund rules pending |
| Payment methods (Chapa, Telebirr, M-Pesa, bank, card, Stripe) | Unconfirmed / planned | Check Feature Status for each method |

> [!NOTE] On the Feature Status page, "live" with **verified: no** means the feature is in MVP scope but has not yet been confirmed in production by the support team. Treat it as live, but if a customer reports it does not work, that is a ticket for Technical — not a reason to argue with the customer.

### The four experience principles
Byfluence describes its experience with four principles. Each one translates into concrete support behaviour.

| Principle | What it means in support |
|---|---|
| **Simplicity** | Short, clear answers. One question at a time. Steps the customer can follow on a phone. |
| **Trust** | Never promise what is not authorized. Never ask for passwords, OTPs or PINs. Verify by records, not by screenshots alone. |
| **Opportunity** | Help sellers succeed: explain verification and listings patiently, especially to first-time entrepreneurs. |
| **Accountability** | Every case gets a ticket number, notes, an owner and a recorded outcome. If you say you will follow up, you do. |

### Support's role today and tomorrow
Support is offered (or intended) through the website, **Telegram**, **email**, **telephone** and, in future, an **AI assistant**. Check the Feature Status page for which channels are confirmed. The planned AI assistant will answer FAQs and explain approved policies. Humans — you — will always handle account-specific investigation, financial decisions, identity verification review, refund approval, seller disputes, fraud, admin access and sensitive data. That is why accurate tickets and internal notes matter: they are how the human side of Byfluence learns.

### A quick tour of your tools
| App area | Use it when… |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Starting your shift: open tickets, follow-ups due, recent announcements |
| Knowledge Base | You need causes, info to collect, troubleshooting steps, EN/AM responses and escalation rules for an issue |
| FAQs | A customer asks a general question with a standard answer |
| Response Templates | You need a ready message; fill placeholders like `{{customer_name}}` and `{{ticket_id}}`, choose Amharic or English, copy |

| Escalation Center | You need the right level and department |

| Policies | Official rules — and "Policy Pending Approval" markers |
| Announcements | Incidents, changes, new decisions |
| Feature Status | What is live, planned or unconfirmed |
| Global search | Type the customer's words, in Amharic or English |

### "Policy Pending Approval" — your first safety rule
Many Byfluence rules (refund eligibility, cancellation windows, payout timelines, commission rates) are **not yet decided**. In the app these are marked **Policy Pending Approval**. When you see the marker:

1. Do **not** invent an answer or use what "usually happens" elsewhere.
2. Tell the customer honestly that the case is being reviewed by the responsible team, and give them their case number.
3. Create or update the ticket and escalate to **Senior Support / Supervisor** (unless the article names another department).
4. Do not promise an outcome or a date. Use approved neutral wording ("we will update you as soon as we have a decision").

> [!POLICY] Policy Pending Approval means "management has not decided yet." It never means "the agent decides."

## Realistic Examples

### Example 1: A delivery question for a planned feature
**Customer (Telegram, Amharic):**
> ሰላም፣ ስልክ ከእናንተ ገዝቼ ባህር ዳር ድረስ ታደርሳላችሁ?

*(Hello, if I buy a phone from you, will you deliver it to Bahir Dar?)*

**What the agent does:** Searches "delivery" and opens Feature Status. Physical products and Addis Ababa delivery are **planned**, not live. Delivery areas are not confirmed.

**Model reply (AM):**
> ሰላም፣ ስላገኙን እናመሰግናለን። በአሁኑ ጊዜ Byfluence በዋናነት የምክክር አገልግሎቶችን ያቀርባል። የቁሳዊ ምርቶች ሽያጭ እና የማድረስ አገልግሎት በዝግጅት ላይ ናቸው፤ ሲጀመሩ በይፋዊ ቻናሎቻችን እናሳውቃለን። ሌላ የምረዳዎት ነገር አለ?

**Why:** It is honest, does not promise a launch date or delivery area, and keeps the door open.

### Example 2: "Byfluence must pay me back for bad advice"
**Customer (email, English):**
> The business consultant I booked on your site gave me useless advice. Byfluence took my money so Byfluence must refund me today.

**What the agent does:** Acknowledges the frustration, explains (without defensiveness) that the consultant delivers the session and Byfluence reviews complaints between buyers and sellers. Creates a ticket (category: Consultations → Session Complaints & Completion), records the consultation ID and what went wrong, and escalates to Senior Support because refund rules are Policy Pending Approval.

**Model reply (EN, extract):**
> I'm sorry the session did not meet your expectations. Consultants on Byfluence provide their own sessions, and our team reviews complaints like yours with both sides. I've opened case {{ticket_id}} and passed it to the team responsible. I can't confirm a refund outcome yet, but I will update you as soon as there is a decision.

### Example 3: A seller asks about commission
**Customer (Telegram, Amharic):**
> አማካሪ ሆኜ መመዝገብ እፈልጋለሁ። ከእያንዳንዱ ክፍያ ስንት ፐርሰንት ትወስዳላችሁ?

*(I want to register as a consultant. What percentage do you take from each payment?)*

**What the agent does:** Searches "commission" and checks Policies and Announcements. No approved rate is published. The agent does **not** guess.

**Model reply (AM):**
> ሰላም፣ Byfluence ላይ ለመሸጥ ስላሰቡ እናመሰግናለን! Byfluence ገቢውን የሚያገኘው በግብይቶች ላይ በሚወሰድ ኮሚሽን ወይም ክፍያ ነው። ትክክለኛው የኮሚሽን መጠን በይፋ ሲጸድቅ እናሳውቅዎታለን፤ ጥያቄዎን መዝግበናል። እስከዚያው የሻጭ ማመልከቻ ሂደቱን ላስረዳዎት?

### Example 4: Payment method question
**Customer (phone, English):** "Can I pay with M-Pesa?"

**What the agent does:** Opens Feature Status while on the call. M-Pesa is **unconfirmed**. The agent says: "Let me check the payment options currently available for you," and only names a method if Feature Status shows it as live. Otherwise: "The payment options available to you are shown on the checkout page; I can't confirm M-Pesa at the moment."

## Practical Exercises
1. **Feature Status audit.** Open the Feature Status page. Copy every feature into a three-column table: Live, Planned, Unconfirmed. Mark which "live" items are not yet verified. Compare your table with the one in this lesson and note any differences — those are recent changes you must know.
2. **Find the pending decisions.** Use global search for "commission", "refund" and "delivery". Open at least three documents that show a **Policy Pending Approval** marker. For each, write one line: "Decision pending: … / What I do meanwhile: …".
3. **Explain Byfluence in two sentences.** Write a two-sentence explanation of Byfluence for a new buyer in Amharic and in English. Then search the FAQs for "What is Byfluence" and compare your version with the approved answer. Adjust yours so it does not promise anything the FAQ does not.
4. **Tool tour.** Open the Knowledge Base and list the categories. For each category, write whether it is mainly for buyers, sellers or both, and whether it covers a live or a planned feature.
5. **Announcements check.** Open Announcements and read the latest three. For each one, write how it would change what you tell a customer today.
