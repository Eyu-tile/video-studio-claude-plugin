# Understanding Buyers and Sellers
id: trn-02
number: 2
duration: 50

## Learning Objectives
- Identify within the first exchange whether you are helping a buyer, a seller, or a person who is both.
- Describe the buyer journey for a consultation and name the points where it most often breaks.
- Describe the seller onboarding journey from application to first sale, and what support can and cannot do at each stage.
- Explain the difference between seller **verification status** (Pending, Verified, Rejected) and **operational status** (Active, Suspended, Banned).
- Explain the seller earnings lifecycle and why a successful customer payment is not immediately a withdrawable balance.
- Choose the right Knowledge Base category and escalation department based on who the customer is.

## Lesson

### Two kinds of customers
Everyone who contacts Byfluence Support is a customer — including sellers. Sellers are businesses that depend on Byfluence for their income, so their problems (verification delays, withdrawals, suspended accounts) are often urgent and emotional. Buyers are paying for something they expect to receive. Your first job in every conversation is to understand **which role the person is contacting you in**, because the role decides which articles apply, what information you collect, and where the case goes.

A seller account starts as a normal user account that then **applies to become a seller**, so one person can contact you as a buyer one day and as a seller the next. Always ask about the specific transaction or listing, not just "are you a buyer or seller?"

> [!TIP] A good opening question: "Is this about something you booked or bought, or about something you sell on Byfluence?" — "ያስያዙት ወይም የገዙት ነገር ነው ወይስ Byfluence ላይ የሚሸጡት ነገር?"

### What buyers can do
Buyers discover products and services, search sellers, compare offers, book consultations, purchase, pay, track transactions, manage their account and contact support. Their **buyer dashboard** contains:

- Profile and settings
- Consultation bookings — upcoming sessions and booking-management actions
- Purchase history and order information
- Transaction history
- Support options

When a buyer says "I can't find my booking", the dashboard is where they should look first, and it is where you guide them before you investigate further.

### The buyer journey for a consultation
| Step | What happens | Where it commonly breaks |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Discover | Buyer finds a consultant listing | Search confusion, listing looks misleading |
| 2. Evaluate | Reads consultant name, topic, description, duration, price, availability | Price/time unclear, time-zone confusion |
| 3. Select & schedule | Chooses a date and time | Slot disappears, wrong time zone selected |
| 4. Pay | Chooses method and pays | Failed, pending, duplicate, deducted-but-not-confirmed |
| 5. Confirmation | Booking confirmed **after successful payment** | No confirmation received, booking stuck as pending |
| 6. Meeting details | Receives Google Meet details | Missing or wrong link |
| 7. Attend | Joins at the scheduled time | Late arrival, no-show (either side), connection problems |
| 8. Completion | Consultation marked completed | Disagreement about whether it was delivered |

Notice that the booking is only confirmed **after** successful payment. Most "I paid but…" tickets sit between steps 4 and 6.

### Planned buyer journeys
Physical products (order with delivery information → pay → seller prepares → fulfillment → completed) and digital products (pay → access) are **planned**. When a buyer asks about them, check the Feature Status page; do not walk them through a journey that does not exist yet.

### What sellers can do
Sellers create a seller account, complete identity verification, build a public profile, create listings, set prices, manage availability, accept bookings and orders, monitor transactions, view earnings and request withdrawals.

### The seller onboarding journey
1. Create a Byfluence account
2. Apply to become a seller
3. Provide personal or business information
4. Submit identity verification
5. Wait for review
6. **Approved** or **Rejected**
7. Complete the public profile
8. Create listings
9. Start selling once permissions are granted

What support can do at each stage:

| Stage | Support can | Support cannot |
|---|---|---|
| Applying / submitting info | Explain the steps, troubleshoot upload or form errors | Fill in the application for them |
| Waiting for review | Confirm the application is received (if you have access), set expectations without a date | Approve, speed up, or promise a decision date |
| Rejected | Share the reason **only if Seller Operations has provided approved wording**, explain how to re-apply if allowed | Overturn a rejection |
| Profile & listings | Guide, explain listing review results | Approve a listing that Seller Operations rejected |

> [!NOTE] The exact registration fields and required identity documents are **not confirmed**. Never tell a seller "you need your Kebele ID and a trade license" unless an approved article or policy says so.

### Two status dimensions for sellers
This is one of the most misunderstood topics, so learn it precisely. A seller has **two separate statuses**:

- **Verification status** — is the identity check done? **Pending**, **Verified** or **Rejected**.
- **Operational status** — is the seller allowed to operate right now? **Active**, **Suspended** or **Banned**.

| Combination | What it means | Typical support response |
|---|---|---|
| Pending + (not yet operating) | Identity check still in review | Explain review is in progress; no date promised |
| Verified + Active | Normal selling seller | Help with day-to-day issues |
| Verified + Suspended | Identity is confirmed, but the seller is temporarily restricted | Do not say "you're not verified." Route questions about the restriction to Seller Operations; Trust & Safety if fraud-related |
| Verified + Banned | Identity is confirmed, but the seller is permanently removed from operating | Explain neutrally; route appeals per policy (pending) to Senior Support |
| Rejected | Verification failed | Use approved wording only; route to Seller Operations |

> [!WARNING] Being Verified does **not** mean a seller can always sell. Being Suspended does **not** mean their identity was rejected. Mixing these up gives sellers wrong information and creates complaints.

### The seller earnings lifecycle
Sellers frequently ask "the customer paid, so where is my money?" Here is the confirmed lifecycle:

1. Customer pays
2. Payment is confirmed
3. The transaction is recorded
4. Platform fees are calculated
5. Seller earnings are calculated
6. Earnings are **pending** until release conditions are met
7. Earnings become **available** (withdrawable)
8. The seller requests a withdrawal
9. Byfluence processes it
10. The withdrawal is recorded as completed after confirmation

Balances a seller might see:

| Balance | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Total Earnings | Everything earned so far |
| Pending Balance | Earned but not yet released (e.g., session not completed, dispute open) |
| Available Balance | Can be withdrawn now |
| Withdrawal Pending | Requested and being processed |
| Withdrawn Amount | Already paid out |

### Why "paid" is not the same as "withdrawable"
A successful customer payment does not immediately become withdrawable because the consultation may not be completed yet, there may be a dispute, a cancellation or refund may happen, and settlement rules apply. **Payout methods, minimum amounts and timelines are not confirmed** — never tell a seller "your money will be available in X days."

### Choosing the right path by role
| Who | Common KB categories | Common departments |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer | Registration, Login & Account Access, Buyer Account, Consultations, Group Consultations, Payments, Refunds | Payments & Finance, Technical, Senior Support |
| Seller | Seller Account, Listings & Storefront, Seller Earnings & Payouts, Consultations (seller side) | Seller Operations, Payments & Finance, Trust & Safety |
| Both | Trust & Safety, Technical, General | Depends on the issue |

## Realistic Examples

### Example 1: Seller confused about suspension
**Customer (Telegram, Amharic):**
> መለያዬ ተረጋግጧል (Verified) ይላል ግን አገልግሎቴ ከገጹ ጠፍቷል። ምን ተፈጠረ?

*(My account says Verified but my service disappeared from the site. What happened?)*

**Agent reasoning:** Verified is the verification status. The listing disappearing suggests either a listing review/rejection or an operational restriction (Suspended). The agent checks the seller status through authorized tools if available. If the seller is Suspended, the agent does not speculate about the reason, creates a ticket (Seller Account → Restrictions, Suspension & Reactivation) and escalates to Seller Operations.

**Model reply (AM, extract):**
> ስለ ጥያቄዎ እናመሰግናለን። "Verified" ማለት የማንነት ማረጋገጫዎ ተጠናቋል ማለት ነው፤ ዝርዝሮችዎ በገጹ ላይ መታየታቸው ግን በሌላ ሁኔታ ሊወሰን ይችላል። ጉዳዩን ለሻጭ ኦፕሬሽንስ ቡድን አስተላልፌያለሁ፤ የጉዳይዎ ቁጥር {{ticket_id}} ነው። ውጤቱን እንዳወቅን እናሳውቅዎታለን።

### Example 2: "The buyer paid yesterday, why can't I withdraw?"
**Customer (email, English):**
> A client booked and paid for my session yesterday. My Available Balance is still zero. Is Byfluence holding my money?

**Agent reasoning:** The session probably has not been completed yet, so the earnings are in the Pending Balance. The agent explains the lifecycle without quoting a release time.

**Model reply (EN, extract):**
> Thank you for checking. When a client pays, the earnings first appear as pending and move to your Available Balance once the release conditions are met — for consultations this includes the session being completed. You should see this payment under your Pending Balance. If it is missing there, reply with the booking ID and I'll investigate under case {{ticket_id}}.

### Example 3: Buyer who is also a seller
**Customer (Telegram, English):**
> I can't log in. I have a session to deliver tonight AND one I booked tomorrow.

**Agent reasoning:** One person, two roles, one root problem (login). One ticket for the login issue, with both booking IDs recorded in the references. Priority High because the seller session is today — a client is waiting on them.

## Practical Exercises
1. **Map the buyer journey.** Open the Consultations category in the Knowledge Base. For each of the eight journey steps in this lesson, find one article that covers a failure at that step and write down its title.
2. **Status matrix drill.** In the Knowledge Base, search "suspended" and "verification". Using what you find, write a one-line customer explanation (EN and AM) for each of these: Pending, Rejected, Verified + Suspended. Have a colleague check that none of your lines promises a date or reveals internal reasons.
3. **Earnings explainer.** Search "pending balance". Using the article and the Response Templates, prepare a message to a seller who asks why their Available Balance is lower than their Total Earnings. Fill the placeholders and copy it in Amharic.
4. **Role triage.** Your trainer will give you six short customer messages. For each, decide: buyer, seller or both; KB category; likely escalation department. Record your answers in a practice ticket's internal notes.
5. **Feature check for sellers.** A seller asks whether they can sell e-books. Open Feature Status and the Digital Products category, then write the reply you would send.

## Knowledge Check
Q: A seller is Verified but Suspended. What does that mean?
- [ ] Their identity verification was rejected
- [ ] They can sell normally but cannot withdraw
- [x] Their identity is confirmed, but they are currently restricted from operating
- [ ] They have been permanently banned
> Verification status and operational status are separate. Verified + Suspended means identity confirmed, operation restricted.

Q: When is a consultation booking confirmed?
- [x] After the payment is successful
- [ ] As soon as the buyer selects a time slot
- [ ] When the seller accepts the Google Meet invitation
- [ ] After the consultation is completed
> The confirmed journey is: schedule → pay → booking confirmed after successful payment.

Q: A seller asks why money from yesterday's booking is not withdrawable. What is the best explanation?
- [ ] Byfluence is holding the money for an unknown reason
- [ ] Withdrawals always take a fixed number of days
- [x] Earnings stay pending until release conditions (such as session completion) are met, then become available
- [ ] The buyer has not paid yet
> A successful payment is not immediately withdrawable. Never quote a timeline — it is not confirmed.

Q: Which of these are the seller verification statuses?
- [x] Pending, Verified, Rejected
- [ ] Active, Suspended, Banned
- [ ] New, Open, Closed
- [ ] Successful, Failed, Refunded
> Active/Suspended/Banned are operational statuses; New/Open/Closed are ticket statuses; Successful/Failed/Refunded are payment statuses.

Q: A seller asks which ID documents they need for verification. No approved article lists them. What do you do?
- [ ] List the documents most Ethiopian platforms require
- [x] Explain that the verification form shows what is required, and escalate to Seller Operations if they are stuck
- [ ] Tell them any photo ID works
> Required documents are not confirmed. Do not invent a list.

Q: Where should a buyer first look for an upcoming consultation and its management actions?
- [x] The Consultation bookings area of the buyer dashboard
- [ ] Their email spam folder
- [ ] The seller's public profile
> The buyer dashboard shows upcoming sessions and booking-management actions.

Q: Which balance shows money a seller has requested to withdraw but has not yet received?
- [ ] Available Balance
- [ ] Total Earnings
- [x] Withdrawal Pending
- [ ] Withdrawn Amount
> Withdrawal Pending covers requested withdrawals still being processed.

Q: A buyer wants to order a physical product delivered to Addis Ababa. What is your first step?
- [ ] Walk them through the order process
- [x] Check the Feature Status page to see whether physical products and delivery are live
- [ ] Give them the seller's phone number
> Physical products and delivery are planned features. Confirm status before answering.
