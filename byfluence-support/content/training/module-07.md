# Seller Operations
id: trn-07
number: 7
duration: 60

## Learning Objectives
- Guide a prospective seller through the onboarding steps and troubleshoot common blockers at each one.
- Handle verification questions for Pending, Verified and Rejected sellers without inventing document requirements or rejection reasons.
- Explain operational restrictions (Suspended, Banned) neutrally and route them correctly.
- Support sellers with listings: creation, review and rejection, pricing and availability, and prohibited items.
- Explain earnings balances and handle withdrawal problems without quoting unconfirmed payout methods, minimums or timelines.
- Protect sellers from payout fraud and social engineering, especially requests to change payout details.
- Handle buyer–seller disputes neutrally, gathering evidence from both sides before escalating.

## Lesson

### Sellers are customers too
A seller's problems directly affect their income and their own customers. A stuck withdrawal can mean rent unpaid; a rejected listing can mean a lost week of business. Many Byfluence sellers are first-time online entrepreneurs — the mission is to help people start without big capital. Treat sellers with the same patience and respect as buyers, and remember that in a dispute you are **neutral**.

### Onboarding: the steps and the blockers
| Step | Common blocker | Your action |
|---|---|---|
| Create account | Code not received, login issues | Module 4 methods |
| Apply to become a seller | Can't find where to apply | Guide using the "Becoming a Seller" article |
| Provide personal/business info | Form errors, unclear fields | Ask for a screenshot of the error; Technical if it's a bug |
| Submit identity verification | Upload fails, file too large, unclear what is required | Troubleshoot upload (Technical Issues → Upload Failures); do not invent requirements |
| Wait for review | "When will I be approved?" | Explain it is in review; no date unless approved wording exists |
| Approved / Rejected | Rejection confusion | Approved wording only; Seller Operations |
| Complete profile, create listings | Listing rejected, can't publish | Listing articles; Seller Operations |
| Start selling once permissions granted | "Approved but can't sell" | Check operational status and listing status |

> [!NOTE] **Required verification documents are not confirmed.** Never give a seller a list of documents from memory or from another platform. The verification form and the approved article are the only sources.

### Verification status: Pending, Verified, Rejected
- **Pending** — the submission is in review. Confirm receipt if you can see it through authorized tools. Do not promise a decision date. If the seller has waited unusually long by your team's standard, escalate to Seller Operations with the submission date.
- **Verified** — identity confirmed. This does **not** automatically mean listings are approved or that the seller is Active.
- **Rejected** — share only the reason and next steps that Seller Operations has approved for the customer. If no approved wording is attached, say the team will explain and escalate. Whether and how a rejected seller can re-apply is a decision for Seller Operations.

> [!WARNING] Never tell a seller their verification was rejected "because the photo looked fake" or similar. Fraud-related reasons are handled by Trust & Safety and are not shared by Level 1.

### Operational status: Active, Suspended, Banned
A seller can be **Verified but Suspended** — identity is fine, but operation is restricted. Typical support response:
1. Confirm the status through authorized tools (if you have access).
2. Explain neutrally: "Your seller account is currently restricted and under review by the responsible team."
3. Ask whether they have open bookings — buyers may be affected and need their own tickets.
4. Escalate to **Seller Operations** (or **Trust & Safety** if the restriction relates to fraud or safety).
5. Do not promise reactivation; the appeals process is Policy Pending Approval unless your Policies section says otherwise.

### Listings
Sellers create listings with a description, price and availability. For consultations, a listing shows the consultant name, description, topic, duration, price and availability.
- **Creating & editing** — guide step by step; for upload errors collect file type and size, device and browser.
- **Review & rejections** — listing moderation is an admin function. Explain the result using approved wording; escalate disputes about the decision to Seller Operations.
- **Pricing & availability** — sellers set their own prices. Support never advises on what to charge. Availability mistakes (wrong time zone, double-booked slots) cause many consultation tickets — help sellers set availability in the correct time zone.
- **Prohibited & restricted items** — check the Policies section. Reports of fake or misleading listings go to Trust & Safety.

### Planned seller programs
**Inventory-free selling (local dropshipping)**, **physical products** and **digital products** are planned. Check Feature Status before discussing them. For inventory-free selling, remember the business definition: "zero investment" means **no upfront stock — not zero costs or zero risk**. Terms, eligibility and cost responsibilities are not defined, so never tell a seller what margin they will earn or what they will have to pay.

### Earnings and withdrawals
Review the lifecycle from Module 2: payment → confirmed → recorded → fees calculated → earnings calculated → **pending** until release conditions are met → **available** → withdrawal requested → Byfluence processes → recorded completed after confirmation.

| Seller says | Likely explanation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| "My Available Balance is zero but I had sales" | Earnings still pending (session not completed, dispute, settlement) | Explain the lifecycle; check the booking is marked completed |
| "My withdrawal is stuck in Withdrawal Pending" | Being processed, or a problem with the payout | Check through authorized tools; escalate to Payments & Finance with the request date and amount |
| "The withdrawal shows completed but I didn't receive it" | Payout delivered to wrong details, or provider delay | Payments & Finance; check if payout details changed recently → Trust & Safety |
| "Why was a fee deducted?" | Platform fees | Commission rates are not confirmed — do not explain a percentage; escalate to Payments & Finance for the breakdown |
| "My earnings are on hold" | Possible dispute or restriction | Neutral explanation; Seller Operations / Payments & Finance |

> [!POLICY] Payout methods, minimum withdrawal amounts and payout timelines are **not confirmed**. Never say "withdrawals arrive in X days" or "the minimum is X ETB."

### Payout security and social engineering
Payout details are a top target for fraud.
- **Never change payout details** (bank account, mobile-money number) based on a chat, email or phone request. If the platform has a self-service flow, guide the verified seller to it; otherwise escalate to Trust & Safety.
- Be alert to people who claim to be "the seller's manager", "her brother", or "Byfluence finance team" asking about balances or payout changes. Only the verified account holder gets account information.
- If a seller reports a payout they didn't request, treat it as **Critical** account compromise (Module 4).

### Buyer–seller disputes
Disputes include "the session was not what was described", "the buyer was abusive", "the buyer is demanding a refund the seller doesn't agree with".
1. Stay neutral; don't take a side in your wording.
2. Collect evidence from both sides: listing as it was, booking details, messages, screenshots, timing.
3. Set **Waiting for Seller** or **Waiting for Customer** while you wait for each side.
4. Escalate to **Senior Support / Supervisor** with a balanced summary. Trust & Safety if there is harassment, fraud or a safety concern.
5. Byfluence's authorized personnel decide; you explain the decision once it is made.

## Realistic Examples

### Example 1: Waiting for verification (Amharic)
**Seller (Telegram):**
> ማመልከቻዬን ከላኩ ቆይቻለሁ፤ አሁንም "Pending" ይላል። መቼ ነው የሚጸድቀው?

*(I sent my application a while ago; it still says "Pending". When will it be approved?)*

**Model reply (AM):**
> ሰላም፣ ስለ ትዕግስትዎ እናመሰግናለን። ማመልከቻዎ በግምገማ ላይ ነው። ትክክለኛ ቀን ቃል መግባት ባልችልም፣ ጉዳዩን ለሻጭ ኦፕሬሽንስ ቡድን አሳውቄያለሁ። የጉዳይዎ ቁጥር {{ticket_id}} ነው፤ አዲስ መረጃ እንዳገኘን እናሳውቅዎታለን።

**Ticket:** Seller Account → Identity Verification; priority Medium; escalated to Seller Operations with the submission date.

### Example 2: Request to change payout number (English)
**Caller (phone):**
> I'm calling for my sister, she's a seller. She changed her phone, please update her payout number to this new one.

**Agent action:** Politely declines to discuss or change the account with a third party, explains that only the account holder can make this request through the verified process, and records the call. Because this matches a social-engineering pattern, adds an internal note and informs Trust & Safety.

### Example 3: Fee question
**Seller (email, English):**
> My client paid 1,000 birr but my earnings show less. How much commission do you take?

**Agent action:** Does not state or calculate a commission rate (not confirmed). Explains that platform fees are calculated on each transaction, escalates to Payments & Finance for the breakdown of this specific transaction, and gives the case number.

### Example 4: Dispute (Amharic)
**Seller (Telegram):**
> ደንበኛው ሙሉ ሰዓቱን ተጠቅሞ አሁን ገንዘቤን መልሱልኝ እያለ ነው።

*(The client used the full session and now says "give me my money back".)*

**Agent action:** Thanks the seller, asks for the consultation ID and any evidence (start/end times, messages), explains the case will be reviewed fairly with both sides, and links the seller's ticket to the buyer's complaint ticket by reference in internal notes.

## Practical Exercises
1. **Onboarding walkthrough.** Open the Seller Account Management category. For each onboarding step in this lesson, find the article you would use and note its escalation department.
2. **Status explanations.** Write approved-style replies (EN and AM) for: Pending; Verified + Suspended; Rejected without approved reason. Ask your trainer to check that you revealed nothing internal.
3. **Withdrawal ticket.** Create a practice ticket for a seller whose withdrawal shows completed but was not received: category Seller Earnings & Payouts → Delayed & Failed Payouts, priority, references, internal note checking for recent payout-detail changes, escalation to Payments & Finance.
4. **SOP check.** Search SOPs for seller verification or payout handling. Compare the SOP steps with this lesson and note anything the SOP adds.
5. **Feature Status for sellers.** A seller asks to join inventory-free selling. Check Feature Status and the Inventory-Free Selling category, then write your reply in Amharic.
