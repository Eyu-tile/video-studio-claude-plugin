# Consultation Support
id: trn-05
number: 5
duration: 60

## Learning Objectives
- Explain the one-on-one consultation flow from booking to completion and locate where a customer's problem sits in it.
- Troubleshoot missing, wrong or non-working Google Meet links quickly when a session is about to start.
- Convert confidently between international time, Ethiopian clock time and other time zones, and between Ethiopian and Gregorian dates.
- Handle late arrivals and no-shows by buyers or sellers fairly, collecting evidence without deciding the outcome.
- Handle rescheduling and cancellation requests while the official rules are Policy Pending Approval.
- Apply the same principles to group consultations and recognise the additional unresolved rules.
- Set the right ticket priority for consultation issues based on how soon the session starts.

## Lesson

### How a consultation booking works
One-on-one consultations are the core MVP service. The confirmed flow is:

1. Buyer discovers a consultant and evaluates the listing (name, description, topic, duration, price, availability).
2. Buyer selects a date and time.
3. Buyer pays.
4. **Booking is confirmed after successful payment.**
5. Buyer receives the **Google Meet** details.
6. Both participants join at the scheduled time.
7. The consultation is marked **completed**.
8. Seller earnings become available according to settlement rules.

Most consultation tickets fall into five groups, which match the Knowledge Base subcategories: **Booking & Confirmation**, **Google Meet Links & Access**, **Late Arrivals & No-Shows**, **Rescheduling & Cancellations**, and **Session Complaints & Completion**. Identify the group first, then open the matching article.

### Priority depends on the clock
For consultation issues, urgency is driven by **when the session starts**.

| Situation | Suggested priority |
|---|---|
| Session is happening now or about to start and the customer cannot join | **Critical** |
| Session is soon (e.g., today or within the next 24 hours) and booking/link is not right | **High** |
| Session is days away; booking or link question | **Medium** |
| General question about how bookings work | **Low** |

> [!TIP] When a session is about to start, solve the access problem first and investigate later. Ask for the consultation ID and the exact problem in one short message, not a long form.

### Google Meet links
The system associates a Google Meet meeting with each booking. Whether the link is generated automatically or provided by the seller is **not yet confirmed** (see the Feature Status note for Google Meet) — check the article before explaining it.

Checklist when a customer cannot join:
- [ ] Is the booking actually **confirmed** (not pending payment)? No confirmation usually means no link.
- [ ] Is the customer looking in the right place — the booking in the buyer dashboard, and any confirmation message?
- [ ] Are they using the **same Google account / email** the invitation was sent to, if the meeting requires it?
- [ ] Are they joining at the right time **in their time zone**?
- [ ] Device issues: Google Meet app installed or updated on mobile, browser permissions for camera/microphone, stable connection.
- [ ] Does the seller have the same link? A mismatch is a Technical issue.

If the link is missing or wrong for a confirmed booking, escalate to **Technical** and, if the session is imminent, notify a supervisor so someone can contact the seller side quickly.

> [!WARNING] **A scheduled meeting does not prove the service was delivered.** A link existing in the system tells you nothing about whether both people joined or the session took place.

### Time zones and Ethiopian time
Ethiopia uses East Africa Time (**UTC+3**) and does not change clocks for daylight saving. Many customers also use the **Ethiopian clock**, where the day's hours are counted from about 6:00 AM (sunrise). Ethiopian clock hour = international hour minus 6.

| International time | Ethiopian clock (spoken) |
|---|---|
| 7:00 AM | ጠዋት 1 ሰዓት |
| 9:00 AM | ጠዋት 3 ሰዓት |
| 10:00 AM | ረፋድ 4 ሰዓት |
| 12:00 noon | ቀን 6 ሰዓት (እኩለ ቀን) |
| 2:00 PM | ከቀኑ 8 ሰዓት |
| 4:00 PM | ከቀኑ 10 ሰዓት |
| 6:00 PM | ምሽት 12 ሰዓት |
| 8:00 PM | ምሽት 2 ሰዓት |
| 12:00 midnight | ሌሊት 6 ሰዓት |

The classic mistake: a customer says "3 ሰዓት" meaning 9:00 AM; an agent or seller hears "3 o'clock" (3:00 PM or 3:00 AM). **Always confirm with both systems:** "ጠዋት 3 ሰዓት ማለትዎ ነው — ማለትም 9:00 AM?"

**Diaspora customers** add a third clock. For example, Dubai is one hour ahead of Addis Ababa; London is three hours behind in winter; Washington, D.C. is eight hours behind in winter (seven in summer, because those places change their clocks and Ethiopia does not). The time zone the platform displays to each user is not confirmed, so ask the customer what time they see on their booking and compare it with what the record shows.

**Dates** can also be confusing. The Ethiopian calendar has 13 months and is roughly seven to eight years behind the Gregorian calendar (for example, 18 November 2026 is ኅዳር 9, 2019 ዓ.ም.). When a customer gives a date, confirm which calendar they mean. In templates, fill `{{booking_date}}` and `{{booking_time}}` with the time zone written out, e.g. "9:00 AM Addis Ababa time (ጠዋት 3 ሰዓት)".

### Late arrivals and no-shows
Possible cases: the buyer is late, the seller is late, the buyer did not join, the seller did not join, both joined but could not connect, or the session ended early.

What you do in every case:
1. Get each side's account of what happened — times they tried to join, any messages exchanged, screenshots of the Meet screen or error.
2. Check the booking and any records available to you through authorized tools.
3. Record facts in the ticket, not judgements ("Buyer states they joined at 9:05 and waited" — not "Seller didn't show").
4. Contact the seller through the proper process and set the ticket to **Waiting for Seller** while you wait.
5. Escalate to **Senior Support / Supervisor** for any request to refund, re-book or compensate.

> [!POLICY] Rules for no-shows (what happens to payment, whether a free re-booking is offered, how late counts as "absent") are **Policy Pending Approval**. Do not promise a refund, a new session or a penalty for the seller.

### Rescheduling and cancellations
Customers often ask "Can I move my session?" or "I want to cancel and get my money back." The cancellation window, any fees and refund eligibility are **not yet approved**. Your job:
- Check whether the buyer dashboard offers a reschedule or cancel action for that booking and guide them to it if the article confirms it exists.
- If not, collect the consultation ID, the reason and preferred new times, contact the seller side through the proper process, and escalate refund questions.
- If the **seller** asks to reschedule, the buyer must agree; never move a booking on one side's word alone.

### Group consultations
In a group consultation one professional serves many buyers. The seller sets the date, duration, price and capacity; buyers register and pay; confirmed participants receive meeting access. Rules for **capacity, full sessions, cancellation, rescheduling, refunds, missed sessions and seller no-shows are not yet approved**. Typical group issues:
- "I paid but the session shows full" — treat like paid-but-not-confirmed; escalate to Payments & Finance.
- One participant cannot join while others can — usually account/email or device; troubleshoot as above.
- Seller cancels the whole session — one ticket per affected buyer, or one master ticket referenced by the others, as your supervisor directs; escalate to Senior Support.

### Session complaints and completion
Complaints about quality ("the advice was useless", "it was shorter than listed") are buyer–seller disputes. Remember the facilitator role: the seller delivers the service. Collect the listing details (stated duration and topic), the buyer's specific complaint, and the seller's response, then escalate to Senior Support. Never tell a buyer the complaint will result in a refund.

## Realistic Examples

### Example 1: Link missing, session soon (Amharic)
**Customer (Telegram):**
> ሰላም፣ ዛሬ ከቀኑ 8 ሰዓት ምክክር አለኝ ግን የGoogle Meet ሊንኩ የትም የለም። ከፍያለሁ።

*(Hello, I have a consultation today at 2 PM but the Google Meet link isn't anywhere. I've paid.)*

**Agent action:** Priority **High** (today). Asks for the consultation ID; checks the booking is confirmed; guides the customer to the booking in their dashboard. Link missing for a confirmed booking → escalate to Technical and notify supervisor.

**Model reply (AM):**
> ሰላም፣ ስለተፈጠረው ችግር ይቅርታ እንጠይቃለን። ምክክርዎ ዛሬ ከቀኑ 8 ሰዓት (2:00 PM) እንደሆነ ተረድቻለሁ። እባክዎ የምክክሩን መለያ ቁጥር (consultation ID) ይላኩልኝ፤ እንዲሁም በዳሽቦርድዎ ላይ "ቀጠሮዎች" ውስጥ ያለውን ቀጠሮ ይመልከቱ። ወዲያውኑ እያጣራሁ ነው። የጉዳይዎ ቁጥር {{ticket_id}} ነው።

### Example 2: Diaspora time-zone confusion (English)
**Customer (email):**
> I'm in Washington DC. My booking says 9:00. I joined at 9 AM my time and nobody was there. I want a refund.

**Agent reasoning:** If the booking was 9:00 AM Addis Ababa time, that is 1:00 AM in Washington in winter — the customer joined eight hours late. The agent checks the time shown on the booking, explains gently, does not blame, and escalates the refund/re-book request to Senior Support because the rule is pending.

**Model reply (EN, extract):**
> I'm sorry for the confusion. Your booking {{consultation_id}} was scheduled for {{booking_time}}, which is Addis Ababa time — that was earlier in the night in Washington. I've recorded what happened under case {{ticket_id}} and passed your request to the team that reviews rescheduling and refunds. I'll update you as soon as I have their answer.

### Example 3: Seller says the buyer never came
**Customer (seller, Telegram, Amharic):**
> ደንበኛው አልመጣም። ለ20 ደቂቃ ጠብቄዋለሁ። ክፍያዬ ይከፈለኛል?

*(The client didn't come. I waited 20 minutes. Will I get paid?)*

**Agent action:** Thanks the seller, asks for the consultation ID and what time they joined, records it, contacts the buyer for their side, sets the ticket to Waiting for Customer, and explains that earnings for this booking will be handled according to Byfluence's review — no promise either way.

### Example 4: Group session full after payment
A buyer paid for a group session but the page now shows "full" and their booking is not listed. The agent follows the paid-but-not-confirmed article, records the transaction reference, priority High if the session is soon, and escalates to Payments & Finance.

## Practical Exercises
1. **Time conversion drill.** Convert these to the Ethiopian clock and back: 8:30 AM, 1:00 PM, 5:00 PM, 9:00 PM. Then convert 9:00 AM Addis Ababa time to Dubai, London and Washington, D.C. time (winter and summer).
2. **Link checklist in a ticket.** Create a practice ticket for Example 1 with category Consultations → Google Meet Links & Access, priority High, consultation reference filled, and an internal note that records each checklist item as you go.
3. **No-show evidence.** Open the no-show article in the Knowledge Base. Write the exact questions you would send to the buyer and to the seller (EN and AM), then set the correct "Waiting for…" status for each step.
4. **Pending policy handling.** Find the policy document that covers consultation cancellations or refunds and locate its Policy Pending Approval marker. Write the message you would send to a buyer who asks "If I cancel today, do I get all my money back?"
5. **Template filling.** Find a consultation template that uses `{{booking_date}}` and `{{booking_time}}`. Fill it for a booking at 4:00 PM Addis Ababa time, writing the time in both international and Ethiopian clock forms.
