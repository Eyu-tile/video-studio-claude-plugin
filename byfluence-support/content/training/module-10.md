# Final Support Assessment
id: trn-10
number: 10
duration: 120

## Learning Objectives
- Handle a complete customer case end to end — from the first message to a documented resolution — using every part of the support app.
- Translate a customer's words (Amharic or English) into the right search terms, Knowledge Base article and Response Template.
- Create tickets with correct category, subcategory, priority, references and status, and keep them accurate as the case moves.
- Choose the correct escalation level and department and write escalation notes that need no follow-up questions.
- Apply every safety rule under pressure: no promises, no invented policy, no OTP/password/PIN, privacy protected, planned features checked on Feature Status.
- Demonstrate readiness to handle live customers through the scenario assessment and final quiz.

## Lesson

### How the final assessment works
This module brings together Modules 1–9. It has three parts:

1. **Scenario practice** — ten realistic cases in *Realistic Examples*, each with a model answer. Read the customer message, cover the model answer, and decide your own approach first. Then compare.
2. **Hands-on assessment** — in *Practical Exercises*, you work the scenarios inside the support app on training tickets, and your trainer reviews your tickets, notes and drafted replies.
3. **Final quiz** — the *Knowledge Check* at the end. Your trainer sets the pass mark and decides whether any module must be repeated.

> [!NOTE] There are no trick questions. Every scenario has a safe, correct path that uses only confirmed facts, the Knowledge Base, templates and escalation. If you find yourself inventing a rule, a date or an amount, stop — that is the mistake the scenario is testing.

### The end-to-end workflow
Use this sequence for every case. It is the backbone of the assessment.

| Step | What you do | App area |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Read & identify | Who is it (buyer/seller/both)? What happened? How urgent? Which language? | — |
| 2. Search | Type the customer's own words, in Amharic or English, plus your keywords | Global search |
| 3. Check existing ticket | Search by email, phone or reference | Tickets |
| 4. Open the article | Causes, info to collect, troubleshooting, responses, escalation, internal notes | Knowledge Base / Troubleshooting Guides |
| 5. Check status & rules | Feature Status for any feature/payment method; Policies for rules and "Policy Pending Approval"; Announcements for incidents | Feature Status, Policies, Announcements |
| 6. Create / update ticket | Customer info, channel, category, subcategory, priority, references | Tickets |
| 7. First reply | Template or article response, correct language, all placeholders filled, case number | Response Templates |
| 8. Log & set status | Communication log entry; status (Open, Waiting for…); follow-up | Tickets |
| 9. Investigate / escalate | Through authorized tools; Escalation Center with a complete note | Escalation Center |
| 10. Resolve & close | Root cause, actions, resolution, customer notified; close per team rule | Tickets |

### How your work is assessed
| Area | What "ready" looks like | Common failure |
|---|---|---|
| Safety | Never asks for OTP/password/PIN; never shares other users' data; reminds customers about codes in login/payment cases | Asking a customer to "confirm the code" |
| Accuracy | Facts match the Knowledge Base, Feature Status and Policies | Saying a planned feature is available |
| No promises | Neutral wording; `{{estimated_resolution_time}}` only with approved wording | "Refund in 3 days" |
| Ticket quality | Correct category, priority, references in fields, notes, log, follow-ups | References buried in description; no follow-up |
| Escalation | Right level and department; complete note | "Pls check urgent" |
| Communication | Amharic by default, respectful እርስዎ form, empathy, concise, case number | English reply to an Amharic customer; unfilled placeholders |
| Resolution | All four resolution fields; resolved only when truly resolved | Resolving at escalation time |

### Priority quick reference
| Priority | Typical triggers |
|---|---|
| Critical | Money or account at active risk; session happening now and blocked; safety threat; widespread incident |
| High | Financial harm already happened (duplicate charge, paid but not confirmed); session soon; seller restricted with bookings imminent |
| Medium | Real problem without an immediate deadline (verification pending, withdrawal question) |
| Low | Information requests; questions about planned features |

### Departments quick reference
| Department | Typical cases |
|---|---|
| Senior Support / Supervisor (L2) | Disputes, refund recommendations, Policy Pending Approval decisions, repeated unresolved issues, no access |
| Payments & Finance (L3) | Payment verification, duplicates, refunds processing, payouts |
| Technical (L3) | Bugs, outages, booking errors, Google Meet links, notifications |
| Seller Operations (L3) | Verification review, listing review, seller restrictions |
| Trust & Safety (L3) | Fraud, account compromise, harassment, privacy incidents |
| Logistics & Fulfillment (L3) | Delivery (planned feature) |
| Management (L4) | Legal, policy exceptions, high-value or reputational issues |

### Traps the scenarios are designed to test
Each scenario hides at least one trap. Experienced agents spot them in the first read.

| Trap | Where it appears | Safe behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| The customer offers a code or PIN | Login and payment cases | Refuse, never use it, remind them, note it |
| The customer demands a date ("by tonight", "today") | Refunds, withdrawals, threats | Acknowledge the urgency, give the case number, no date unless approved |
| The question is about a planned feature | Delivery, products, payment methods | Check Feature Status first; never present planned as live |
| The answer depends on an undecided rule | Refunds, no-shows, cancellations, group sessions | Policy Pending Approval → escalate to Senior Support, be honest |
| Someone asks about another person | Spouses, relatives, "managers", fake staff | Privacy first; only the verified account holder |
| Two problems in one message | Login + session today; double charge + threat | One ticket, priority set by the most urgent impact, all references recorded |
| The clock is ambiguous | "3 ሰዓት", diaspora customers | Confirm both Ethiopian and international time, and the time zone |
| A screenshot is the only proof | Payment disputes, refund claims | Verify by transaction reference; escalate suspicions without accusing |

When you review your own answers, go through this table and ask: "Did I fall into any of these?" If you did, rewrite your reply before comparing it with the model answer.

### Final reminders
- **Search in the customer's words.** Tags include Amharic terms like "ክፍያ ተቆርጧል" and "ኮድ አልደረሰኝም".
- **One problem, one ticket.** Repeat contacts go in the communication log.
- **The app sends nothing.** You send in the channel, then log it.
- **Pending means pending.** Escalate, be honest, follow up.
- **When unsure, ask your supervisor** — that is always a correct answer in training and in real life.

## Realistic Examples

### Scenario 1: Paid, booking pending, session tomorrow
**Customer (Telegram, Amharic):**
> ሰላም፣ ለነገ ጠዋት 3 ሰዓት ምክክር ከፈልኩ። ገንዘቡ ተቆርጧል ግን ቀጠሮዬ አሁንም "pending" ነው።

**Model answer:**
- **Search:** "ክፍያ ተቆርጧል", "paid but no booking".
- **Article:** *Customer Paid but Consultation Booking Was Not Confirmed*.
- **Template:** request transaction reference (Amharic, Telegram).
- **Ticket:** Payments → Paid but Not Confirmed / Wrong Amounts · Priority **High** (session tomorrow) · consultation reference · transaction reference when received · status Waiting for Customer + follow-up.
- **Investigate:** verify reference through authorized tools. Successful + booking pending → **Escalate to Payments & Finance** with a full note (session tomorrow 9:00 AM Addis Ababa time / ጠዋት 3 ሰዓት).
- **Resolution:** booking confirmed and Meet details received; root cause recorded (e.g., delayed provider confirmation); customer notified and logged.
- **Reply (AM):** "ሰላም {{customer_name}}፣ ስለተፈጠረው ስጋት ይቅርታ እንጠይቃለን። ለማጣራት እባክዎ በክፍያ ኤስኤምኤስዎ ወይም ደረሰኝዎ ላይ ያለውን የግብይት ቁጥር ይላኩልኝ። ፒን ወይም ኮድ በፍጹም አያጋሩ። የጉዳይዎ ቁጥር {{ticket_id}} ነው።"

### Scenario 2: Code not received, customer sends a code
**Customer (Telegram, English):**
> I never got the registration code. Wait, now I got one: 5 1 7 3 9 0. Can you activate my account?

**Model answer:**
- **Search:** "verification code", "code not received".
- **Article:** the verification-code / OTP article in Account Registration.
- **Action:** do **not** use or repeat the code. Tell them never to share codes; ask them to enter the newest code themselves on the registration page.
- **Ticket:** Account Registration → Email, Phone & OTP Verification · Priority Low/Medium · internal note "customer shared a code in chat; not used; advised".
- **Escalate:** only if the code repeatedly fails → Technical with device/browser/time details.
- **Reply (EN):** "Thank you — please don't share codes with anyone, including us; we will never ask for them. Please enter the newest code yourself on the registration page. If it shows an error, send me a screenshot of the error message (not the code). Case: {{ticket_id}}"

### Scenario 3: Seller didn't join, buyer wants money back
**Customer (Telegram, Amharic):**
> አማካሪው አልመጣም። Google Meet ላይ ብቻዬን ጠበቅሁ። ገንዘቤን መልሱልኝ።

**Model answer:**
- **Search:** "seller no-show", "አማካሪው አልመጣም".
- **Article:** seller no-show (Consultations → Late Arrivals & No-Shows); check Policies for the no-show/refund marker.
- **Ticket:** Consultations → Late Arrivals & No-Shows · Priority High · consultation reference · status Waiting for Seller after contacting seller side + follow-up.
- **Collect:** consultation ID, when they joined, screenshots; seller's account.
- **Escalate:** refund request → **Senior Support / Supervisor** (Policy Pending Approval).
- **Do not:** promise refund or re-booking; conclude the seller was at fault before hearing both sides.
- **Reply (AM):** "ሰላም {{customer_name}}፣ ጠብቀው ምክክሩ ባለመካሄዱ ይቅርታ እንጠይቃለን። እባክዎ የምክክሩን መለያ ቁጥር እና ወደ Google Meet የገቡበትን ሰዓት ይላኩልኝ። ጉዳዩን ከሁለቱም ወገን አጣርተን ለሚመለከተው ቡድን እናቀርባለን፤ ውሳኔው እንደደረሰን እናሳውቅዎታለን። የጉዳይዎ ቁጥር {{ticket_id}} ነው።"

### Scenario 4: Group session full after payment
**Customer (email, English):**
> I paid for the Saturday group workshop but now it says "Session full" and I'm not on the list.

**Model answer:**
- **Search:** "group session full", "paid group".
- **Article:** Group Consultations → Registration & Capacity; paid-but-not-confirmed for the payment side.
- **Ticket:** Group Consultations → Registration & Capacity · Priority High if Saturday is close · transaction reference + consultation reference.
- **Escalate:** Payments & Finance (payment recorded, no seat). Capacity/refund rules are pending → note for Senior Support.
- **Reply (EN):** acknowledge, request the transaction reference, explain the team is checking the payment and the session registration, case number, no promise of a seat or refund.

### Scenario 5: Withdrawal stuck
**Customer (seller, Telegram, Amharic):**
> ገንዘብ ለማውጣት ጠይቄ "Withdrawal Pending" ላይ ቆሟል። ለምንድን ነው?

**Model answer:**
- **Search:** "withdrawal pending", "ገንዘብ ማውጣት".
- **Article:** Seller Earnings & Payouts → Delayed & Failed Payouts.
- **Ticket:** Payouts → Delayed & Failed Payouts · Priority Medium · amount and request date in notes.
- **Check:** recent payout-detail changes (if yes and not by the seller → Trust & Safety, Critical).
- **Escalate:** Payments & Finance.
- **Do not:** quote a payout timeline (not confirmed).
- **Reply (AM):** "ሰላም {{customer_name}}፣ ስለጠየቁን እናመሰግናለን። የገንዘብ ማውጣት ጥያቄዎ በሂደት ላይ ነው። ጉዳዩን ለክፍያ እና ፋይናንስ ቡድን አሳውቄያለሁ፤ መረጃ እንዳገኘሁ አሳውቅዎታለሁ። የጉዳይዎ ቁጥር {{ticket_id}} ነው።"

### Scenario 6: Seller account takeover
**Customer (email, English, from the registered address):**
> Someone changed my phone number on Byfluence last night and there's a withdrawal I didn't make!

**Model answer:**
- **Search:** "unauthorized access", "account hacked".
- **Article:** Login & Account Access → Recovery & Unauthorized Access.
- **Ticket:** Account Access → Recovery & Unauthorized Access · Priority **Critical**.
- **Escalate immediately:** **Trust & Safety**, plus **Payments & Finance** for the withdrawal.
- **Advise:** change password via official reset and secure their email account; never share codes.
- **Do not:** promise the money will be recovered.

### Scenario 7: Delivery to Adama
**Customer (Telegram, Amharic):**
> ጫማ ማዘዝ እፈልጋለሁ፤ አዳማ ድረስ ታደርሳላችሁ? ክፍያው ስንት ነው?

**Model answer:**
- **Search:** "delivery", "ማድረስ".
- **Check:** Feature Status → physical products and delivery are **planned**; delivery areas and fees not confirmed.
- **Ticket:** log per team rule (Physical Products → Shipping & Delivery, Low) — useful demand data.
- **No escalation.**
- **Reply (AM):** "ሰላም፣ ስለጠየቁን እናመሰግናለን። የቁሳዊ ምርቶች ሽያጭ እና የማድረስ አገልግሎት በዝግጅት ላይ ናቸው፤ የሚደርስባቸው አካባቢዎች እና ክፍያዎች ገና አልተወሰኑም። ሲጀመር በይፋዊ ቻናሎቻችን እናሳውቃለን።"

### Scenario 8: Seller suspended with a session today
**Customer (seller, phone, Amharic):** "መለያዬ 'Suspended' ይላል፤ ዛሬ ከቀኑ 10 ሰዓት ደንበኛ አለኝ!"

**Model answer:**
- **Article:** Seller Account → Restrictions, Suspension & Reactivation.
- **Ticket:** Seller Account → Restrictions, Suspension & Reactivation · Priority **High** (session today, 4:00 PM) · consultation reference · communication log for the call.
- **Escalate:** Seller Operations (Trust & Safety if fraud-related); note that a buyer session today is affected so the buyer can be informed.
- **Do not:** reveal reasons or promise reactivation.

### Scenario 9: Double charge plus public threat
**Customer (Telegram, Amharic):**
> ሁለት ጊዜ ቆርጣችሁብኛል! እስከ ማታ ካልመለሳችሁ ፌስቡክ ላይ እለጥፋለሁ።

**Model answer:**
- **Search:** "charged twice", "ሁለት ጊዜ ተቆርጧል".
- **Article:** duplicate charge (Payments → Paid but Not Confirmed / Wrong Amounts).
- **Ticket:** Priority High · both references · supervisor informed about the social-media threat.
- **Escalate:** Payments & Finance.
- **Communication:** CALM method; no promise "by tonight".
- **Reply (AM):** "ሁለት ጊዜ መቆረጡ እንዳበሳጨዎት ይገባኛል፤ ይቅርታ እንጠይቃለን። ይህንን ጉዳይ እኔ እከታተለዋለሁ። እባክዎ የሁለቱንም ክፍያዎች የግብይት ቁጥሮች ይላኩልኝ። የጉዳይዎ ቁጥር {{ticket_id}} ነው፤ መረጃ እንዳገኘሁ አሳውቅዎታለሁ።"

### Scenario 10: Privacy probe
**Caller (phone, English):**
> I think my husband is booking sessions behind my back. Tell me if his number has an account and what he booked.

**Model answer:**
- **Policy:** privacy — never confirm whether someone has an account or share their activity.
- **Reply:** "I understand this is a difficult situation. For privacy reasons, I can't share any information about another person's account. If you have concerns about your own account, I'm happy to help with that."
- **Ticket:** log per team rule (Trust & Safety → Privacy & Data Requests, Low); escalate to Trust & Safety only if there are signs of a safety risk.

## Practical Exercises
1. **Work all ten scenarios in the app.** For each, create a training ticket with customer info, channel, category, subcategory, priority, references, a first reply copied from the correct template (with all placeholders filled) and logged in the communication log, the right status and a follow-up.
2. **Escalate three of them.** Choose Scenarios 1, 6 and 8. Escalate each through the Escalation Center with a complete escalation note (what, facts, done, why, urgency, language).
3. **Resolve two.** Your trainer will play the department and send you an outcome for Scenarios 1 and 5. Resolve those tickets with root cause, actions, resolution and customer-notified, then send and log the resolution message in Amharic.
4. **Feature Status and Policies check.** For Scenarios 3, 4, 7 and 9, write which Feature Status entry or Policy Pending Approval marker you checked and what it changed in your reply.
5. **Live-style role-play.** Your trainer plays a customer in a new, unseen scenario (Amharic or English) under time pressure. Handle it end to end while your trainer scores you against the assessment table in this lesson.

- [x] Do not use it, tell them never to share codes, and note it in internal notes
- [ ] Use it to activate their account quickly
- [ ] Forward it to Technical

Q: A seller reports an unrequested withdrawal and a changed phone number. Which priority and departments?
- [ ] Medium; Seller Operations
- [ ] High; Technical

Q: A buyer asks for a refund after a seller no-show. Refund rules are Policy Pending Approval. Which escalation is correct?
- [x] Senior Support / Supervisor, after collecting both sides' facts
- [ ] Logistics & Fulfillment
- [ ] Management immediately
- [ ] No escalation; refuse the refund
> Pending refund decisions go to Level 2 with evidence.

Q: A customer asks about delivery to Adama. Feature Status shows physical products as planned. What is correct?
- [x] Explain that products and delivery are being prepared and will be announced officially, without dates or fees
- [ ] Say delivery takes a few days
- [ ] Create an order for them manually
> Planned features are never presented as live.

Q: Which ticket status should you use after contacting the seller about a no-show and waiting for their answer?
- [x] Waiting for Seller
- [ ] Resolved
- [ ] Closed
- [ ] New

Q: What must be recorded before you mark a ticket Resolved?
- [ ] Only the customer's satisfaction rating
- [x] Root cause, actions, resolution and that the customer was notified

> All four are required.

- [x] Explain it is being processed, check for payout-detail changes, escalate to Payments & Finance and avoid any timeline

- [x] The ticket's communication log

Q: Which escalation level handles a legal threat or a policy exception?
