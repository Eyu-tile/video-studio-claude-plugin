## Summary
Explains what buyers can find and do in their Byfluence dashboard, so agents can point customers to the right place quickly.

## Overview
According to the confirmed business overview, the buyer dashboard includes:

| Section | What the buyer can do there |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | View and update personal information (see *Updating profile information*). |
| **Settings** | Account settings and preferences (see *Account settings and preferences*). |
| **Consultation bookings** | See upcoming sessions and use booking-management actions (e.g., view details, meeting information; other actions depend on policy). |
| **Purchase history** | See past purchases/bookings. |
| **Order information** | Details of individual orders (for physical products this is a planned feature). |
| **Transaction history** | Payments and their status (Pending, Successful, Failed, Canceled, Refund Pending, Refunded, Disputed). |
| **Support options** | Ways to contact Byfluence Support about an account or transaction. |

Exact menu names, layout and which booking actions are available (reschedule, cancel) are **not confirmed** — check the live dashboard before giving click-by-click directions.

## Customer Steps
1. Log in at https://byfluence.com.
2. Open your account/dashboard (usually from your name or profile icon).
3. Choose the section you need: profile, settings, bookings, purchase history, transactions, or support.

## Summary
The buyer's name or other personal information on their account is wrong (misspelled, wrong person, Amharic/English mismatch) and they cannot fix it themselves.

## Issue Description
Common examples: name typed wrongly at sign-up, a relative's name used when registering, name in Amharic script when the customer wants English (or the reverse), or the name was changed by someone else. Names may appear to sellers on bookings, so customers want it right. Changes to identity data must be verified because they can be used in fraud.

## Possible Causes
- Typo at registration.
- Account registered by a relative/assistant with their own details.
- Script preference (Ethiopic vs Latin) or name order (given name, father's name, grandfather's name).
- Legal name change.
- Unauthorized change (compromise).

## Information to Collect
- What is wrong and the correct value
- Reason for the correction
- Identity check items (registered email/phone + recent booking/transaction details)
- Whether the customer can edit the field themselves (screenshot if it's locked)

> [!WARNING] Never ask for passwords or codes. Do not request ID documents unless the official procedure allows it and a secure upload method exists.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check whether the field is editable in the profile; if yes → guide them (*Updating profile information*).
2. If it is locked, verify identity (interim check: registered email/phone + recent booking/transaction details).
3. Check for risk signals: was the name recently changed by someone else? Other recent changes? → *Unauthorized access* if so.
4. If the account was registered with **another person's** details entirely, this may be an ownership question — escalate to **Senior Support**.
5. Escalate verified corrections to **Senior Support** (authorized staff make the change).
6. Record the old and new value and the evidence.

- Purchase made from another (duplicate) account, or while logged out/as a guest (if possible).

> [!WARNING] Never ask for PINs, passwords, codes or full card numbers. A transaction reference is enough.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Ask the customer to check all tabs/filters (upcoming, past, cancelled) and the transaction history.

## Summary
The buyer asks who can see their personal information (name, email, phone, bookings), how it's used, or wants it removed. The privacy policy is not yet confirmed, so agents answer carefully and route specific requests.

## Issue Description
Buyers worry that sellers or other users can see their phone number or email, that their data is shared or sold, or that a seller contacted them outside the platform. Some want their data deleted. Privacy complaints can involve legal obligations, so accuracy matters.

## Possible Causes
- General concern before booking.
- A seller contacted the buyer directly (phone/Telegram) outside Byfluence.
- Unwanted marketing messages.
- Suspicion of a data leak or phishing using their details.
- Request to access or delete their data.

## Information to Collect
- What specifically worries the customer (which data, who saw it, how they know)
- Any evidence (screenshots of off-platform contact, message source)
- Seller/consultation involved, if any
- For data access/deletion: identity check items

> [!WARNING] Never ask for passwords or codes. Do not share any other user's personal data while investigating.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Listen and identify the specific concern.
2. Explain only confirmed facts: Byfluence staff access to account data is restricted by role; staff never ask for passwords or codes. Do **not** state what sellers can see, data-sharing practices or retention periods until the privacy policy is approved.
3. **Seller contacted the buyer off-platform** → collect evidence; escalate to **Trust & Safety** (and **Seller Operations**); advise the buyer to keep payments on Byfluence.
4. **Suspected leak or phishing** → *Phishing messages or fake Byfluence contacts*; **Trust & Safety**.
5. **Data access or deletion request** → verify identity and follow tns-data-deletion-request; account closure → *Account deletion / closure request*.
6. **Unwanted messages** → *Changing notification preferences*.
7. Record the concern clearly; privacy complaints are tracked for Management.
