## Summary
A customer wants to know how to create a Byfluence account. Every buyer and every future seller starts with a regular Byfluence account created on byfluence.com.

## Overview
Byfluence is an Ethiopian online marketplace (https://byfluence.com). Creating an account is the first step for everyone:
- **Buyers** need an account to book consultations, pay, see their booking and purchase history, and contact support about their transactions.
- **Sellers** also start with a regular account and then apply to become a seller from it (application → personal/business information → identity verification → review). See *Buyer or seller account: which one to create*.

The exact registration fields and the verification method (email link, SMS code, or both) depend on the live sign-up system and are **not yet confirmed in this knowledge base**. Before guiding a customer, make sure you know what the live form currently asks for (see Internal Notes).

What the customer generally needs:
- A device with internet access and a web browser (phone or computer).
- An email address and/or phone number they can access **right now**, because a verification message may be sent to it.
- A password they will remember and will not share.

## Customer Steps
1. Open **https://byfluence.com** in your phone or computer browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, etc.).
2. Select the sign-up / register option on the page.
3. Fill in the details the form asks for (for example, your name, email address and/or phone number, and a password). Check the spelling of your email and phone number carefully before continuing.
4. Read and accept the terms if the form asks you to.
5. Submit the form.
6. If Byfluence sends you a verification link or code, open it or enter it on the page. Use only the most recent code you received.
7. Log in with the details you registered with.

> [!TIP] Customers with low digital literacy: stay on the line or in the chat and go one step at a time. Ask them to tell you what they see on the screen instead of describing it yourself. Never ask them to read a code or password to you.

## Summary
A step-by-step guide an agent can use to walk a new buyer — including first-time internet users — through registration and their first steps after signing up.

## Overview
Most new buyers register because they want to book a consultation (one-on-one or group). A good walkthrough gets them registered, verified, and able to find their dashboard, so their first booking goes smoothly.

The walkthrough has three stages:
1. **Before starting** — make sure the customer has the email/phone they will register with available on the same device or nearby.
2. **Registration** — the sign-up form and verification (see *How to Create a Byfluence Account*).
3. **After registration** — log in, check the profile, find the dashboard (bookings, purchase history, transaction history, settings, support options).

### Agent script for a guided walkthrough (phone or Telegram)
1. Ask: "Are you using a phone or a computer?" and "Do you have access to your email / SIM card right now?"
2. Ask them to open byfluence.com and tell you what they see.
3. Guide them to the sign-up / register option.
4. Ask them to fill in each field and read back **only** the email address or phone number spelling (never the password).
5. After submitting: "Did you receive an email or SMS from Byfluence?" — if yes, ask them to open the link or enter the code themselves. If no, follow *OTP / verification code not received* or *Email verification link not received or expired*.
6. Ask them to log in and confirm they can see their dashboard.
7. Point out where upcoming bookings and purchase history will appear (see *The buyer dashboard explained*).

## Customer Steps
1. Make sure you can open your email inbox or receive SMS on the phone number you will use.
2. Go to **https://byfluence.com** and choose the sign-up / register option.
3. Fill in the form and check the spelling of your email and phone number.
4. Create a password that you have not used on other websites, and keep it private.
5. Complete the verification step if a link or code is sent to you.
6. Log in and open your dashboard. From there you can update your profile, see your bookings and purchase history, and find support options.
7. When you are ready, browse consultations, choose a time, and pay to confirm your booking.

## Summary
Customers often ask whether to create a "buyer" or "seller" account. Everyone starts with a regular Byfluence account; selling is unlocked by applying to become a seller from that account and passing review.

## Overview
**Confirmed seller onboarding flow (from the business overview):**
1. Create a Byfluence account.
2. Apply to become a seller.
3. Provide personal and/or business information.
4. Submit identity verification.
5. Wait for review.
6. Application approved or rejected.
7. Complete the seller profile.
8. Create listings.
9. Start selling once permissions are granted.

What this means for the customer:
- **Want to buy, book or attend consultations?** Create a regular account. Nothing else is needed.
- **Want to sell (offer consultations or, later, products)?** Create a regular account first, then apply to become a seller from it. Do not look for a separate "seller sign-up" unless the live site shows one.
- **Not sure yet?** Create a regular account. The seller application can be started later.

Seller status concepts the customer may hear about:
| Type | Statuses | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Verification | Pending, Verified, Rejected | Result of the identity/application review |
| Operational | Active, Suspended, Banned | Whether the seller can currently operate |

These are separate: a seller can be **Verified** but **Suspended**.

## Customer Steps
1. Create a Byfluence account at https://byfluence.com (or log in if you already have one).
2. If you want to sell, find the option to apply to become a seller in your account.
3. Fill in the requested personal/business information and submit your identity verification.
4. Wait for the review result. You will not be able to sell until your application is approved and permissions are granted.

## Summary
A customer wants to create or manage an account for someone else (a family member, an employee, a client) or for a business. Rules for this are not confirmed, so agents give safe guidance and escalate decisions.

## Issue Description
Common situations:
- A son or daughter registering an elderly parent who wants to book a consultation.
- An assistant or agency creating a seller account for a consultant.
- A business owner wanting a "company" seller account managed by staff.
- One person wanting to pay for a consultation that someone else will attend.
- A third party contacting support about someone else's account.

Risks: the real account holder may lose control of the account (codes and resets go to the helper's email/phone), identity verification for sellers may not match the person actually selling, and support may disclose personal data to someone who is not the account holder.

## Possible Causes
- The intended user has low digital literacy or no email address.
- The business wants a single account for several people.
- The customer does not know they can simply help the person register with that person's own details.
- In rare cases, an attempt to get around seller verification or a ban (see *Suspicious or bulk registration attempts*).

## Information to Collect
- Who will actually use the account (name, relationship to the person contacting us)
- Whether the account is for buying or selling
- Whose email/phone will be used for registration
- For businesses: business name and who is the authorized representative
- Whether the account already exists (registered email/phone)

> [!WARNING] Never ask for or accept the other person's password or verification code, even if the helper offers it.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Find out whether the account already exists. If it does, **only discuss account details with the account holder** (or follow an approved authorization process once one exists).
2. For a **buyer helped by a relative**: recommend registering with the account holder's own email/phone where possible. The helper may guide them, but the account holder should receive codes and set the password.
3. If the account holder has no email/phone of their own, do not refuse — record this in the ticket and escalate to **Senior Support** for guidance (policy pending).
4. For a **seller account created by an agency/assistant**: explain that the seller application and identity verification must reflect the real seller. Route business-account questions to **Seller Operations**.
5. For **paying for someone else's consultation**: whether a booking can be made for another attendee is not confirmed — escalate the question rather than advising a workaround.
6. If anything suggests the helper is trying to control someone else's account without consent, or to bypass verification or a ban → **Trust & Safety**.
7. Record who contacted us, their relationship to the account holder, and what was advised.

- [ ] Real account user identified
- [ ] No personal data disclosed to a third party
- [ ] Business/seller questions routed to Seller Operations
- [ ] Ticket notes complete

## Summary
The customer registered but the email verification link never arrived, or the link they clicked says it is expired, invalid or already used.

## Issue Description
After signing up, the customer is asked to verify their email address but either cannot find the email, or opening the link shows an error ("expired", "invalid", "already used", a blank page). They cannot finish registration or log in, and may try registering again, which can create duplicate accounts or "email already registered" errors.

> [!NOTE] Whether Byfluence verifies accounts by email link is **not yet confirmed**. Confirm the live behaviour before using this article; if verification is by SMS code instead, use *OTP / verification code not received*.

## Possible Causes
- The email went to the Spam, Junk, Promotions or Updates folder.
- The email address was mistyped at registration (e.g., `gmial.com`, missing letter).
- Email delivery is delayed by the email provider.
- The customer requested several emails; only the newest link works.
- The link expired before it was opened.
- The link was already used — the account is already verified.
- The link opened inside an app's built-in browser (Gmail/Telegram in-app browser) that broke the page.
- Workplace or school email filters blocked the message.
- Byfluence email sending is failing for everyone (incident).

## Information to Collect
- Registered email address (ask the customer to type it — check the spelling)
- Approximate date and time of registration
- Whether they requested a new link, and how many times
- Device and browser/app used to open the link
- Screenshot of the error page, if any (no codes or passwords visible)

> [!WARNING] Never ask for the customer's password or the content of any code. You do not need them to check verification status.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Search the admin dashboard for the email the customer gives you. Also try likely typos (e.g., `gmail.con`, `gmial.com`) and the customer's phone number.
2. Decide the case:
   - **Account not found** → registration did not complete or used a different email. Ask the customer to register again carefully (see *Registration form error*).
   - **Account found with a typo in the email** → the email cannot reach them. Do not edit it yourself. Escalate to **Senior Support** to decide whether to correct it or whether the customer should re-register with the correct email (policy pending).
   - **Account found, already verified** → the link was already used. Ask the customer to log in normally.
   - **Account found, not verified** → continue to step 3.
3. Ask the customer to check Spam/Junk/Promotions and search their inbox for "Byfluence".
4. Ask them to request a new verification email from the site (if the option exists) and use **only the newest** email.
5. If the link breaks inside an app, ask them to copy it and open it in a regular browser (Chrome, Safari).
6. If nothing arrives after these steps, check *Email notifications not received* (tec-email-notifications-not-received) and Announcements for an email incident.
7. If the customer still cannot verify, escalate to **Technical / Engineering** with the email, timestamps and screenshots.

- [ ] Account located (or confirmed missing)
- [ ] Email spelling checked
- [ ] Spam/Promotions checked
- [ ] Fresh link requested and opened in a normal browser

## Summary
The customer cannot verify their phone number during registration: the number is rejected, flagged as already used, or the verification step fails.

## Issue Description
This article covers problems with the **phone number itself** (format, acceptance, ownership). If the number is accepted but the SMS code never arrives, use *OTP / verification code not received*; if the code arrives but is rejected, use *Verification code rejected as invalid or expired*.

> [!NOTE] Whether phone/SMS verification is used at registration is **not yet confirmed**. Check the live sign-up flow first.

## Possible Causes
- Wrong format: the form may expect `+251 9…`, `09…` or `9…` — the customer used another format or added spaces/dashes.
- A digit is missing or extra.
- The number is a non-Ethiopian (foreign) number and may not be supported.
- The number is already registered to another account — the customer's own older account, a relative's account, or a previous owner of a **recycled** number.
- The SIM is not active, has no network, or is in a second SIM slot the customer isn't checking.
- The customer is typing Ethiopic or Arabic-Indic digits from their keyboard instead of 0–9.

## Information to Collect
- The phone number the customer is trying to use (and which country code)
- Mobile operator (e.g., Ethio telecom, Safaricom Ethiopia)
- Exact error message or a screenshot
- Whether they have registered before, with this or another number/email

> [!WARNING] Never ask for the verification code itself. If the customer tries to send it to you, tell them to delete the message and never share codes.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Ask for the exact error text or a screenshot.
2. If it's a **format error**, ask the customer to retype the number using the format the form shows, with no spaces or dashes, using normal digits (0–9).
3. If the error says **already registered**, search the admin dashboard for the number:
   - Linked to an account the customer can identify as theirs → guide them to log in or reset their password.
   - Linked to an account they don't recognise (recycled number, relative) → do not share any detail of that account; follow *"Email or phone already registered" error*.
4. If it's a **foreign number**, follow *Unsupported registration methods*.
5. If the number is accepted but no code arrives, switch to *OTP / verification code not received*.
6. If the form rejects a correctly formatted Ethiopian number for several customers, escalate to **Technical / Engineering**.

## Summary
The customer is waiting for a one-time verification code (OTP) by SMS or email to register, log in or confirm an action, and it does not arrive.

## Issue Description
The customer is stuck on a screen asking for a verification code. They may have pressed "resend" several times. They are often frustrated and may offer to send you screenshots of their messages — which could expose codes. This blocks registration or login completely, so respond quickly.

## Possible Causes
- Phone number or email entered incorrectly.
- Mobile network delay or no coverage; SIM in the second slot of a dual-SIM phone.
- Phone SMS inbox is full, or messages from short codes/unknown senders are blocked or filtered as spam.
- Email code went to Spam/Promotions.
- Too many resend requests — the system may temporarily stop sending (rate limit).
- The customer is looking for the code on a different phone/email than the one entered.
- SMS or email sending service outage on the Byfluence side or the operator side.
- The number is a foreign number that may not be supported.

## Information to Collect
- The phone number or email the code should go to (ask the customer to type it)
- Whether it is SMS or email
- Mobile operator, and whether the phone has signal
- Roughly how many times they pressed "resend"
- What they were doing (registering, logging in, changing details)

> [!WARNING] Never ask the customer to read, forward or screenshot the code. If they send one by mistake, do not use it; tell them to never share codes, and note it in the ticket.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the destination: ask the customer to type the phone/email exactly as they entered it. Check it against the account in the admin dashboard if an account exists.
2. **SMS codes:**
   - Check the phone has network; try turning airplane mode on and off.
   - Check the correct SIM slot and that the SMS inbox is not full.
   - Check blocked/spam messages in the SMS app.
   - Wait a few minutes before pressing "resend" again — repeated requests can delay or block sending.
3. **Email codes:** check Spam/Junk/Promotions and search for "Byfluence".
4. Ask the customer to request **one** new code and use only the newest code.
5. If the number/email was wrong, they need to go back and enter it correctly (or re-register).
6. Check Announcements for a known SMS/email incident. Several reports at once → escalate as an incident.
7. If still not received, follow tec-sms-not-received / tec-email-notifications-not-received and escalate to **Technical / Engineering** with the destination, operator, time of each request and a screenshot of the screen (not the messages).

- [ ] Destination confirmed correct
- [ ] Network / spam / inbox checks done
- [ ] One fresh code requested
- [ ] Incident check done

## Summary
The customer receives a verification code but the site says it is invalid, incorrect or expired.

## Issue Description
The customer enters the code they received and gets an error. They are usually sure they typed it correctly. Repeated failures may temporarily block further attempts or lock the account.

## Possible Causes
- They entered an **older** code after requesting a new one (only the newest code works).
- The code expired before they entered it.
- A typing mistake, an extra space, or the phone's autofill pasted a different code.
- The keyboard entered Ethiopic numerals instead of 0–9.
- The code was for a different action or account (e.g., a code from another service, or another Byfluence account).
- Too many wrong attempts triggered a temporary block.
- A system fault (several customers affected).

## Information to Collect
- What they were doing (registering, logging in, changing details)
- Whether it is an SMS or email code
- How many codes they requested and which one they used
- The exact error message or a screenshot of the screen (with the code field empty)

> [!WARNING] Do not ask the customer to tell you the code, and do not try to validate it for them.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Ask the customer to request **one** fresh code and wait for it.
2. Ask them to delete or ignore all older codes and type the newest code **manually**, using English digits, without spaces.
3. Enter it promptly after receiving it.
4. If the error says too many attempts / temporarily blocked, ask them to wait before trying again (do not quote a time unless approved), then follow *Locked account* if the block persists.
5. If the newest code entered correctly still fails, ask for a screenshot of the error and the time, and escalate to **Technical / Engineering**.
6. If the customer mentions receiving codes they did not request, treat as a security concern → *Unauthorized access*.
