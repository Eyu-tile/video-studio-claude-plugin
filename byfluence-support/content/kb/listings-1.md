## Summary
A verified seller asks how to create a listing for a service or product on Byfluence.

## Overview
A listing is what buyers see and buy. At MVP stage the confirmed listing types are **one-on-one consultations** and **group consultations** (see `lst-create-consultation-listing` and `grp-seller-create-group-session`). **Physical products** and **digital products** are planned expansion areas.

> [!WARNING] Before guiding a seller to list a physical or digital product, check the **Feature Status** page. If the feature is not live, do not tell the seller they can sell it now and do not give a launch date. For digital products when live, see `dig-seller-upload-guide`; for physical orders, see `phy-seller-order-management`.

### Before creating a listing
- Seller verification status is **Verified** and operational status is **Active**.
- Seller profile is complete.
- The item/service is allowed (see `lst-prohibited-items`).

### What a good listing contains
| Field | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Title | Clear and specific: "Small Business Bookkeeping — 1:1 Consultation (60 min)" not "Best service!!!" |
| Description | What the buyer gets, who it is for, what is **not** included, any preparation needed |
| Category / topic | Choose the closest category so buyers can find it |
| Price | In ETB; the full price the buyer pays for the listing (see `lst-pricing-questions`) |
| Duration / details | For consultations: session length; for products: size, condition, quantity (when live) |
| Images | Real, clear images the seller has the right to use |
| Availability / stock | For consultations: available time slots; for products: stock (when live) |

Listings may be reviewed before going live (see `lst-listing-under-review`).

## Customer Steps
1. Sign in to your seller account and open the dashboard.
2. Choose the option to create a new listing (confirm the current label on the live site).
3. Select the listing type available to you.
4. Fill in title, description, category, price and details.
5. Upload clear images.
6. Set availability (consultations) or stock (products, when live).
7. Review and submit/publish; check the listing status afterwards.

## Summary
A seller asks how to create a one-on-one or group consultation listing and what information to include.

## Overview
Consultations are the core MVP offering. Buyers evaluate a consultation listing by **consultant name, description, topic, duration, price and availability**, then schedule a date/time, pay, and receive Google Meet details once the booking is confirmed after successful payment.

### One-on-one vs. group
| | One-on-one | Group |
|---|---|---|
| Buyers per session | One | Many, up to the capacity the seller sets |
| Seller sets | Topic, description, duration, price, availability slots | Topic, description, date, duration, price, **capacity** |
| Meeting | Google Meet associated with the booking | Confirmed participants get meeting access |
| Rules still pending | Reschedule/no-show rules | Capacity, full sessions, cancellation, rescheduling, refunds, missed sessions, seller no-shows |

For group sessions, also see `grp-seller-create-group-session`. For availability setup, see `con-seller-availability-setup` and `lst-availability-calendar`.

### Writing a strong consultation listing
- **Topic and outcome**: what problem the session helps with, what the buyer will leave with.
- **Who it's for**: beginners, small business owners, students, etc.
- **Format**: duration, language (Amharic/English), what the buyer should prepare.
- **Not included**: e.g., "This is advice only; I do not file documents for you."
- **Qualifications**: honest experience; no unverifiable claims.
- **Time zone**: be clear that times are shown in the platform's time zone (confirm how the platform displays times).

> [!NOTE] Whether Google Meet links are generated automatically or provided by the seller is **not confirmed** — check Feature Status / internal notes before explaining. A scheduled meeting does not prove the session was delivered.

## Customer Steps
1. From your seller dashboard, create a new listing and choose consultation (1:1 or group, as offered).
2. Write the title, topic and description.
3. Set the session duration and price.
4. For group sessions, set date, time and capacity.
5. Set your availability (1:1) or session schedule (group).
6. Submit and check the listing status.

## Summary
A seller wants to edit, pause, hide or delete a listing — or asks what happens to existing bookings when they do.

## Overview
Sellers should be able to update listings from their dashboard. The main risk is **existing buyers**: changing the price, duration or content of a listing — or deleting it — must not change what an existing buyer already paid for and booked.

### What to tell sellers
- **Small edits** (typos, clearer description, images) — normally fine; may trigger re-review.
- **Price changes** — apply to new bookings; buyers who already paid keep their booking at the paid price (see `lst-pricing-questions`).
- **Duration/content changes** — must not reduce what booked buyers receive.
- **Pause/hide** — stops new bookings; existing bookings must still be delivered.
- **Delete** — only when there are no upcoming bookings/open orders; otherwise deliver or properly handle them first.

Whether edits are reviewed again and whether listings can be paused vs. deleted are **not confirmed** — check the live dashboard.

## Customer Steps
1. Open your seller dashboard and go to your listings.
2. Select the listing and choose edit (or pause/remove, if available).
3. Make your changes and save.
4. Check the listing status (it may go back to review).
5. Before pausing or removing, check your upcoming bookings — you still need to deliver them.

## Escalation Conditions
- Seller disputes rejection → **Seller Operations**, priority Medium.
- No reason recorded → **Seller Operations**.
- Counterfeit, fraud, illegal content → **Trust & Safety**.
- Repeated rejections for the same seller with prohibited items → **Seller Operations** (possible account review).

## Resolution Procedure
1. Listing resubmitted and approved, or final decision communicated.
2. Record reason category, changes advised, escalation and outcome.

## Policy Pending Approval
- Approve standard listing rejection reasons and wording.
- Decide whether repeated rejections affect seller standing.
- Approve the prohibited/restricted list (see `lst-prohibited-items`).

## Internal Notes
- The most common fixable reason is contact details in the description or images. Explain why: keeping contact on-platform protects buyers and sellers.
- Don't coach sellers on how to "get around" a prohibited-item rejection.
