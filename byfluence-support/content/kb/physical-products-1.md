## Summary
A buyer wants to know how to find physical products on Byfluence, or cannot find a specific product, category or seller through search.

## Overview
> [!WARNING] **Planned feature.** The physical product marketplace is an expansion area and is not confirmed as live. Check the **Feature Status** page before telling a customer they can browse or buy physical products. If it is not live, tell the customer it is not yet available and log their interest as feedback — do not walk them through steps that do not exist yet.

When the physical product marketplace is live, buyers discover products listed by many independent sellers (clothing, electronics, accessories, beauty, home and lifestyle items). Byfluence is the marketplace; each seller is responsible for their own listing, photos, description, price and stock.

Typical reasons a buyer "cannot find" something:
- They are searching with a spelling the seller did not use (Amharic vs English, brand abbreviations, local names for items).
- The listing was removed, hidden by the seller, sold out, or is under listing review.
- The product is in a different category than the buyer expects (e.g., phone cases under "Accessories" instead of "Electronics").
- They saw the product on a seller's social media page, not on Byfluence.
- A temporary technical problem with search or page loading.

Agents do **not** recommend specific sellers or products, and do not comment on whether one seller is better than another.

## Customer Steps
1. Open https://byfluence.com (or the Byfluence app, if one is available) and go to the products section.
2. Browse by category, or type the product name into the search bar.
3. If nothing comes up, try:
   - a shorter or more general word (e.g., "shoes" instead of the full model name);
   - the English and the Amharic name of the item;
   - the brand name alone, or the seller's shop name if you know it.
4. Use any filters shown on the page (category, price, etc.) — if no results appear, clear the filters and search again.
5. Open the product page to see photos, description, price, seller name and availability.
6. If the page will not load, refresh, check your internet connection, or try another browser.

## Summary
A buyer asks whether a product is in stock or when it will be available again, or a seller marked an item available that they cannot actually supply.

## Issue Description
> [!WARNING] **Planned feature.** Check the **Feature Status** page before confirming to a customer that physical product ordering is available.

Questions fall into three groups:
1. **Before ordering** — "Is this in stock?", "When will size 42 be back?", "Can I reserve one?"
2. **After ordering** — the buyer paid, then the seller says the item is out of stock (stock mismatch). This is more serious because the buyer's money is involved.
3. **Seller side** — a seller asks how to mark an item out of stock, or keeps receiving orders for items they no longer have.

Stock is managed by the seller. Byfluence does not hold stock for regular sellers and support cannot see a seller's warehouse — only what the listing shows.

## Possible Causes
- The seller sold the last item in their physical shop or on another channel and did not update the listing.
- The seller set a quantity incorrectly or never set a quantity.
- A size/colour variant is sold out while the main listing still shows as available.
- The listing is paused, under review, or was removed.
- Two buyers ordered the last unit at nearly the same time.

## Information to Collect
- Product name and listing link or screenshot
- Variant needed (size, colour, model)
- If already ordered: order ID ({{order_id}}), payment date and payment method
- For sellers: listing name and which quantities are wrong

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Open the listing in the admin dashboard (or on the public site) and check its status and any stock/quantity shown.
2. **Before ordering:**
   - If the listing shows out of stock → tell the buyer it is currently unavailable; restock dates are decided by the seller.
   - If it shows available but the buyer is unsure → suggest they ask the seller through the order/contact option before paying (see *phy-contact-seller*).
3. **Already paid and the seller says it is out of stock:**
   - Check the order status. If the seller has cancelled, follow *phy-order-cancellation-seller*.
   - If the seller has not cancelled but told the buyer informally, ask the seller to update the order officially; do not let it sit in "processing".
   - Never suggest the buyer accept a substitute item unless the buyer clearly agrees and the change is recorded on the ticket.
4. **Seller asking for help:** explain how to update quantity or pause the listing (see *lst-create-listing*). If they cannot edit it, escalate to Seller Operations.
5. Record the listing ID and, if relevant, order ID in the ticket.

## Escalation Conditions
- Buyer has paid, the seller confirms no stock, and the order has not been cancelled → **Senior Support / Supervisor** to push the seller to cancel officially so the refund process can start.
- The same seller repeatedly accepts orders for items they do not have → **Seller Operations** (listing accuracy / seller performance).
- A seller offers the buyer a "replacement" only if they pay extra outside Byfluence → **Trust & Safety**.
- The listing shows available but cannot be ordered (button error) → **Technical / Engineering**.

## Resolution Procedure
1. Before ordering: confirm the buyer understands availability is set by the seller, then resolve.
2. After payment: keep the ticket open until the order is either fulfilled or officially cancelled, and any refund case is handed to Payments (see *phy-refund-request-product*).
3. Record listing ID, order ID, seller's explanation and outcome.

- Watch for sellers who list popular items cheaply, collect orders, then claim "out of stock" and redirect buyers to private chats: possible fraud pattern → Trust & Safety.

## Summary
A buyer asks support about a product's size, specifications, material, compatibility, originality or warranty before or after ordering.

## Overview
> [!WARNING] **Planned feature.** Check the **Feature Status** page before confirming that physical product ordering is available.

The **seller** is responsible for the accuracy of the listing: photos, description, sizes, specifications, brand and condition. Support agents do not have the physical product and must **never guess** details such as size fit, battery life, compatibility, whether an item is original, or what warranty applies.

What support can do:
- Point the buyer to the information already in the listing (description, photos, size information).
- Explain how to ask the seller directly before buying (see *phy-contact-seller*).
- Take a report if the listing appears misleading or wrong.

Common question types and what to say:
| Question | Agent approach |
|---|---|
| "Will size M fit me?" | Ask the buyer to check the size information on the listing and ask the seller for measurements (e.g., chest/length in cm). Sizes differ between brands and between EU/US/local sizing. |
| "Is this phone original?" | Support cannot verify. Ask the seller in writing before buying. If it arrives and appears counterfeit, use *phy-counterfeit-misleading*. |
| "Does it have warranty?" | Warranty is offered (or not) by the seller/manufacturer. Byfluence warranty rules are not confirmed — do not promise any. |
| "Is it new or used?" | Must be stated by the seller in the listing; if not clear, ask the seller. |

## Customer Steps
1. Open the product page and read the full description, and zoom into all photos.
2. Check any size chart or specification section on the listing.
3. If something is not clear, ask the seller before you pay (use the contact or question option on the listing or order page, if available).
4. Take a screenshot of the listing and of the seller's answer — it is useful if there is a problem later.

## Summary
A buyer wants to know whether their order went through and what confirmation they should have received, or says they did not receive one.

## Overview
> [!WARNING] **Planned feature.** Check the **Feature Status** page before confirming that physical product ordering is available.

Per the confirmed payment flow, an order is confirmed only **after Byfluence receives confirmation from the payment provider**. Until then it may appear as pending.

After a successful order the buyer should be able to see:
- the order in their dashboard (order information / purchase history) with an **order ID**;
- the product, quantity, total paid and delivery details they entered;
- an order status (e.g., confirmed or processing — exact status names depend on the live system);
- a payment record in transaction history.

Email/SMS notifications are **not confirmed** for every event — check the Feature Status page for `notifications-email` and `notifications-sms` before telling a customer they "should have received an email/SMS". The dashboard is the reliable place to check.

## Issue Description
The buyer is unsure whether the order was placed: they did not see a confirmation screen, did not get a message, or cannot find the order. The real risk is a buyer paying twice "to be safe".

## Possible Causes
- Payment is still pending with the provider, so the order is not yet confirmed.
- Payment failed, and no order was confirmed.
- The buyer closed the browser before returning to Byfluence.
- The notification was not sent, went to spam, or the buyer used a different email/phone.
- The buyer is logged into a different account than the one used to order.

## Information to Collect
- Registered email or phone number
- Product name and seller name
- Date and time of the order/payment, payment method
- Transaction reference from the payment receipt/SMS (if they paid)
- Screenshot of what they see in their dashboard

> [!WARNING] Never ask for passwords, OTP codes, PINs or full card numbers.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Verify the customer's identity by registered email or phone.
2. Search orders on the account, including pending and cancelled ones, around the stated time.
3. Decide:
   - **Order found and confirmed** → give the order ID and explain where to see it in the dashboard.
   - **Order found but pending payment** → check the transaction; if the provider shows success, follow *pay-paid-order-not-confirmed*.
   - **No order, but payment taken** → follow *pay-paid-order-not-confirmed* (Payments).
   - **No order and no payment** → the order was not placed; the buyer can try again.
   - **Two orders/payments** → follow *pay-duplicate-charge*.
4. Tell the buyer **not to pay again** until you have checked.
5. Record the findings and references in the ticket.

## Summary
A seller asks how to receive, prepare, pack and hand over physical product orders, and how to keep order status up to date.

## Overview
> [!WARNING] **Planned feature.** Physical product selling and Addis Ababa delivery are planned. Check the **Feature Status** page before explaining order management to a seller as a live feature.

Confirmed flow: buyer orders and pays → seller prepares → fulfillment → order completed → seller settlement. The seller is responsible for the product and its accuracy. Who delivers (seller, Byfluence-arranged partner, or another party) is **not confirmed**, and liability for loss or damage in transit is **policy pending**. For inventory-free (reseller) orders, see *inv-fulfillment-responsibility*.

Seller earnings from an order are recorded as **pending** and become available only when release conditions are met (completion, no open dispute, etc.). A paid order does not mean immediately withdrawable money.

## Customer Steps
For the seller:
1. Check your seller dashboard regularly for new orders.
2. Open each new order and confirm the product, options, quantity and the buyer's delivery details.
3. If you cannot fulfil the order (no stock, wrong price), contact support or cancel through the official option **immediately** — do not leave the order waiting.
4. Prepare the item and check it for damage or defects before packing.
5. **Take packing evidence:** photos (or a short video) of the item, the item inside the package, the sealed package and the label with the order ID.
6. Pack securely, especially fragile items and electronics.
7. Hand the package over according to the delivery arrangement shown for the order, and update the order status if the dashboard lets you.
8. If anything delays the order, inform the buyer through Byfluence and update the status.
9. Never ask the buyer to pay extra or pay outside Byfluence.

> [!TIP] Packing photos with the order ID visible are the seller's best protection in missing-item, wrong-item and damage disputes.
