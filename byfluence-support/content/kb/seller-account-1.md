## Summary
A person asks how they can start selling on Byfluence. Explain the onboarding path: account → seller application → identity verification → review → profile → listings → selling once permissions are granted.

## Overview
Byfluence is a marketplace that lets people start an online business without building their own website, payment or booking system. Anyone who wants to sell must go through the seller onboarding path. The confirmed stages are:

| Stage | What happens | Who acts |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Create an account | Register a normal Byfluence account | Customer |
| 2. Apply to become a seller | Start the seller application from their account | Customer |
| 3. Provide personal / business information | Fill in the application details | Customer |
| 4. Submit identity verification | Upload the requested identity information through the official platform flow | Customer |
| 5. Wait for review | Seller Operations reviews the application | Seller Operations |
| 6. Approved or rejected | Verification status becomes **Verified** or **Rejected** | Seller Operations |
| 7. Complete profile | Build the public seller profile | Customer |
| 8. Create listings | Add consultation (and later other) listings | Customer |
| 9. Sell | Accept bookings/orders once selling permissions are granted | Customer |

What sellers can offer **today** (MVP): one-on-one consultations and group consultations, with Google Meet for the session. Physical products, inventory-free selling and digital products are planned expansion areas — check the **Feature Status** page before telling anyone they can sell these.

> [!NOTE] Support agents explain the process and help with problems. Agents never approve, reject or speed up a seller application — only Seller Operations decides.

## Customer Steps
1. Create a Byfluence account (or sign in to an existing one) at byfluence.com.
2. Open the seller application option in the account (confirm the current button/menu name on the live site before guiding the customer).
3. Fill in the requested personal or business information accurately — it should match the identity documents they will submit.
4. Submit identity verification **inside the platform** (never by Telegram, email or chat).
5. Wait for the review result; check the seller dashboard or notifications for the status.
6. Once Verified, complete the public seller profile and create the first listing.

## Summary
A seller applicant needs detailed help completing the seller application form and submitting it correctly the first time.

## Overview
Most rejections and delays come from avoidable mistakes in the application: names that don't match the ID, blurry uploads, incomplete business information or a submission that never actually finished. Guide the applicant through each stage and make sure the application reaches **Pending** (submitted, waiting for review).

The exact fields and verification methods depend on the live system and are **not confirmed**. Always check the current form in a test/admin view (if you have access) before giving field-by-field instructions.

### Common stages of the application
1. **Account check** — the applicant must be signed in to their own Byfluence account (not a friend's or relative's account).
2. **Seller type** — individual or business (if the form asks).
3. **Personal / business information** — full legal name as on ID, contact details, and, for businesses, business name and any details the form requests.
4. **What they plan to sell** — category/topic of consultations or services, if requested.
5. **Identity verification** — upload documents/photos inside the platform.
6. **Review and submit** — check every field, accept the terms, press submit and wait for a confirmation message or status change.

## Customer Steps
1. Sign in to your own Byfluence account on a stable internet connection.
2. Open the seller application.
3. Enter your full name exactly as it appears on your identity document.
4. Fill in every required field; for a business, use the official business name.
5. Upload clear, complete photos or scans of the requested documents (all four corners visible, no glare, not cropped).
6. Review the summary page, accept the terms and press **Submit**.
7. Confirm the status shows the application is submitted / under review. If the page shows an error or nothing changes, take a screenshot and contact support.

## Summary
A person asks whether they are allowed to become a seller (age, business registration, location, profession, foreigners, organizations). Most eligibility rules are not yet confirmed.

## Overview
Byfluence's mission is that **anyone** should be able to start and grow an online business, even without significant starting capital. The confirmed requirements are only:
- The person must have a Byfluence account.
- They must apply to become a seller and complete **identity verification**.
- Seller Operations must approve the application before they can sell.
- Sellers are responsible for what they offer and must follow marketplace rules.

Everything else — minimum age, whether a trade licence is required, whether professional licences are needed for regulated advice (medical, legal, financial), whether people outside Ethiopia can sell, whether organizations can register — is **not confirmed**. Do not tell anyone they are or are not eligible based on assumptions. Explain the general path and say that the final decision is made during review.

> [!WARNING] Never tell an applicant "you will definitely be approved". Approval is decided by Seller Operations after review.

### Typical questions and how to handle them
| Question | What to say |
|---|---|
| "Do I need a business licence?" | Requirements are confirmed during the application; follow what the form asks. Escalate if they need a firm answer before applying. |
| "I'm a student / under 18, can I sell?" | Age requirements are being confirmed; escalate to Seller Operations — do not encourage a minor to apply. |
| "I live outside Ethiopia." | Not confirmed; escalate to Seller Operations. |
| "I'm a doctor/lawyer/financial adviser, can I sell consultations?" | Regulated advice may need extra checks; escalate to Seller Operations. |
| "Can my company register?" | Escalate to Seller Operations for confirmation of business accounts. |

## Customer Steps
1. Read the application form — it lists what is required for your seller type.
2. Prepare the requested identity information before you start.
3. Submit the application; Seller Operations will review it and decide.

## Summary
A seller asks what identity verification is, why it is needed and what the verification statuses mean.

## Overview
Every seller must pass identity verification before they can sell. It protects buyers (they know a real, accountable person is behind each listing), protects honest sellers from impersonation, and supports trust on the marketplace.

### Verification statuses
| Status | Meaning | What the seller can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Pending** | Application submitted and waiting for, or in, review | Wait; make sure contact details are correct |
| **Verified** | Identity approved by Seller Operations | Complete profile and create listings (if operational status allows) |
| **Rejected** | Verification not approved | Read the reason shown in the account; resubmit if allowed |

### Verification status vs. operational status
Verification status (Pending / Verified / Rejected) is **separate** from operational status (**Active / Suspended / Banned**). A seller can be **Verified but Suspended** — verification only proves who they are; it does not guarantee permission to sell. Always check both statuses before answering.

### Who decides
Only **Seller Operations** reviews documents and approves or rejects verification. Support agents:
- explain the process,
- check the status through authorized tools (admin dashboard, if you have access),
- collect non-sensitive details and escalate.

Agents **never** view, request, store or forward ID documents in chat, and never approve, reject or "push" a verification.

## Customer Steps
1. Complete the seller application.
2. Upload the requested identity information on the platform's verification page.
3. Watch the account/dashboard for status changes and any requests for more information.
4. If rejected, read the reason and follow the resubmission steps if offered.

A seller asks which documents are needed for verification and how to prepare them. The official list of accepted documents is not yet confirmed.

## Overview
The **required ID documents are not confirmed** in the business overview. Do not tell sellers that a specific document (kebele ID, national ID / Fayda, passport, driving licence, trade licence, TIN certificate) is accepted or required unless the live verification page or an approved policy says so. Direct them to the list shown on the verification page in their account.

What you *can* safely advise on is **document quality**, because poor photos are the most common reason a submission cannot be reviewed:
- The whole document is visible — all four corners, nothing cropped or covered by fingers.
- Text is sharp and readable; no blur, no glare from a flash or lamp.
- The document is valid (not expired) and is the person's own document.
- Name and details entered in the application match the document exactly.
- Original photo — not a photocopy of a photocopy, not edited, no filters or stickers.
- If both sides are requested, upload both sides.

### Official channel rule
> [!WARNING] Identity documents must only be submitted through the official verification flow on the Byfluence platform. Never request, accept or forward ID photos through Telegram, email, phone or chat. If a seller sends one anyway, follow `sel-id-documents-sent-in-chat`.

4. Upload on the verification page and submit.

## Summary
A seller applied and submitted identity verification, but the status has stayed "Pending" longer than they expected and they want to know why or when it will be decided.

## Issue Description
The applicant has completed (or believes they completed) the seller application and verification. Their account still shows **Pending**, they have received no decision and they are frustrated — often because they have clients waiting, have promoted their Byfluence page or compare themselves to a friend who was approved quickly. The real cause may be a normal queue, a request for more information they missed, an incomplete submission, or a technical failure. The agent's job is to find out which, set expectations honestly, and route correctly — **not** to approve or speed up the review.

## Possible Causes
- Normal review queue; the application is waiting its turn.
- Seller Operations requested more information or a clearer document and the seller missed the notification.
- The submission was never completed (documents not uploaded, final Submit not pressed, session timed out).
- Uploaded files are unreadable or failed to upload, so the application cannot be reviewed.
- Details need a manual check (name mismatch, duplicate account, flagged for risk review).
- Technical issue: the status did not update after a decision was made.
- The applicant is looking at a different account from the one they applied with.

## Information to Collect
- Full name and registered email or phone number of the account used to apply
- Date they submitted the application (approximate is fine)
- What they see on screen (status text) — a screenshot **without** any ID document visible
- Whether they received any email/SMS/notification from Byfluence about the application
- Any business urgency (e.g., clients already waiting) — record it, but it does not guarantee priority

> [!WARNING] Do not ask the seller to re-send ID documents in chat. Never ask for passwords, OTP codes or PINs.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm identity: the contact details given must match the account in the admin dashboard.
2. Check the **verification status** and **operational status** through authorized tools (admin dashboard, if you have access).
3. Decide the case:
   - **Status = Pending, submission complete, no requests** → normal queue. Explain, log the ticket, do not promise a date. If the application is clearly older than the team's normal review time (ask your supervisor what is normal), escalate to Seller Operations with "aged application".
   - **Status shows "more information requested" / resubmission needed** → guide the seller to the resubmission flow (`sel-resubmit-documents`).
   - **Submission incomplete** (no documents attached or not submitted) → explain what is missing and guide them to finish it on the platform.
   - **Status = Verified or Rejected in admin but Pending for the seller** → technical sync issue → **Technical / Engineering**; tell the seller you are checking.
   - **No application found** → check for a second account (other email/phone); if none, guide them to apply again; if they insist they applied, **Technical / Engineering**.
   - **Flagged for risk/manual review** → do not tell the seller about the flag; say it is under review and escalate to Seller Operations only if needed.
4. Add a clear internal note: submission date, current statuses, what you told the seller.
5. If you cannot see verification records with your access level, escalate to **Senior Support / Supervisor**.

## Issue Description
The seller sees **Rejected** as their verification status, or received a rejection message. They may be upset, believe it was a mistake, or ask the agent to "approve it manually". Many rejections are fixable (blurry photo, name mismatch, expired ID) and the seller can resubmit. Agents explain the reason shown to the seller, guide resubmission where allowed, and escalate disputes to Seller Operations. **Agents never reverse a rejection.**

## Possible Causes
- Document photo unreadable, cropped, glare or incomplete.
- Document expired, or not an accepted document type.
- Name or details in the application don't match the document.
- Document belongs to someone else (relative, friend, business partner).
- Required business information missing.
- The planned offering is prohibited or restricted.
- Duplicate account or person previously banned.
- Suspected manipulated or fake document (fraud).

## Information to Collect
- Registered email or phone number of the applicant account
- The rejection message or reason they see (screenshot without ID documents)

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm identity using the registered email/phone.
2. Check verification status, rejection reason code/notes and operational status through authorized tools (admin dashboard, if you have access).
3. Decide the case:

   - **Prohibited/restricted offering** → explain that the planned service/product cannot be listed under marketplace rules (see `lst-prohibited-items`); escalate questions to Seller Operations.
   - **Fraud-related reason, duplicate or previously banned** → do **not** explain the internal reason. Use neutral wording ("we are unable to approve this application") and escalate to **Trust & Safety** if the seller disputes.
   - **No reason visible** → escalate to Seller Operations for the reason that may be shared.
   - **Seller says it is a mistake and the reason looks wrong** → escalate to Seller Operations for re-review; do not promise reversal.
4. Record the reason category and what you told the seller.

## Summary
A seller (or applicant) sends photos of their ID, passport or business documents to support through Telegram, email or another chat channel instead of the official verification flow.

## Issue Description
This happens often: sellers think sending the document to support is faster, or they are asked by an impostor pretending to be Byfluence. Identity documents in chat create privacy and fraud risks — chats can be seen by many agents, forwarded, or stored where they should not be. Support must **not** use these documents for verification, must not forward them, and must guide the seller back to the platform. This also matters for spotting scams: a real Byfluence agent never requests ID by chat.

## Possible Causes
- Seller believes support can verify them faster.
- Upload on the platform failed and the seller is trying a workaround.
- Seller misunderstood an instruction (e.g., "send a screenshot of the error").
- Someone impersonating Byfluence asked them to send ID documents.

## Information to Collect
- Which channel the document was sent through and when
- Whether anyone asked them to send it (and who — username, number, link)
- Whether they also uploaded on the platform
- Why they used chat (upload failure? misunderstanding?)

> [!WARNING] Do not open, save, download, forward or comment on the document's contents. Never ask for passwords, OTP codes or PINs.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Do **not** use the document. Do not reply with any detail from it.
2. Reply immediately with the response below: explain that documents are only accepted through the platform.
3. Remove/limit exposure according to your channel's tools and the instructions of your supervisor (e.g., delete the message from the support side if the channel allows, do not attach the image to the ticket). Note in the ticket: "Customer sent ID document via [channel]; not used; handled per sensitive-data procedure."
4. Ask whether anyone asked them to send it. If yes → treat as possible impersonation → **Trust & Safety**, priority High.
5. If the platform upload failed → troubleshoot with `sel-resubmit-documents`.
6. Inform your supervisor so the exposure is recorded.
