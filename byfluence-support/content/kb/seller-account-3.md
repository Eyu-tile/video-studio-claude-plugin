## Summary
A seller asks where to find things in the seller dashboard (listings, bookings, availability, earnings, withdrawals, profile, status).

## Overview
The seller dashboard is where sellers run their business on Byfluence. According to the business overview, sellers can: build a public profile, create listings, set prices, manage availability, accept bookings/orders, monitor transactions, view earnings and request withdrawals. The exact menu names and layout depend on the live product — check the current dashboard (or a test account) before giving click-by-click directions.

### Main areas (concepts)
| Area | What the seller does there | Related article |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | Public seller profile, photo, bio, expertise | `sel-profile-management` |
| Listings | Create, edit, pause listings; see review status | `lst-create-listing`, `lst-edit-listing` |
| Availability | Set dates/times for consultations | `lst-availability-calendar` |
| Bookings / Orders | Upcoming and past sessions or orders | `con-seller-availability-setup` |
| Transactions | Payments received for bookings/orders | — |
| Earnings | Total Earnings, Pending Balance, Available Balance, Withdrawn Amount, Withdrawal Pending | `out-how-earnings-work`, `out-pending-vs-available` |
| Withdrawals | Request a payout of Available Balance | `out-how-to-withdraw` |
| Account status | Verification and operational status | `sel-account-restrictions` |

### Balance terms (confirmed concepts)
- **Total Earnings** — everything earned so far.
- **Pending Balance** — earned but not yet released (completion, dispute, refund or settlement conditions not yet met).
- **Available Balance** — can be withdrawn.
- **Withdrawn Amount** — already paid out.
- **Withdrawal Pending** — requested, not yet completed.

A successful buyer payment does **not** mean the money is immediately withdrawable.

## Customer Steps
1. Sign in to your seller account.
2. Open the dashboard from your account menu.
3. Use the sections above to manage your profile, listings, availability, bookings and earnings.

## Summary
A seller wants to create or update their public seller profile (name shown, photo, bio, expertise) or asks why a profile change is not showing.

## Overview
The public seller profile is what buyers see when evaluating a seller — especially for consultations, where buyers choose the **person**. A complete, honest profile builds trust and helps sales. Changing **display** information (photo, bio, expertise description) is usually something the seller can do themselves. Changing **identity, business or payout** details is different and is covered in `sel-change-business-details`.

### A strong profile
- Clear, professional photo of the seller (or business logo for businesses, if allowed).
- Name consistent with the verified identity or registered business.
- Short bio: who they are, experience, what they help with.
- Languages they serve in (Amharic, English, others).
- Honest qualifications — no fake certificates, titles or claims.
- No personal phone numbers, Telegram handles or payment details meant to take buyers off the platform (policy pending — advise against).

### Why a change might not show
- Not saved/published.
- Under review (if profile edits are moderated — not confirmed).
- Browser/app cache showing old version.
- The change was rejected for breaking rules.

## Customer Steps
1. Sign in and open your seller profile from the dashboard.
2. Edit the photo, bio, expertise or languages.
3. Save and view your public profile to check the result.
4. If the change does not appear, refresh or try another browser, then contact support.

## Summary
A seller wants to change verified identity details (legal name, business name, business registration) or the payout account/identity where their earnings are sent.

## Issue Description
These are **high-risk changes**. Fraudsters who take over a seller account often try to change the payout account first, or ask support to "update the bank details" by chat. Legitimate reasons exist too: a name correction, registering a business, closing an old bank account, or a business changing ownership. Because verified identity and payouts are linked, changes typically need Seller Operations review and possibly re-verification. Agents never make these changes themselves based on a chat request.

## Possible Causes
- Spelling correction of a legal name.
- Seller registered a business and wants to sell as the business.
- Bank or mobile-money account closed or changed.
- Business sold/transferred to a new owner.
- Account takeover — someone else trying to redirect payouts.

## Information to Collect
- Registered email/phone of the seller account (confirm identity first)
- Which detail they want changed and why
- Whether they can still sign in normally
- Whether any withdrawal is pending right now
- Recent unusual activity they noticed (login alerts, password changes)

> [!WARNING] Never ask for passwords, OTP codes, PINs, full card numbers or full bank account numbers in chat. New identity or business documents must be uploaded on the platform only.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm identity using registered contact details. If the request comes from a different email/phone than the account, **stop** and treat as suspicious.
2. Check for red flags:
   - Request to change payout details **and** recent password/email/phone change.
   - Urgency ("change it now, I need the money today").
   - Request to send payouts to a third party's account.
   - Pending withdrawal to a new account.
   Any red flag → **Trust & Safety**, priority High; ask Payments & Finance to watch pending withdrawals.
3. If the platform lets the seller edit payout details themselves → guide them to do it in the dashboard; remind that changes may trigger review.
4. If the change requires review (legal name, business identity, ownership transfer, payout identity different from verified identity) → collect the reason and escalate to **Seller Operations**; any new documents must be uploaded through the platform (`sel-resubmit-documents`).
5. Ownership transfer of a store → escalate to **Seller Operations**; do not promise it is possible.
6. Record the request and all checks in the ticket.

- [ ] Red flags checked
- [ ] Self-service vs. review path decided
- [ ] Escalated with reason

## Summary
A seller wants to stop selling, close their store, or delete their seller account entirely.

## Issue Description
Closing a seller account affects buyers (upcoming bookings, open orders), money (pending and available earnings, pending refunds/disputes) and data (records that may need to be kept). The seller may want a temporary pause rather than full closure. Agents must find out what the seller really wants, protect buyers with open commitments, make sure earnings questions are handled, and route the closure to Seller Operations. Agents do not delete accounts themselves.

## Possible Causes
- Seller is leaving the marketplace or changing business.
- Seller is unhappy (low sales, fees, a dispute) — may be retained if the issue is solved.
- Seller wants a temporary break (holiday, illness).
- Seller wants their personal data deleted.
- Account security concern (they think the account was compromised).

## Information to Collect
- Registered email/phone of the seller account
- Pause vs. full closure vs. data deletion
- Reason (optional — helps with feedback and retention)
- Upcoming bookings/open orders (check yourself; confirm with seller)
- Earnings status: pending/available balance, withdrawal pending
- Open disputes or refund requests

> [!WARNING] Never ask for passwords, OTP codes or PINs.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm identity.
2. Ask what they want: **pause** (stop new bookings), **close store** (stop selling), or **delete account/data**.
3. If they only need a break → guide them to pause listings or remove availability (`lst-edit-listing`, `lst-availability-calendar`).
4. If the reason is a solvable problem (e.g., a payout issue, a dispute) → offer to help with that first; respect their decision.
5. Check through authorized tools (admin dashboard, if you have access):
   - Upcoming bookings/orders → they must be delivered or properly handled before closure; flag to **Senior Support / Supervisor** if buyers would be affected.
   - Pending/available balance and pending withdrawals → explain that earnings questions will be handled by **Payments & Finance**; do not promise a final payout date.
   - Open disputes/refunds → must be resolved first.
6. Escalate the closure request to **Seller Operations** with a summary (bookings, balances, disputes).
7. Data deletion requests → also note for **Trust & Safety** (privacy).

### Confirmed seller responsibilities (from the business model)
- **Accurate identity**: complete identity verification honestly and keep information current.
- **Accurate listings**: describe products/services truthfully — title, description, topic, duration, price, availability.
- **Delivery**: deliver what was sold. For consultations: attend on time, at the scheduled time, using the meeting details provided; a scheduled meeting does not prove delivery.
- **Availability**: keep availability up to date so buyers don't book times the seller can't attend.
- **Follow marketplace rules**: no prohibited or misleading listings.
- **Professional conduct**: respectful communication with buyers and staff.

### Expected good practice (advise, while formal rules are pending)
- Keep communication and payments on the platform.
- Respond to buyer questions and support requests promptly.
- Inform support early if they cannot attend a session.
- Protect buyer personal data; use it only to deliver the service.

> [!POLICY] A formal Seller Code of Conduct and Seller Terms have not yet been approved. Present the good-practice points as guidance, not as rules with penalties.

## Customer Steps
1. Keep your profile and listings accurate and current.
2. Keep your availability calendar up to date.
3. Attend every booked session on time; contact support immediately if you can't.
4. Keep communication and payments on Byfluence.

## Summary
A seller reports a problem with a buyer: no-show, harassment or abuse, unreasonable demands, a complaint or refund request the seller believes is false, or a request to transact off-platform.

## Issue Description
Sellers need to feel protected too. Typical reports: the buyer did not attend the consultation; the buyer was abusive in the session or in messages; the buyer recorded the session or shared materials without permission; the buyer demands a refund after a delivered session; the buyer asks to pay outside Byfluence; the buyer leaves threats. The agent must collect facts neutrally from the seller, **not** take sides, protect both parties' personal data, and route to the right team. Safety threats are urgent.

## Possible Causes
- Buyer no-show or late arrival (time-zone confusion, connection problems, forgot).
- Misunderstanding about what the service included.
- Buyer dissatisfaction leading to a dispute/refund request.
- Abusive or harassing behavior.
- Fraud attempt (chargeback after delivery, fake payment proof, off-platform scam).

## Information to Collect
- Seller's registered email/phone
- Booking/order ID ({{consultation_id}} / {{order_id}}), date and time
- What happened, in the seller's words, with times
- Evidence: screenshots of on-platform messages, session notes; **no** recordings shared without consent
- Whether the seller feels unsafe or was threatened
- What outcome the seller wants (report only, block, dispute response)

> [!WARNING] Do not share the buyer's personal details with the seller (or vice versa) beyond what the platform already shows. Never ask for passwords, OTP codes or PINs.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Confirm the seller's identity and locate the booking/order through authorized tools (admin dashboard, if you have access).
2. Classify the report:
   - **Threats, harassment, hate speech, sexual misconduct** → **Trust & Safety**, priority High (Critical if a physical threat). Advise the seller not to respond further.
   - **Buyer no-show** → record attendance facts (seller joined? when? evidence on platform). Explain that no-show handling and any earnings impact follow official rules (pending). Escalate to **Senior Support / Supervisor** if the buyer is also disputing.
   - **Buyer dispute/refund request the seller disagrees with** → ask the seller for their account of delivery; attach to the dispute; **Senior Support / Supervisor** reviews. Do not promise the seller the dispute will be rejected.
   - **Off-platform payment request** → thank the seller for reporting; note it; **Trust & Safety** if suspicious or repeated.
   - **Suspected fraud (chargeback, fake proof)** → **Trust & Safety** + **Payments & Finance**.
3. Check whether the buyer has other reports — tell the seller only that the report is recorded.
4. Respond to the seller and record everything.
