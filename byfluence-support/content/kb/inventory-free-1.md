## Summary
A person asks what Byfluence's inventory-free selling (local dropshipping) is and how it works. This is a **planned** feature.

## Overview
> [!WARNING] Inventory-free selling is a **planned** expansion feature. Check the **Feature Status** page before every conversation. If it is not live, do not tell anyone they can join or start selling now, and do not give launch dates, margins or terms.

Inventory-free selling is designed to let people start selling **without buying stock first**. The model described in the business overview:

1. Byfluence partners with **suppliers** or verified sellers who own the products.
2. Eligible **resellers** promote/resell approved products under **platform pricing rules**.
3. A buyer orders and pays on Byfluence.
4. The **supplier or a designated fulfillment partner** fulfils the order.
5. The reseller earns an **agreed margin or commission**.

### Who is who
| Role | Responsibility (as described) |
|---|---|
| Supplier | Provides approved products, stock and product information; fulfils or hands over to a fulfillment partner |
| Reseller | Promotes and sells approved products honestly within pricing rules |
| Fulfillment partner | Delivers orders where designated (delivery details not confirmed) |
| Byfluence | Marketplace, payments, rules, approval of products/suppliers/resellers |
| Buyer | Orders and pays on Byfluence |

**Not defined yet:** commercial terms, eligibility, margins, cost responsibilities, return/refund handling, delivery areas and fees. Physical-product delivery starts with an **Addis Ababa** focus, but delivery details are not confirmed.

## Customer Steps
1. Follow official Byfluence announcements for the program launch.
2. Do not pay anyone claiming to "register you early" for the program.

## Summary
A potential reseller understands "zero investment" as "no costs and guaranteed income". Explain what it really means.

## Overview
> [!WARNING] Inventory-free selling is a **planned** feature. Check the **Feature Status** page before discussing it. Do not promise availability, income or terms.

The business overview is explicit: **"Zero investment" means no upfront stock — it does NOT mean zero costs or zero risk.**

### It means
- The reseller does not need to buy products in advance to sell them.
- The supplier holds the stock.

### It does NOT mean
- No costs at all — resellers may still spend on internet, phone, time, marketing/advertising, and possibly fees (terms not defined).
- Guaranteed sales or income — earnings depend on actual sales completed under the program rules.
- No responsibility — resellers must describe products honestly and follow pricing and marketplace rules.
- No risk — earnings may be affected by cancellations, returns, refunds or disputes (rules not defined).

Agents must correct "get rich with no money" expectations kindly but clearly — misleading expectations lead to complaints and can be a sign of scam promoters using Byfluence's name.

## Customer Steps
1. Read official program terms when they are published.
2. Plan for your own costs (internet, time, promotion).
3. Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed income.

## Recommended Response (EN)

It does not mean there are no costs or risks. You may still have your own costs, such as internet, time and promotion, and earnings depend on real sales completed under the program rules. Nobody can guarantee income.

The program is part of Byfluence's plans, and its official terms have not been announced yet. We will share them through {{support_channel}} when available. Please be careful of anyone promising guaranteed income in Byfluence's name.
