## Summary
A seller asks what fees apply to their sales or withdrawals (payment processing, withdrawal or bank fees). No fee amounts are confirmed — explain the possible types and point to the recorded breakdown only.

## Overview
Fees a seller might encounter, each **not confirmed** for Byfluence:
- **Platform commission** — Byfluence's share of a sale (see *Platform Commission Questions*).
- **Payment processing fee** — the cost charged by the payment provider for the buyer's payment; whether it is absorbed by Byfluence, passed to the seller, or charged to the buyer is not confirmed.
- **Withdrawal / payout fee** — a charge for sending the payout; not confirmed.
- **Bank or mobile-money charges** — the seller's own bank or provider may charge to receive funds; Byfluence does not control these.

> [!POLICY] No fee type or amount is confirmed. Never quote a percentage or fixed amount and never say "there are no fees". Read back only what the system shows for the seller's specific transaction or withdrawal.

## Customer Steps
1. Open the earnings breakdown for a sale, or the details of a withdrawal, in your seller dashboard.
2. Check the lines shown (sale amount, fees, net amount).
3. If something is unclear, contact support with the booking/order number or withdrawal date.

## Summary
A seller formally disagrees with a deduction, a refund taken from their earnings, a chargeback adjustment, or the amount of a payout. Gather the facts and the seller's evidence and route the case for an authorized decision.

## Issue Description
Common triggers: a buyer refund reduced the seller's earnings, a chargeback removed earnings for a session the seller says was delivered, a payout was smaller than the Available Balance shown, or a fee/commission the seller thinks is wrong. The seller may feel they are being punished unfairly and may threaten to leave or complain publicly. Decisions on refunds, chargebacks and deductions need authorized personnel; support's role is to investigate and present the case.

## Possible Causes
- Refund approved after a buyer complaint or dispute.
- Chargeback/reversal by the buyer's bank or provider.
- Cancellation (by buyer, seller or system).
- Commission/fee applied as per (unconfirmed) terms.
- Payout fee or partial payout.
- Calculation or system error.
- Penalty or adjustment for policy violation (policy not confirmed).

## Information to Collect
- Seller identity
- Booking/order IDs, withdrawal ID/date, and amounts in question
- What the seller believes the correct amount is and why
- Seller's evidence: proof the session was delivered (e.g. messages, session notes, screenshots showing participation), delivery proof for products, communication with the buyer through Byfluence
- Any notification the seller received about the deduction

> [!WARNING] Never ask for passwords, PINs or OTPs. Do not share the buyer's personal or payment details with the seller.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Verify identity.
2. Locate each item in the admin dashboard and identify the event that caused the deduction (refund, chargeback, cancellation, fee, adjustment).
3. Check whether a decision was already made and documented (e.g. dispute decision on the buyer side).
4. Explain what the record shows, neutrally.
5. If the seller still disagrees:
   - Collect their evidence and a clear statement.
   - Escalate: refund/dispute decisions → **Senior Support / Supervisor** (with **Payments & Finance**); chargebacks → **Payments & Finance**; fees/calculation errors → **Payments & Finance**; policy penalties → **Seller Operations**.
6. Tell the seller the case is under review; no outcome promised.
7. Record everything.

- [ ] Identity verified
- [ ] Cause of each deduction identified
- [ ] Record explained to seller
- [ ] Evidence collected
- [ ] Escalated to the correct owner

## Summary
A decision tree for any seller money question — earnings, balances, withdrawals, payouts, fees, holds and deductions — with the first checks, the right article and the right department.

## Overview
Seller payout tickets are high-trust: sellers depend on this income, and payout systems are a major fraud target. Use this guide when a seller contacts support about money and you are not sure which article applies.

> [!WARNING] Universal rules for every seller money ticket:
> - Verify the seller's identity before discussing any balance, sale or payout.
> - Never quote commission/fee rates, payout methods, minimums or schedules — all are **Policy Pending**.
> - Never promise a payout date or release date.
> - Never ask for PINs, OTPs, passwords or full account numbers; discuss only the last digits.
> - Never change payout details over chat; treat last-minute payout-detail changes as a fraud signal.
> - Never share buyer personal or payment details with a seller.

## Troubleshooting Steps

### Step 1 — First checks (every case)
- [ ] Identity verified (registered email or phone number).
- [ ] Seller verification status (Pending / Verified / Rejected) and operational status (Active / Suspended / Banned) checked.
- [ ] Any payout hold flag or open investigation checked.
- [ ] Balances noted: Total Earnings, Pending, Available, Withdrawal Pending, Withdrawn.
- [ ] Recent payout-detail, email, phone or password changes checked.
- [ ] Announcements checked for payout incidents.

### Step 2 — Identify the symptom
| Seller says | Go to |
|---|---|
| "How do I get paid?" / "How do earnings work?" | *How Seller Earnings Work* |
| "Why is my money pending?" | *Pending vs Available Balance* |
| "A sale is missing from my balance" | Step 3A |
| "Why did I get less than the price?" | Step 3B |
| "How do I withdraw?" | *How to Request a Withdrawal* |
| "My withdrawal is still pending / I haven't received it" | Step 3C |
| "My withdrawal was rejected / cancelled" | *Withdrawal Cancelled or Rejected* |
| "My payout failed / was returned" | *Payout Failed* |
| "I need to change my bank / Telebirr number" | Step 3D |
| "I can't withdraw / my account is on hold" | Step 3E |
| "You took money from my earnings unfairly" | Step 3F |
| "What fees / commission do you charge?" | *Transaction and Payout Fees* / *Platform Commission Questions* |
| "I need an invoice / statement / tax info" | *Tax, Receipts and Invoice Questions* |
| "Did my buyer pay?" | Check the booking/order payment status; confirm only whether it is confirmed — never share payment references or buyer payment details |

### Step 3A — Missing earnings
1. Booking/order belongs to this seller account? If not → **Seller Operations**.
2. Buyer payment verified? No → no earnings yet; explain.
3. Earnings record exists? Pending → explain; already withdrawn → show; refunded/reversed → explain neutrally; none despite verified payment → **Payments & Finance**.
4. Seller says buyer paid directly → off-platform; **Trust & Safety**.

### Step 3B — Calculation
1. Read the recorded breakdown; never estimate rates.
2. Adjustments (refund/chargeback) → explain; seller disagrees → Step 3F.
3. Numbers don't reconcile → **Payments & Finance**.

### Step 3C — Withdrawal delayed or not received
1. Locate the request; status and destination (last digits).
2. Pending within internal threshold → status update; beyond → **Payments & Finance**.
3. Completed but not received → confirm destination; then **Payments & Finance** for provider confirmation.
4. Destination not the seller's → **Trust & Safety**, Critical; hold withdrawals.

### Step 3D — Payout details change
1. Extra identity care; check change history.
2. No withdrawal pending, no fraud signal → seller updates in dashboard / approved process.
3. Withdrawal pending with wrong details → **Payments & Finance** to hold.
4. Fraud signals (urgency, new device, recent account changes, name mismatch) → **Trust & Safety**, Critical.

### Step 3E — Hold or restriction
1. Identify hold type: verification, suspension/ban, disputes/chargebacks, risk review, missing info.
2. Share only the approved reason category; route to **Seller Operations** / **Trust & Safety** / **Payments & Finance**.

### Step 3F — Deduction dispute
1. Identify cause of each deduction.
2. Collect seller evidence; escalate to the decision owner (*Seller Disputes a Payout or Deduction*).

### Step 4 — Before you close
- [ ] Seller received a clear explanation or a status update with `{{ticket_id}}`.
- [ ] No timeline or rate was promised.
- [ ] Fraud checks recorded for any payout-detail change.
- [ ] Root cause tagged (pending release, already withdrawn, refund/chargeback, wrong details, provider delay, hold, system error).
