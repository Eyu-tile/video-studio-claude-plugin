## Summary
Explains the full life cycle of a one-on-one consultation on Byfluence — from finding a consultant to the session being marked completed — so agents can answer "how does this work?" questions from buyers and sellers.

## Overview
One-on-one consultations are Byfluence's core MVP product. A **seller** (consultant, coach, professional) lists a paid session; a **buyer** books and pays for a time; both meet on **Google Meet** at the scheduled time.

**Byfluence's role:** Byfluence is a marketplace facilitator. It provides the listing, booking, payment and meeting infrastructure. The **seller delivers the consultation** and is responsible for its content and quality, subject to marketplace rules. Never describe Byfluence as the consultant.

### The life cycle (confirmed flow)
| # | Step | Who | What the agent should know |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discover | Buyer | Buyer browses or searches for consultants and topics. |
| 2 | Evaluate the listing | Buyer | The listing shows consultant name, description, topic, duration, price and availability. |
| 3 | Select a time | Buyer | Buyer picks an available date and time from the seller's availability. |
| 4 | Pay | Buyer | Payment is made through the platform's payment provider. |
| 5 | Booking confirmed | System | The booking is confirmed **only after the payment is successful**. |
| 6 | Meeting details | System / Seller | Buyer receives the Google Meet details for the session. |
| 7 | Attend | Both | Both join the Google Meet at the scheduled time. |
| 8 | Completed | System / Seller | The consultation is marked completed. |
| 9 | Seller earnings | System | Seller earnings move from pending to available according to settlement rules. |

### Key facts to repeat to customers
- A booking is **not confirmed until payment succeeds**. Choosing a time alone does not reserve it.
- Consultations happen on **Google Meet**, not inside the Byfluence website.
- Times should always be communicated in **East Africa Time (EAT, UTC+3)** using the international clock, with the Ethiopian-clock equivalent when speaking Amharic (e.g., 9:00 AM EAT = ጠዋት 3 ሰዓት). See *Time-zone or wrong-time confusion*.
- A scheduled meeting does not prove the session happened. If something goes wrong, evidence matters (see *Seller didn't join the consultation*).
- The buyer's upcoming sessions and booking-management actions are in the **buyer dashboard → consultation bookings**.

> [!NOTE] Whether the Google Meet link is generated automatically by Byfluence or added by the seller is not yet confirmed. Check the internal Feature Status page and the booking record before explaining where the link comes from.

## Customer Steps
1. Sign in to Byfluence (or create a buyer account).
2. Find a consultant by browsing or searching, and open their consultation listing.
3. Read the description, topic, duration and price carefully.
4. Choose an available date and time.
5. Complete payment.
6. Check the buyer dashboard for the confirmed booking and the Google Meet details.
7. Join the Google Meet at the scheduled time (EAT), signed in to a Google account if possible.

## Summary
Step-by-step guidance for a buyer who wants to book a one-on-one consultation, including what to check before paying and what should happen after.

## Overview
Booking is a short flow: **listing → time → payment → confirmation**. Most "how do I book?" contacts are from first-time buyers. Walk them through it patiently; many are on a mobile phone and may be using a payment method for the first time on a website.

Before the buyer pays, make sure they have checked:
- **The topic and description** — is this the help they need?
- **The duration and price** — shown on the listing.
- **The date and time** — in EAT, international clock (e.g., 3:00 PM EAT = ከሰዓት 9 ሰዓት in the Ethiopian clock).
- **That they are signed in to the right account** — the booking will appear only in the account used to book.

> [!NOTE] Which payment methods are live is not confirmed for every provider. Check the Feature Status page before telling a buyer which method they can use.

## Customer Steps
1. Sign in to your Byfluence account.
2. Search or browse for the consultant or topic you need, and open the consultation listing.
3. Read the description, duration and price.
4. Select an available date, then an available time. Check the time carefully — times are in EAT (international clock).
5. Continue to payment and choose your payment method.
6. Complete the payment on the payment page. Do not close the page until you are returned to Byfluence.
7. Check your dashboard → consultation bookings. The booking should show as confirmed.
8. Find the Google Meet details for your session in the booking (or in your confirmation message).

## Summary
Helps a buyer understand and choose from a consultant's available times, and avoid the most common mistake: misreading the time because of time zones or the Ethiopian clock.

## Overview
The times a buyer can choose come from the **seller's availability settings**. The buyer can only select times the seller has opened and that are not already booked.

The biggest risk at this step is **misreading the time**:
- Byfluence times should be read in **EAT (UTC+3)** on the **international (24-hour / AM-PM) clock**.
- Many Ethiopians tell time with the **Ethiopian clock**, where the day starts at 6:00 AM international time. "3 o'clock in the morning" in Ethiopian time (ጠዋት 3 ሰዓት) is **9:00 AM** international.
- A slot shown as "9:00" may be read by a buyer as "ሰዓት 9" (= 3:00 PM international). Always clarify AM/PM and the period of the day.
- Buyers outside Ethiopia, or whose phone is set to another time zone, may see a converted time. Whether Byfluence displays times in EAT or in the device's local time is **not yet confirmed** — see *Calendar and time display problems* (`tec-calendar-time-display`).
- Dates: Byfluence uses Gregorian dates (to be verified). A buyer thinking in the Ethiopian calendar (e.g., ኅዳር, ታኅሣሥ) may pick the wrong day. Ask for the **weekday** to double-check.

### Quick Ethiopian-clock reference
| International (EAT) | Ethiopian clock (Amharic) |
|---|---|
| 7:00 AM | ጠዋት 1 ሰዓት |
| 9:00 AM | ጠዋት 3 ሰዓት |
| 12:00 PM (noon) | ቀን 6 ሰዓት |
| 3:00 PM | ከሰዓት 9 ሰዓት |
| 6:00 PM | ምሽት 12 ሰዓት |
| 8:00 PM | ማታ 2 ሰዓት |

## Customer Steps
1. On the consultation listing, choose a date (check the weekday too).
2. Look at the available times for that date. Unavailable or booked times cannot be selected.
3. Read the time carefully: it is in EAT, international clock. Check AM or PM.
4. If you think in Ethiopian time, convert before choosing (international time minus 6 hours = Ethiopian clock hour; e.g., 2:00 PM = ከሰዓት 8 ሰዓት).
5. Select the time and continue to payment. The slot is yours only after payment succeeds.

## Summary
A buyer sees no bookable times on a consultation listing, or a seller reports that buyers cannot find any times to book.

## Issue Description
The buyer opens a consultation listing and the calendar is empty, every time is greyed out, or the dates they want show nothing. Sometimes the seller contacts support because they are not receiving bookings and suspect buyers cannot see their times. The buyer may be frustrated or think the listing is fake; the seller may be losing sales.

## Possible Causes
- The seller has not set any availability, or availability only covers past dates.
- All the seller's open times are already booked.
- The seller's availability is outside the dates the buyer is looking at (e.g., only next month).
- A minimum notice period (if one exists) hides times that are too soon.
- The seller's account or listing is paused, suspended, unpublished or under review.
- A time-zone/display error shifts slots so they appear in the past or disappear.
- A page loading problem: slow connection, outdated browser/app cache, or a booking system error.

## Information to Collect
- Buyer: name and registered email/phone; the listing link or consultant name; the dates they tried; screenshot of the empty calendar; device and browser/app.
- Seller: name and registered email/phone; listing name; a screenshot of their availability settings; the dates/times they believe are open.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Open the listing yourself (buyer view). Are times visible to you?
   - **Visible to you but not to the buyer** → device/browser problem. Ask the buyer to refresh, try another browser, update the app/browser, clear cache, or try mobile data vs Wi-Fi. Collect a screenshot if it persists.
   - **Not visible to you either** → continue.
2. Check the listing and seller status in the admin dashboard: is the listing active and published? Is the seller Active (not Suspended/Banned)? If not, do **not** explain internal enforcement details to the buyer — say the consultant is not accepting bookings at the moment.
3. Check the seller's availability (if you have access): are there open times in the future? Are they all booked?
4. If availability exists but is not shown → possible display or booking error → escalate to **Technical / Engineering** with the listing ID, seller's availability screenshot and your own screenshot.
5. If no availability exists → for a buyer: offer alternatives (check again later, choose another consultant) and, if appropriate, send a contact request to the seller via `tpl-seller-contact-request`. For a seller: guide them through *Seller: setting up consultation availability*.
6. Record what you found in the ticket.

## Summary
Explains to buyers why a consultation booking is confirmed only after successful payment, and handles requests to "pay later", "pay the consultant directly" or "reserve without paying".

## Overview
On Byfluence, **a booking is confirmed only after the payment is successful**. Until then, the time is not reserved for the buyer.

Why this protects everyone:
- **The buyer**: the payment is recorded on Byfluence, so if something goes wrong (e.g., the consultant doesn't join) there is a record and a support process.
- **The seller**: their time is reserved only for buyers who have committed.
- **The marketplace**: payments outside Byfluence cannot be traced, protected or supported.

Common requests and how to answer:
| Request | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Can I pay after the session?" | No — the booking is confirmed only after payment. |
| "Can I reserve now and pay later?" | Choosing a time does not reserve it; pay to confirm. Whether slots are held during payment is not confirmed. |
| "The consultant told me to pay them directly / by bank transfer." | Please do not pay outside Byfluence. Report it to us. (Trust & Safety signal.) |
| "My payment is pending — is my booking safe?" | It is confirmed when the payment is confirmed. See `pay-pending-transaction`. |

## Customer Steps
1. Choose your time on the listing.
2. Pay through the Byfluence payment page only.
3. Wait to be returned to Byfluence and check that the booking shows as confirmed in your dashboard.
4. If money was deducted but the booking is not confirmed, contact support with your transaction reference.

## Summary
The buyer says they booked a consultation, but it does not appear in their dashboard's consultation bookings.

## Issue Description
The buyer believes they completed a booking — often they paid — but the upcoming sessions list is empty or does not include this session. They are worried that the booking failed, that they lost money, or that they won't receive the meeting link. If the session is close, they may be panicking.

First separate two very different situations:
- **Payment was taken** → this is primarily a payment-confirmation issue: use `pay-paid-booking-not-confirmed` as the main article.
- **No payment was taken / payment unclear** → the booking was probably never completed, or is under another account.

## Possible Causes
- The payment is still pending, failed, or was never completed — so no booking was confirmed.
- The buyer is signed in to a different account (another email or phone number).
- The session date has passed and it moved to history/past sessions.
- The booking was cancelled (by the seller, the buyer, or the system).
- The dashboard is showing a cached/old version (app or browser cache).
- A display or synchronisation bug between bookings and the dashboard.

## Information to Collect
- Full name and the email/phone of the account they are signed in with now
- Any other email/phone they might have used
- Consultant name and consultation title
- Date and time they booked (ask them to confirm EAT vs Ethiopian clock)
- Whether they paid; if yes, payment method, transaction reference and time
- Screenshot of the dashboard showing the empty list (optional)

> [!WARNING] Never ask for passwords or verification codes. The transaction reference is enough to trace the payment.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Verify the customer's identity with their registered email or phone.
2. Search bookings in the admin dashboard by customer, consultant and date. Include **pending, cancelled and past** bookings.
3. Decide the case:
   - **Booking confirmed on the customer's account** → display problem. Ask them to sign out and back in, refresh, update the app, or try another browser. If still missing → escalate to **Technical / Engineering** with the booking ID and screenshots. Meanwhile, give the customer the booking details and Meet link yourself (after identity check).
   - **Booking confirmed on another account of the same person** → explain which account (by masked email/phone, e.g., "the account ending in …45"); ask them to sign in there.
   - **Booking pending + payment successful** → `pay-paid-booking-not-confirmed`.
   - **Booking cancelled** → find who/what cancelled it and follow `con-cancellation-by-seller` or `con-cancellation-by-buyer`.
   - **No booking and no payment** → the booking was not completed; help them book again.
   - **No booking but payment taken** → `pay-paid-booking-not-confirmed`.
4. If the session starts within 24 hours, set priority **High**; if within the next hour, **Critical**.
5. Record what you found in internal notes.

- [ ] Identity confirmed
- [ ] All booking statuses searched
- [ ] Payment checked
- [ ] Customer given booking details if confirmed

## Summary
A seller says they cannot see a booking that a buyer says they made and paid for, or the buyer reports that the seller "doesn't know about" their session.

## Issue Description
This can reach support from either side: the **seller** says "a buyer messaged me about a booking I can't see", or the **buyer** says the consultant told them there is no booking. If not fixed before the session time, it causes a seller no-show and a dispute. Treat it as **High**, and **Critical** if the session is today.

## Possible Causes
- The payment is still pending, so the booking is not yet confirmed and not shown to the seller.
- The booking is under a different listing or seller account (the seller has two accounts).
- The seller is looking at the wrong date because of time zone / Ethiopian clock confusion.
- Seller dashboard display bug or notification failure.
- The booking was cancelled or rescheduled and the seller is looking at the old time.
- The "buyer" is attempting a scam (claims a booking that does not exist, then asks for direct payment or a "refund").

## Information to Collect
- Seller: name, registered email/phone, listing name
- Buyer's name (as given) and booking date/time
- Booking ID or transaction reference, if either side has one
- Screenshot of the seller's bookings view

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Verify the seller's identity.
2. Search the booking in the admin dashboard by booking ID, buyer, seller, listing and date.
3. Decide the case:
   - **Booking confirmed and assigned to this seller** → display/notification problem. Give the seller the booking details (date, time EAT, consultation title, buyer's first name — not the buyer's personal contact), confirm they will attend, and escalate to **Technical / Engineering**.
   - **Booking pending payment** → tell the seller it is not yet confirmed; follow `pay-paid-booking-not-confirmed` for the buyer.
   - **Booking under another seller account/listing** → confirm with the seller which account they use; route to **Seller Operations** if accounts need merging/clarifying.
   - **No booking found** → do not ask the seller to deliver anything. If a "buyer" is asking the seller for direct payment or a refund off-platform → **Trust & Safety**.
4. If the session is today and the Meet link setup depends on the seller, make sure the seller has or creates the link (see `con-missing-meet-link`).
5. Inform the buyer that the seller has been notified (if confirmed booking).

## Summary
Guides a seller through setting up the times buyers can book for their consultation, and the habits that prevent double bookings, wrong-time sessions and no-shows.

## Overview
Sellers manage availability as part of their seller tools; buyers can only book times the seller has opened. A listing with no future availability cannot receive bookings.

Before setting availability, the seller should have:
- A **verified seller account** with selling permissions (status Verified and Active).
- An approved, published **consultation listing** (see `lst-create-consultation-listing`).

Good availability habits to share with sellers:
1. **Set times in EAT on the international clock.** If you think in Ethiopian time, convert first: ጠዋት 3 ሰዓት = 9:00 AM; ከሰዓት 8 ሰዓት = 2:00 PM; ማታ 2 ሰዓት = 8:00 PM.
2. **Only open times you can definitely attend.** A booked time is a commitment to a paying buyer.
3. **Leave a buffer** between sessions for overruns and connection problems.
4. **Remove or block times** in advance when you will be unavailable (travel, holidays, power or internet outages you expect).
5. **Check your Google account** works with Google Meet before your first session.
6. **Check your dashboard regularly** for new bookings.

> [!NOTE] The exact screens, labels and options (recurring weekly hours, one-off dates, buffer settings) are not documented yet. Ask the seller for a screenshot and guide from what they see; escalate to Technical if an option does not work.

## Customer Steps
1. Sign in to your seller account and open your seller dashboard.
2. Open your consultation listing or the availability/calendar section.
3. Add the days and times you are available, in EAT (international clock). Check AM/PM.
4. Save, then open your listing as a buyer would (or ask support) to confirm the times appear.
5. Update availability whenever your schedule changes; block times you cannot attend before buyers book them.

## Escalation Conditions
- Saved availability does not appear on the listing → **Technical / Engineering** (`tec-calendar-time-display`).
- Seller cannot access availability settings because the account is not verified/active → **Seller Operations**.
- Seller asks to remove a time that a buyer has already booked → this is a reschedule/cancellation: `con-reschedule-request-seller` or `con-cancellation-by-seller`.

## Internal Notes
- New sellers often set times in Ethiopian clock by mistake (e.g., enter "3:00" meaning ጠዋት 3 ሰዓት). After they save, read the times back to them in both clocks.
- Sellers outside Ethiopia must still think in EAT if the platform displays EAT — confirm display behaviour on the Feature Status page.
- Never edit a seller's availability on their behalf unless an approved procedure allows it.
