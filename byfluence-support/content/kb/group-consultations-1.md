## Summary
Explains group consultations — one professional, many buyers in the same Google Meet session — and how they differ from one-on-one consultations.

## Overview
A **group consultation** is a session where **one seller (professional) meets many buyers at once**, for example a workshop, a class or a Q&A. It is part of the MVP.

| | One-on-one | Group |
|---|---|---|
| Participants | 1 buyer + seller | Many buyers + seller |
| Time | Buyer picks from seller's availability | Seller sets one fixed date and time |
| Capacity | One booking per slot | Seller sets a maximum number of participants |
| Booking | Book a time and pay | Register and pay |
| Meeting | Google Meet | Google Meet (shared by all participants) |
| Attention | Personal | Shared; questions may be limited by time |

### The flow (confirmed)
1. The seller creates the group session: date, time, duration, price and capacity.
2. Buyers register and pay.
3. **Confirmed participants** (successful payment) receive meeting access.
4. Everyone joins the same Google Meet at the scheduled time.

### What is NOT yet decided (policy pending)
Rules for capacity changes, full sessions, cancellations, rescheduling, refunds, missed sessions, partial attendance and seller no-shows in group sessions have **not been approved**. Do not promise outcomes; escalate decisions.

### Things buyers should know
- Times are in **EAT**, international clock (add Ethiopian clock in Amharic: 6:00 PM = ምሽት 12 ሰዓት).
- Other participants may see your name (from your Google account) and your camera if it is on.
- The meeting link is for **registered participants only** — please don't share it.

## Customer Steps
1. Open the group session listing and check the topic, date, time (EAT), duration, price and remaining places.
2. Register and pay. Your place is confirmed only after successful payment.
3. Check your dashboard for the confirmed registration and the meeting details.
4. Join the Google Meet at the scheduled time, signed in to your Google account.

## Summary
Step-by-step help for a buyer registering and paying for a group consultation, and how to check that the registration is confirmed.

## Overview
Registering for a group session is similar to booking a one-on-one consultation, except the date and time are fixed by the seller and the number of places is limited. **A place is confirmed only after successful payment.** Whether a place is held while the buyer pays is not confirmed — when only a few places remain, a buyer may lose the place if payment is delayed.

## Customer Steps
1. Sign in and open the group session listing.
2. Check the topic, date (weekday), time in EAT (international clock), duration, price and places remaining.
3. Register / continue to payment.
4. Complete payment and wait to be returned to Byfluence (don't close the page).
5. Check your dashboard for the confirmed registration and the meeting details.
6. If money was deducted but the registration isn't confirmed, contact support with your transaction reference.

## Summary
A buyer cannot register because the group session is full, paid while the last place was taken, or a seller asks about changing capacity.

## Issue Description
Common situations:
1. **Buyer sees "full"** and asks to be added anyway or put on a waitlist.
2. **Buyer paid but the session filled** while they were paying — money taken, no place (urgent).
3. **Seller wants more places** because of demand, or fewer.
4. **More people in the meeting than registered** — the link was shared.
Capacity rules (changes, overbooking, waitlists) are **not yet approved**.

## Possible Causes
- Capacity reached legitimately.
- Simultaneous payments for the last places.
- Seller set capacity low by mistake.
- Display error showing "full" when places remain.
- Google Meet's own participant limit (depends on the organizer's Google plan) is lower than the capacity set.

## Information to Collect
- Session name, seller, date/time
- Buyer: whether they paid; transaction reference; screenshot of the "full" message
- Seller: requested new capacity and reason

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Check the session's capacity and confirmed registrations in the admin dashboard.
2. **Buyer, not paid, session full** → explain it is full; suggest checking for future sessions by the same seller; optionally tell the seller there is demand.
3. **Buyer paid, no place** → treat as High: escalate to **Payments & Finance** and **Senior Support / Supervisor** (place vs refund decision is policy pending). Do not tell the buyer they are registered.
4. **Shows full but places remain** → **Technical / Engineering**.
5. **Seller wants to change capacity** → if the dashboard allows it, guide them; remind them that the Google Meet participant limit of their account must be high enough. Reducing capacity below current registrations → **Senior Support / Supervisor**.
6. **Unregistered people in the meeting** → see `grp-participant-access-denied` (link sharing).

## Summary
Guides a seller through setting up a group session (date, time, duration, price, capacity) and running it smoothly on Google Meet.

## Overview
Sellers with selling permissions can create group sessions. The seller sets **date, time, duration, price and capacity**. Buyers register and pay; confirmed participants get meeting access.

### Set-up checklist
1. Seller account Verified and Active; listing permissions granted.
2. Clear title and description: topic, what participants will learn, language (Amharic/English), level.
3. Date and time in **EAT, international clock** — double-check AM/PM; write the Ethiopian-clock time in the Amharic description if helpful (e.g., 6:00 PM = ምሽት 12 ሰዓት).
4. Realistic capacity — within your Google Meet account's participant limit.
5. Price and duration.

### Running the session
- **Meeting link:** whether it is created automatically or by you is not yet confirmed — check your session details; see `con-seller-meet-link-setup`.
- **Join early** to admit participants. Admit only registered participants — names in Google Meet may differ from Byfluence names, so check carefully and ask in the chat if unsure.
- **Host controls:** mute participants if needed; manage chat and screen sharing.
- **Privacy:** don't share participants' contact details with other participants; ask before recording (see `con-recording-requests`).

## Summary
A registered participant is stuck on "Asking to join", is denied entry or removed from a group meeting — or a seller reports unregistered people trying to join.

## Issue Description
In group sessions the host must often admit many people. A participant may be missed, denied because their Google name doesn't match their registration, blocked by meeting settings, or removed by mistake. Sellers may also see strangers (people who got a shared link) asking to join. Time is lost quickly, so this is urgent while the session runs.

## Possible Causes
- Host hasn't seen the request among many.
- Participant's Google account name differs from their Byfluence name; host denied them as "unknown".
- Participant isn't signed in to Google, and the meeting blocks anonymous users.
- Repeated "Ask to join" requests were denied, so Google temporarily blocks further requests.
- Participant removed by mistake or for behaviour.
- Meeting participant limit reached.
- Unregistered people using a shared link (seller side).

## Information to Collect
- Session, seller, time; participant's registered name and the name shown on their Google account
- Exact screen message; screenshot
- For sellers: the names of people asking to join that they don't recognise

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Verify the participant is confirmed for this session.
2. Contact the seller immediately (`tpl-seller-contact-request`): give the participant's Byfluence name **and** the Google name they are joining with, and ask the seller to admit them.
3. Ask the participant to sign in to Google and try once more (don't spam requests).
4. If "You can't join this video call" persists → seller checks access settings or shares a fresh link to confirmed participants only.
5. If the meeting is at its participant limit → check whether unregistered people are inside; seller should remove them.
6. If the participant was removed for behaviour → get the seller's account; **Trust & Safety** if conduct is serious.
7. Seller reports strangers → advise admitting only names that match the participant list; if the link was leaked, report to **Trust & Safety** if it seems deliberate (e.g., link resold).
8. Record time lost for the participant.

## Escalation Conditions
- Seller unreachable during the session → **Senior Support / Supervisor**.
- Participant missed all or most of the session → **Senior Support / Supervisor** (`grp-partial-attendance`).
- Participant removed for alleged misconduct, or harassment inside the meeting → **Trust & Safety**.
- Leaked or resold links → **Trust & Safety**.

## Resolution Procedure
1. Participant admitted, or time lost recorded for review.
2. Ticket records the blocker and timestamps.
