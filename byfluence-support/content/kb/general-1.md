## Summary
A short, approved explanation of what Byfluence is, for customers who are new to the platform.

## Overview
**Byfluence** (byfluence.com) is an Ethiopian online marketplace where many independent sellers offer their services and products to buyers in one place.

Key points agents can share:
- **Mission:** anyone should be able to start and grow an online business, even without a lot of starting money.
- **What it provides:** shared tools that a small business would otherwise have to build itself — a public profile and listings, online payments, consultation booking, and a trusted place to buy and sell.
- **Who uses it:** **buyers** (who discover, book and pay) and **sellers** (professionals and businesses who list their offers).
- **Available now (MVP):** one-on-one consultations and group consultations with professionals, held online through Google Meet.
- **In development:** physical products, inventory-free selling (reselling approved products) and digital products. These are **planned** — check the Feature Status page before telling a customer any of them is available.
- **Role:** Byfluence is a **marketplace facilitator**. In most cases it does not own or provide the products or services; each seller is responsible for what they offer, under Byfluence's marketplace rules.
- **Language:** Amharic first, English also available.

## Summary
A simple step-by-step explanation of how buying and selling works on Byfluence today, for buyers and for sellers.

## Overview

### For buyers (consultations — available now)
1. **Create an account** on byfluence.com (reg-how-to-create-account).
2. **Find a consultant**: browse or search listings; each shows the consultant's name, description, topic, duration, price and available times.
3. **Choose a date and time** from the consultant's availability (one-on-one), or register for a scheduled **group** session.
4. **Pay** on the platform with an available payment method (pay-supported-methods).
5. **Booking confirmed** after the payment is successful; you receive the **Google Meet** details.
6. **Join** the meeting at the scheduled time.
7. The consultation is marked **completed**.
8. Manage everything in your **dashboard**: upcoming sessions, purchase and transaction history, profile, and support options.

### For sellers
1. Create an account → **apply to become a seller**.
2. Provide personal/business information and submit **identity verification**.
3. Wait for review → **approved** or **rejected**.
4. Complete your **public profile** and create **listings** (price, description, availability).
5. Accept bookings, deliver the service, and monitor transactions in the seller dashboard.
6. **Earnings**: after a customer pays and the service is completed, earnings are recorded as **pending**, then become **available** when release conditions are met; sellers then request a **withdrawal**.

### Coming later (planned — check Feature Status first)
Physical products (initially prioritising Addis Ababa), inventory-free selling (reselling approved supplier products) and digital products.

- Explain the confirmed model (fees/commissions on transactions) in general terms.
- Say that the exact current fee details will be confirmed by the team, and escalate for the approved answer if the customer needs specifics (especially sellers deciding whether to join).

## Summary
Who can become a seller on Byfluence today, what the process involves, and which seller requirements are not yet confirmed.

## Overview
Byfluence wants anyone — individuals, professionals and small businesses — to be able to start selling online without needing their own website, payment system or booking system.

**What sellers can offer today:** one-on-one and group **consultations** (for example professional advice, coaching, tutoring, business or creative services delivered online through Google Meet).

**Planned (check Feature Status before confirming):** physical products, inventory-free selling / reselling approved products, and digital products.

**How to become a seller (confirmed process):**
1. Create a Byfluence account.
2. Apply to become a seller.
3. Provide personal or business information.
4. Submit identity verification.
5. Wait for review — the application is **approved** or **rejected**.
6. Complete the public profile and create listings.
7. Start selling once seller permissions are granted.

Seller statuses: verification (**Pending, Verified, Rejected**) is separate from operational status (**Active, Suspended, Banned**).

**Not confirmed — do not state:** minimum age, required ID documents, whether a business licence or tax registration is required, which professions/topics are allowed or restricted, and whether sellers outside Ethiopia can join.

## Overview
**Confirmed protections in the transaction flow:**
1. **Payments happen on the platform** through payment providers. Byfluence records the payment and its status (Pending, Successful, Failed, Canceled, Refund Pending, Refunded, Disputed).
2. **Bookings are confirmed only after the payment is confirmed** by the provider.
3. **Seller earnings are not immediately withdrawable.** After payment, earnings are calculated and kept **pending** until release conditions are met (e.g., completion, no open dispute, settlement rules). This gives time to handle problems.
4. **Sellers are verified** before they can sell, and can be suspended or banned for rule violations.
5. **Support investigates complaints**: identify the transaction → collect evidence → review policy → contact the seller/department → authorised staff decide → explain and process approved adjustments.
6. **Records**: every booking and transaction is recorded and visible in the customer's dashboard.

**What is NOT confirmed:** refund eligibility, deadlines, deductions and who approves refunds; what happens in each dispute type. The *Refund processing* feature is unconfirmed — check Feature Status.

**Protections only work on-platform.** Payments made directly to a seller (mobile money, bank transfer, cash) are outside Byfluence's records and cannot be investigated in the same way.
