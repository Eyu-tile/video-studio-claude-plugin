## Summary
Explains to a seller how money from a buyer's payment becomes seller earnings, why it is first pending and later available, and how withdrawals fit in. Commission, fee rates, release conditions and payout timelines are not confirmed.

## Overview
The confirmed earnings lifecycle on Byfluence is:

1. **Customer pays** for a consultation, product or service.
2. **Payment is confirmed** by the payment provider and recorded by Byfluence.
3. **Platform fees are calculated** (commission/fees — rates not confirmed).
4. **Seller earnings are calculated** (the amount due to the seller after fees).
5. Earnings are **Pending** until release conditions are met (for example, the consultation is completed and there is no dispute, cancellation or refund — exact conditions not confirmed).
6. Earnings become **Available** — they can be withdrawn.
7. The seller **requests a withdrawal**.
8. Byfluence **processes** the withdrawal.
9. The withdrawal is **recorded as completed** after confirmation.

Balance terms the seller may see in the dashboard:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Total Earnings** | All earnings recorded for the seller over time |
| **Pending Balance** | Earnings not yet released (waiting for completion, dispute window, settlement, etc.) |
| **Available Balance** | Earnings the seller can request to withdraw |
| **Withdrawal Pending** | Amounts the seller has requested that are being processed |
| **Withdrawn Amount** | Amounts already paid out |

> [!NOTE] A successful customer payment does **not** mean the money is immediately withdrawable. Completion, disputes, cancellations, refunds and settlement can all affect when — and whether — earnings become available.

> [!POLICY] Commission and fee rates, release conditions, payout methods, minimum withdrawal amounts and payout schedules are **not confirmed**. Do not quote any of them.

## Customer Steps
1. Open your seller dashboard and go to the earnings section.
2. Review your Total Earnings, Pending Balance and Available Balance.
3. When you have an Available Balance, you can request a withdrawal (see *How to Request a Withdrawal*).
4. Keep your payout details accurate and up to date.

## Summary
A seller asks why their earnings are still "pending" and not "available" to withdraw. Explain the difference, check the specific sales, and never promise a release date.

## Overview
- **Pending Balance**: earnings recorded but not yet released. They are waiting for release conditions such as the consultation being completed, the order being fulfilled, a possible dispute/refund period, or settlement with the payment provider. Exact conditions are **not confirmed**.
- **Available Balance**: earnings released and ready to withdraw.

Common reasons a sale stays pending:
- The consultation has not happened yet, or has not been marked **completed**.
- The order (physical/digital — expansion features) is not yet fulfilled or confirmed received.
- The buyer opened a dispute, requested a refund, or the payment was reversed.
- The booking/order was cancelled.
- The payment is still being settled by the provider.
- The seller account or payouts are on hold/restricted (see *Payouts on Hold / Restricted*).

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Verify the seller's identity (registered email or phone number).
2. Ask which sale(s) the seller is asking about (booking/order IDs, dates).
3. In the admin dashboard, check for each sale: payment status, booking/order status (completed?), any dispute, refund or cancellation, and the earnings status.
4. Check the seller's account status (Verified/Active vs Suspended, any payout hold).
5. Decide:
   - **Session not completed / not marked completed** → explain; if the session did happen but was not marked completed, see consultation completion articles and escalate if needed.
   - **Dispute/refund/cancellation** → explain that the earnings are affected while it is reviewed (do not share buyer details).
   - **Everything looks complete and still pending** → escalate to **Payments & Finance** with the sale IDs.
   - **Account/payout hold** → follow *Payouts on Hold / Restricted*.

## Customer Steps
1. In your seller dashboard, open the earnings section and check which sales are pending.
2. Make sure your consultations are completed and, where the system asks you to, marked as completed.
3. If a sale has been completed for a while and is still pending, contact support with the booking or order number.

## Summary
A seller says a sale (consultation, product) is not reflected in their earnings at all, or their balance dropped unexpectedly. Check whether the buyer's payment was verified and what happened to that sale.

## Issue Description
The seller delivered a consultation or sold an item but cannot see the earnings in Total Earnings or Pending Balance, or the balance is lower than they expected. Sellers are often anxious because this is their income. They may have been told by the buyer "I paid" — which does not mean Byfluence received a verified payment. Some sellers will have arranged payment off-platform and expect Byfluence to reflect it.

## Possible Causes
- The buyer's payment was never completed or verified (pending/failed).
- The booking/order is attached to a different seller account (seller has two accounts).
- The sale was cancelled, refunded or reversed (chargeback), so earnings were removed or reduced.
- A dispute is open and earnings are held.
- Earnings were recorded but the seller is looking at Available instead of Pending.
- Earnings were already withdrawn (part of a previous withdrawal).
- The buyer paid off-platform (no Byfluence earnings will exist).
- A system or calculation error.

## Information to Collect
- Seller identity (registered email or phone number) and seller profile name
- Booking/order IDs of the missing sale(s), dates, buyer name as shown on the booking
- Amount the seller expected
- Screenshot of the earnings section showing the issue
- Whether the buyer paid through the Byfluence checkout (as far as the seller knows)

> [!WARNING] Never ask for passwords, PINs or OTP codes. Do not share buyer payment references or personal data with the seller.

## Troubleshooting Steps
1. Verify the seller's identity.
2. Find the booking/order in the admin dashboard and confirm it belongs to this seller account.
3. Check the buyer payment status for that booking/order (**do not** share payment references with the seller):
   - **Successful (verified)** → go to step 4.
   - **Pending/Failed/none** → explain there is no confirmed payment for this sale yet, so no earnings were recorded.
4. Check the earnings record for the sale: recorded? status pending/available/withdrawn? any refund, reversal, cancellation or dispute?
5. Decide:
   - **Recorded as pending** → explain (*Pending vs Available*).
   - **Already withdrawn** → show which withdrawal it was part of.
   - **Refunded/reversed/cancelled** → explain neutrally that the sale was adjusted; see *Seller Disputes a Payout or Deduction* if they disagree.
   - **Dispute open** → explain the sale is under review.
   - **Successful payment but no earnings record** → escalate to **Payments & Finance**.
   - **Seller says buyer paid them directly** → explain off-platform payments are not recorded or protected; flag to **Trust & Safety** per *Seller Asks Buyer to Pay Outside Byfluence*.
6. Record findings.

- [ ] Seller identity verified
- [ ] Booking/order matched to seller account
- [ ] Buyer payment status checked
- [ ] Earnings record status checked
- [ ] Outcome explained or escalated
