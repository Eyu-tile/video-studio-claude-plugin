# How do I clear my browser's cache and cookies?
id: faq-gen-6
type: faq
category: technical
subcategory: browser-device
audience: both
priority: low
department: support_l1
tags: clear cache, clear cookies, browser data, ካሽ ማጽዳት, ኩኪ
related: tec-clear-cache-cookies, tec-tsg-browser-basics
policy_pending: no

## Answer (EN)
In Chrome on Android: open byfluence.com → tap the icon to the left of the address → "Cookies and site data" (or "Site settings") → "Delete" → reload the page and log in again.
In Opera Mini: Menu → Settings → Clear browsing data → tick cookies and cached files → Clear.
On iPhone: Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data.
Note: you will be logged out, so make sure you know your login details first.

## Answer (AM)
በAndroid ስልክ Chrome ላይ፦ byfluence.com ይክፈቱ → ከአድራሻው በስተግራ ያለውን ምልክት ይጫኑ → "Cookies and site data" (ወይም "Site settings") → "Delete" → ገጹን እንደገና ከፍተው ይግቡ።
በOpera Mini ላይ፦ ምናሌ → Settings → Clear browsing data → cookies እና cached files ምልክት ያድርጉ → Clear።
በiPhone ላይ፦ Settings → Safari → Clear History and Website Data።
ማሳሰቢያ፦ ከአካውንትዎ ስለሚወጡ በመጀመሪያ የመግቢያ መረጃዎን እንደሚያውቁ ያረጋግጡ።

## Answer (EN)
For your security, Byfluence may log you out after a period of inactivity. Frequent logouts usually happen when the site is opened inside Telegram or Facebook, when private mode or a phone "cleaner" app deletes cookies, or when your phone's date and time are wrong. Open byfluence.com directly in Chrome or Safari, keep your phone's date and time automatic, and log in again. If you get login codes you didn't request, contact us immediately.

If Amharic shows as boxes or symbols, open byfluence.com in an updated Chrome, turn off Opera Mini's "Extreme" mode, and choose "Show original" if your browser is translating the page.

## Answer (EN)
Byfluence shows times in the international clock format, not in Ethiopian local time. For example, 3:00 PM on Byfluence is ከሰዓት 9 ሰዓት, and 7:00 PM is ምሽት 1 ሰዓት. If you are outside Ethiopia, contact us and we will help you confirm your session time.
