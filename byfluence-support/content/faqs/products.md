## Answer (EN)
Physical products (such as clothing, electronics, accessories, beauty, home and lifestyle items) are part of Byfluence's planned expansion. Availability is being rolled out step by step. type: faq
category: physical-products
subcategory: delivery
audience: buyer
priority: low
department: support_l1
features: physical-products, delivery-addis
tags: address, landmark, delivery address tips, ስልክ ቁጥር, አድራሻ, መለያ ምልክት
related: phy-incorrect-address, phy-how-to-order

## Answer (EN)
Please include: the recipient's name, a phone number that will be switched on, your sub-city, woreda and neighbourhood, and a clear landmark nearby (for example a well-known church, mosque, school, shop or building), plus the building name and floor if relevant. Double-check the phone number before you submit — it is the most common cause of missed deliveries.

## Answer (AM)
እባክዎ የሚከተሉትን ያካትቱ፦ የተቀባዩን ስም፣ ክፍት የሚሆን ስልክ ቁጥር፣ ክፍለ ከተማ፣ ወረዳ እና ሰፈር፣ እንዲሁም በአቅራቢያዎ ያለ ግልጽ መለያ ምልክት (ለምሳሌ የታወቀ ቤተ ክርስቲያን፣ መስጊድ፣ ትምህርት ቤት፣ ሱቅ ወይም ሕንፃ)፣ አስፈላጊ ከሆነም የሕንፃውን ስም እና ፎቅ። ከማስገባትዎ በፊት ስልክ ቁጥሩን ደግመው ያረጋግጡ — ዕቃ ሳይደርስ የሚቀርበት ዋነኛ ምክንያት ነው።

category: physical-products

priority: medium
department: support_l1
features: physical-products

related: phy-order-payment, phy-contact-seller

## Answer (EN)
Please pay only through the payment options shown in the Byfluence checkout. Money sent directly to a seller's personal account, or cash given to a delivery person when that option is not shown in your checkout, is outside Byfluence's records and cannot be verified or protected by us. If anyone asks you to pay outside Byfluence, please report it to support.

## Answer (AM)
እባክዎ በ Byfluence የክፍያ ገጽ (checkout) ላይ በሚታዩት የክፍያ አማራጮች ብቻ ይክፈሉ። በቀጥታ ወደ ሻጩ የግል ሂሳብ የሚላክ ገንዘብ፣ ወይም አማራጩ በክፍያ ገጽዎ ላይ ሳይታይ ለአድራሹ የሚሰጥ ጥሬ ገንዘብ ከ Byfluence መዝገብ ውጭ ነው፤ ልናረጋግጠውም ሆነ ልንጠብቀው አንችልም። ማንም ሰው ከ Byfluence ውጭ እንዲከፍሉ ከጠየቀዎት፣ እባክዎ ለድጋፍ ቡድናችን ያሳውቁ።

## Internal Notes
Cash on delivery is not confirmed. Report off-platform payment requests to Trust & Safety.

## Answer (EN)
Contact Byfluence Support as soon as possible with your order number. Before you do anything else, take clear photos or a short video of the package (all sides and the delivery label), the item and the problem. Keep the item and all packaging as they are, and do not send anything back until we confirm the next steps. Recording a short video while you open the package is very helpful.

# My order says delivered but I didn't receive it. What should I do?
id: faq-prd-8
type: faq
category: physical-products
subcategory: delivery
audience: buyer
priority: high
department: fulfillment
features: physical-products, delivery-addis
tags: marked delivered, not received, never arrived, ደርሷል ይላል, አልደረሰኝም
related: phy-not-delivered-marked-delivered, phy-incorrect-address

## Answer (EN)
First, check with anyone who could have received it at your address — family members, a guard, neighbours or reception — and check for missed calls. If you still cannot find it, contact Byfluence Support right away with your order number. We will request the delivery record and investigate. Never share a PIN or verification code with a delivery person over the phone.

በመጀመሪያ በአድራሻዎ ዕቃውን ሊቀበሉ የሚችሉ ሰዎችን — የቤተሰብ አባላት፣ ጥበቃ፣ ጎረቤቶች ወይም እንግዳ ተቀባይ — ይጠይቁ፤ ያመለጡ ጥሪዎችንም ይመልከቱ። አሁንም ካላገኙት በትዕዛዝ ቁጥርዎ ወዲያውኑ Byfluence ድጋፍን ያግኙ። የማድረሻ መዝገቡን ጠይቀን እናጣራለን። ፒን ወይም የማረጋገጫ ኮድ በስልክ ለአድራሽ በፍጹም አያጋሩ።
