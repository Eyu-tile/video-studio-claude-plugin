# I didn't get my verification code. What should I do?
id: faq-acc-3
type: faq
category: registration
subcategory: verification-otp
audience: both
priority: medium
department: support_l1
features: accounts, notifications-sms, notifications-email
tags: no code, otp not received, verification code missing, sms not coming, ኮድ አልደረሰኝም, ኦቲፒ
related: reg-otp-not-received, reg-otp-invalid-expired, tec-sms-not-received

## Answer (EN)
First, check that the phone number or email you entered is correct. For SMS, make sure your phone has signal, check the right SIM card, and look in blocked or spam messages. For email, check Spam or Promotions. Then wait a few minutes and request just one new code — only the newest code works. If it still doesn't arrive, contact us. Never share your code with anyone, including people who say they are from Byfluence.

## Answer (AM)
በመጀመሪያ ያስገቡት ስልክ ቁጥር ወይም ኢሜይል ትክክል መሆኑን ያረጋግጡ። ለኤስኤምኤስ፣ ስልክዎ ኔትወርክ እንዳለው እና ትክክለኛውን ሲም ካርድ እንደሚጠቀሙ ያረጋግጡ፤ የታገዱ ወይም ስፓም መልዕክቶችን ይመልከቱ። ለኢሜይል፣ Spam ወይም Promotions ፎልደርን ይመልከቱ። ከዚያም ጥቂት ደቂቃዎች ጠብቀው አንድ አዲስ ኮድ ብቻ ይጠይቁ — የሚሰራው የቅርብ ጊዜው ኮድ ብቻ ነው። አሁንም ካልደረሰ ያግኙን። ኮድዎን ለማንም አያጋሩ፤ ከByfluence ነን ለሚሉ ሰዎችም ጭምር።

Verification links and codes only work for a limited time, and only the newest one works. Request a new link or code, then use it soon after it arrives. ## Answer (EN)
You don't need to create a separate account to sell. Everyone starts with a regular Byfluence account. To sell, log in and apply to become a seller from your account; you'll provide some personal or business information and complete identity verification, and our team will review your application. Please don't open a second account just for selling.
