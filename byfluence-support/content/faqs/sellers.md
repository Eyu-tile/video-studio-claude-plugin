## Answer (EN)
Sign in to your Byfluence account (or create one), open the seller application, fill in your personal or business details and complete identity verification on the platform. Our Seller Operations team reviews your application. Once you are verified, complete your seller profile and create your first listing.

# What is the difference between "Verified" and "Suspended"?
id: faq-sel-7
type: faq
category: seller-account
subcategory: seller-status
audience: seller
features: seller-verification
tags: verified vs suspended, seller status, account status, ተረጋግጧል, ታግዷል, የመለያ ሁኔታ
related: sel-identity-verification-process, sel-suspension, sel-account-restrictions
policy_pending: no

## Answer (EN)
They are two separate statuses. Verification (Pending, Verified or Rejected) shows whether your identity has been confirmed. Your account status (Active, Suspended or Banned) shows whether you are currently allowed to sell. So it is possible to be Verified but Suspended — your identity is confirmed, but selling is paused while a review or decision is in place.

## Answer (AM)
እነዚህ ሁለት የተለያዩ ሁኔታዎች ናቸው። ማረጋገጫ (Pending፣ Verified ወይም Rejected) ማንነትዎ መረጋገጡን ያሳያል። የመለያዎ ሁኔታ (Active፣ Suspended ወይም Banned) ደግሞ በአሁኑ ጊዜ መሸጥ ይፈቀድልዎት እንደሆነ ያሳያል። ስለዚህ "Verified" ሆኖ "Suspended" መሆን ይቻላል — ማንነትዎ ተረጋግጧል፣ ነገር ግን ግምገማ ወይም ውሳኔ እስኪጠናቀቅ መሸጥ ለጊዜው ቆሟል።

## Internal Notes
If a seller asks this because they are suspended, move to `sel-suspension` for the full process.
