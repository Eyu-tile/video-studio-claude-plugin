## Answer (EN)
The payment methods available to you are shown on the checkout page when you pay. Byfluence works with trusted payment providers, and available options may change over time. Always pay through the Byfluence checkout page only — never to a personal phone number or bank account. If you are not sure which options are available, contact Byfluence Support and we will tell you.

# Will Byfluence ever ask for my PIN or verification code?
id: faq-pay-2
type: faq
category: payments
subcategory: payment-security
audience: both
priority: medium
department: trust_safety
tags: PIN, OTP, verification code, scam, is it safe, ፒን, ኮድ, ማጭበርበር
related: pay-payment-security, pay-off-platform-payment, tns-report-fraud
policy_pending: no

## Answer (EN)
No — never. Byfluence will never ask for your PIN, password, OTP/verification code, card number or CVV, by phone, SMS, Telegram or email. Enter your PIN only in your own Telebirr, M-Pesa or bank app, or in a payment prompt you started yourself. If anyone asks for these details — even if they say they are from Byfluence or your bank — do not share them, and report it to Byfluence Support. If you already shared a PIN or code, contact your mobile-money provider or bank immediately through their official channels and change your PIN.

## Answer (AM)
በፍጹም አይጠይቅም። Byfluence ፒንዎን፣ የይለፍ ቃልዎን፣ የማረጋገጫ ኮድ (OTP)፣ የካርድ ቁጥርዎን ወይም CVV በስልክ፣ በኤስኤምኤስ፣ በቴሌግራም ወይም በኢሜይል በፍጹም አይጠይቅም። ፒንዎን የሚያስገቡት በራስዎ የ Telebirr፣ M-Pesa ወይም የባንክ መተግበሪያ ውስጥ፣ ወይም እርስዎ በጀመሩት የክፍያ መልዕክት ላይ ብቻ ይሁን። ማንም ሰው — ከ Byfluence ወይም ከባንክዎ ነኝ ቢልም — እነዚህን ዝርዝሮች ከጠየቀዎት አያጋሩ፤ ለ Byfluence ድጋፍ ሪፖርት ያድርጉ። አስቀድመው ፒን ወይም ኮድ አጋርተው ከሆነ፣ የሞባይል ገንዘብ አቅራቢዎን ወይም ባንክዎን በይፋዊ መንገዶቻቸው ወዲያውኑ አነጋግረው ፒንዎን ይቀይሩ።

related: pay-failed-transaction, pay-pending-transaction, pay-paid-booking-not-confirmed

## Answer (EN)
Please don't pay again yet. Sometimes a failed payment shows as a temporary hold on your account, and sometimes the payment actually went through but the confirmation was delayed. Contact Byfluence Support with the transaction reference from your SMS or receipt, the time of payment and what you were paying for. We will check our records and tell you whether a payment was received. If no payment was received by Byfluence, any held amount is handled by your bank or payment provider.

## Answer (EN)
No, please don't pay again while a payment is pending — you could be charged twice. "Pending" means we are waiting for the final result from the payment provider. First, check that you approved the payment on your phone if you were asked to. If it stays pending, contact Byfluence Support with your transaction reference and payment time, and we will check it for you.

# Where do I find my transaction reference?
id: faq-pay-7

tags: transaction reference, transaction ID, receipt, Telebirr SMS, bank SMS, የግብይት ቁጥር, ደረሰኝ
related: pay-transaction-reference-help, pay-payment-verification, pay-chapa

## Answer (EN)
The transaction reference is a code of letters and numbers your payment provider gives you after you pay. Look in the confirmation SMS or receipt for a code labelled "Transaction", "Trans ID" or "Ref". For Chapa, check the success page or receipt. For Telebirr or M-Pesa, check the confirmation SMS or the transaction history in the app. For bank payments, check the bank SMS or the transaction details in your banking app. Send only that code to support — never your PIN or verification codes.

## Answer (AM)
የግብይት መለያ ቁጥር (transaction reference) ከከፈሉ በኋላ የክፍያ አቅራቢዎ የሚሰጥዎ የፊደላትና የቁጥሮች ኮድ ነው። በማረጋገጫ ኤስኤምኤሱ ወይም በደረሰኙ ላይ "Transaction"፣ "Trans ID" ወይም "Ref" የሚል ምልክት ያለበትን ኮድ ይፈልጉ። ለ Chapa፣ የስኬት ገጹን ወይም ደረሰኙን ይመልከቱ። ለ Telebirr ወይም M-Pesa፣ የማረጋገጫ ኤስኤምኤሱን ወይም በመተግበሪያው ውስጥ ያለውን የግብይት ታሪክ ይመልከቱ። ለባንክ ክፍያ፣ የባንኩን ኤስኤምኤስ ወይም በባንክ መተግበሪያዎ ያለውን የግብይት ዝርዝር ይመልከቱ። ለድጋፍ የሚልኩት ያንን ኮድ ብቻ ይሁን — ፒንዎን ወይም የማረጋገጫ ኮድ በፍጹም አይላኩ።

# Why isn't my payment screenshot enough to confirm my booking?
id: faq-pay-8
type: faq
category: payments
subcategory: verification-references
audience: buyer
priority: low
department: support_l1
tags: screenshot, payment proof, receipt not accepted, verification, ስክሪንሾት, የክፍያ ማስረጃ
related: pay-payment-verification, pay-fake-payment-screenshots, pay-transaction-reference-help
policy_pending: no

## Answer (EN)
To protect every customer and seller, Byfluence confirms payments by matching the transaction reference with our payment records, not by screenshots. Screenshots are helpful, but they can be mistaken (for example, showing a different or pending payment), so we always check the reference too.
