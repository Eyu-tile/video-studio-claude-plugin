# How do I book a consultation?
id: faq-con-1
type: faq
category: consultations

audience: buyer

department: support_l1
features: consultations-1on1
tags: how to book, book consultation, schedule session, ቀጠሮ ማስያዝ, ምክክር ማስያዝ

## Answer (EN)
Open the consultant's listing, check the topic, duration and price, choose an available date and time, and complete the payment. Your booking is confirmed once the payment is successful, and you'll find it — with the Google Meet details — in your dashboard under your consultation bookings. Times are shown in East Africa Time (EAT) using the international clock.

የአማካሪውን ገጽ ይክፈቱ፤ ርዕሱን፣ የቆይታ ጊዜውን እና ዋጋውን ይመልከቱ፤ ክፍት ቀን እና ሰዓት ይምረጡ፤ ከዚያ ክፍያውን ያጠናቅቁ። ክፍያው ሲሳካ ቀጠሮዎ ይረጋገጣል፤ ቀጠሮዎንም ከGoogle Meet መረጃው ጋር በዳሽቦርድዎ ውስጥ በምክክር ቀጠሮዎች ክፍል ያገኙታል። ሰዓቶቹ በምሥራቅ አፍሪካ ሰዓት (EAT) እና በፈረንጅ አቆጣጠር ይታያሉ።

# What time zone are consultation times shown in?
id: faq-con-4
type: faq
category: consultations
subcategory: meeting-access
audience: both
priority: low
department: support_l1
features: consultations-1on1, consultations-group
tags: time zone, Ethiopian time, EAT, ሰዓት አቆጣጠር, በኢትዮጵያ ሰዓት, በፈረንጅ ሰዓት
related: con-time-zone-confusion, con-select-time-slot, grp-scheduling-time-zones, tec-calendar-time-display
policy_pending: yes

## Answer (EN)
Consultation times are in East Africa Time (EAT, UTC+3), using the international clock with AM/PM. If you use the Ethiopian clock, remember the difference: 9:00 AM is ጠዋት 3 ሰዓት, 12:00 PM is ቀን 6 ሰዓት, 2:00 PM is ከሰዓት 8 ሰዓት, and 8:00 PM is ማታ 2 ሰዓት. Dates are Gregorian calendar dates — check the weekday too. If you are outside Ethiopia, convert from EAT to your local time. When in doubt, ask us and we'll confirm.

## Answer (AM)
የምክክር ሰዓቶች በምሥራቅ አፍሪካ ሰዓት (EAT፣ UTC+3) እና በፈረንጅ (ዓለም አቀፍ) አቆጣጠር ከAM/PM ጋር ይታያሉ። በኢትዮጵያ አቆጣጠር የሚጠቀሙ ከሆነ ልዩነቱን ያስታውሱ፦ 9:00 AM = ጠዋት 3 ሰዓት፣ 12:00 PM = ቀን 6 ሰዓት፣ 2:00 PM = ከሰዓት 8 ሰዓት፣ 8:00 PM = ማታ 2 ሰዓት። ቀኖቹ በፈረንጅ (Gregorian) አቆጣጠር ናቸው — የሳምንቱን ቀንም ያረጋግጡ። ከኢትዮጵያ ውጭ ከሆኑ ከEAT ወደ አካባቢዎ ሰዓት ይቀይሩ። ጥርጣሬ ካለዎት ይጠይቁን፤ እናረጋግጥልዎታለን።

- Confirm that the platform always displays EAT (not the device's time zone) and uses Gregorian dates.

# What is a group consultation?
id: faq-con-10
type: faq

audience: buyer
priority: low

features: consultations-group

## Answer (EN)
A group consultation is a session where one professional meets several participants together on Google Meet — like a workshop or Q&A. The consultant sets the date, time, duration, price and number of places. ## Answer (EN)
In your seller dashboard, open your consultation listing or availability settings and add the days and times you can attend.
