related: ref-how-to-request-refund, ref-eligibility, ref-evidence-collection

## Answer (EN)
Contact Byfluence Support through our official channel ({{support_channel}}) and share:
1. Your order or booking ID, or the transaction reference from your payment receipt or SMS
2. A short description of what went wrong
3. Any screenshots or photos that show the problem

You will receive a case number. Our authorized team reviews every refund request, including information from the seller, and we will keep you updated. Please never share your password, PIN or verification codes — we will never ask for them.

## Answer (AM)
በይፋዊ መስመራችን ({{support_channel}}) የByfluence ድጋፍን ያግኙ እና የሚከተሉትን ያጋሩ፦
1. የትዕዛዝ ወይም የቀጠሮ መለያ ቁጥርዎን፣ ወይም ከክፍያ ደረሰኝዎ ወይም ከኤስኤምኤስ ላይ ያለውን የግብይት መለያ ቁጥር
2. የተፈጠረውን ችግር በአጭሩ
3. ችግሩን የሚያሳዩ ስክሪንሾቶች ወይም ፎቶዎች ካሉ

የጉዳይ ቁጥር ይሰጥዎታል። እያንዳንዱን የተመላሽ ገንዘብ ጥያቄ ስልጣን ያለው ቡድናችን የሻጩንም መረጃ ጨምሮ ይገመግማል፤ ሂደቱንም እናሳውቅዎታለን። እባክዎ የይለፍ ቃልዎን፣ ፒንዎን ወይም የማረጋገጫ ኮዶችን በፍጹም አያጋሩ — በፍጹም አንጠይቅዎትም።

# Will I get a refund? What is the refund policy?
id: faq-ref-2
type: faq
category: refunds-disputes
subcategory: refund-requests
audience: both
features: refunds
tags: refund policy, am i eligible, refund rules, can i get my money back, የተመላሽ ገንዘብ ፖሊሲ, ብቁ ነኝ
related: ref-eligibility, ref-how-to-request-refund, con-refund-eligibility

## Answer (EN)
Every refund request at Byfluence is reviewed individually by our authorized team. They look at what happened, the evidence from both the buyer and the seller, and the type of purchase (consultation, product or digital product). Because of this, our support agents cannot confirm in advance whether a refund will be approved or how much it will be. What we can do is make sure your request is complete so it is reviewed fairly.

## Answer (AM)
በByfluence እያንዳንዱ የተመላሽ ገንዘብ ጥያቄ ስልጣን ባለው ቡድናችን በተናጠል ይገመገማል። ቡድኑ የተፈጠረውን ሁኔታ፣ ከገዢውም ከሻጩም የቀረበውን ማስረጃ እና የግዢውን ዓይነት (ምክክር፣ ዕቃ ወይም ዲጂታል ምርት) ይመለከታል። በዚህ ምክንያት የድጋፍ ሰራተኞቻችን ተመላሽ ገንዘብ ይፈቀድ እንደሆነ ወይም መጠኑን አስቀድመው ማረጋገጥ አይችሉም። እኛ ማድረግ የምንችለው ጥያቄዎ በፍትሃዊነት እንዲገመገም ሙሉ መረጃ ይዞ መቅረቡን ማረጋገጥ ነው።

No official refund policy is approved yet. Never quote deadlines, percentages or fees. See `ref-eligibility`.

related: ref-dispute-investigation, ref-final-decision-communication, ref-tsg-dispute-handling-master

## Answer (EN)
Refund decisions are made by Byfluence's authorized team, not by the support agent you are chatting with. Your support agent collects the information from you, asks the seller for their side, and passes the complete case to the authorized team. Once a decision is made, your agent will explain it to you and tell you what happens next.

## Answer (EN)
It depends on two things: how long the review of your case takes, and — if a refund is approved — how long your payment provider takes to return the money to your account. We will tell you when your refund has been approved and again when it has been processed on our side. If it has been processed but you don't see it, contact us with your case number and we will check it with our payments team.

To protect your money, refunds are returned to the original payment method you used for the purchase. Support agents cannot change the destination of a refund through chat. id: faq-ref-6
type: faq
category: refunds-disputes
subcategory: decisions
audience: both
features: refunds

related: ref-evidence-collection, ref-buyer-seller-disagreement, ref-how-to-request-refund

## Answer (EN)
Send only what shows the problem, for example:
- **Consultation**: a screenshot of the Google Meet screen showing the time, and any messages with the consultant
- **Physical product**: clear photos of the item, the damage and the packaging
- **Digital product**: a screenshot of the error, and the device you used
- **Payment**: the transaction reference from your receipt or SMS

Send original photos or screenshots without cropping or editing, with the date and time visible if possible. You may hide unrelated personal information. Never send your password, PIN, verification codes or card numbers.

## Answer (AM)
ችግሩን የሚያሳየውን ብቻ ይላኩ፤ ለምሳሌ፦

- **ቁሳዊ ዕቃ**፦ የዕቃውን፣ የጉዳቱን እና የማሸጊያውን ግልጽ ፎቶዎች
- **ዲጂታል ምርት**፦ የስህተቱን ስክሪንሾት እና የተጠቀሙበትን መሣሪያ
- **ክፍያ**፦ ከደረሰኝዎ ወይም ከኤስኤምኤስ ላይ ያለውን የግብይት መለያ ቁጥር

ያልተቆረጡ እና ያልተስተካከሉ የመጀመሪያ ፎቶዎችን ወይም ስክሪንሾቶችን ይላኩ፤ ከተቻለ ቀን እና ሰዓት ይታይ። ከጉዳዩ ጋር ያልተያያዘ የግል መረጃን መደበቅ ይችላሉ። የይለፍ ቃልዎን፣ ፒንዎን፣ የማረጋገጫ ኮዶችን ወይም የካርድ ቁጥሮችን በፍጹም አይላኩ።

related: tns-account-compromise, tns-unauthorized-transaction, acc-unauthorized-access

## Answer (EN)
Act quickly:
1. If you can still log in, change your Byfluence password to a new one you don't use anywhere else.
2. Change the password of the email account linked to Byfluence.
3. Contact Byfluence Support right away and tell us what you noticed (changed details, bookings, orders or withdrawals you didn't make).
4. If you see payments you didn't make, contact your bank or mobile-money provider too.

Never share your password, verification codes or PIN with anyone — including anyone who says they are from Byfluence. Our security team will contact you only through your registered contact details.
