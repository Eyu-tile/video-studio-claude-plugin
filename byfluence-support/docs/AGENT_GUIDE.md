# Support Employee Guide

You can learn this system in about 15 minutes. Training modules 1–10 (Training Center) cover the rest.

## The daily loop

1. **Start your shift.** Open the Dashboard. Read any red or orange announcements, check **My follow-ups**, then check **Urgent issues**. The full routine is in *SOP: Starting a support shift*.
2. **A customer writes.** Press **Ctrl K** (or `/`) and type the problem in the customer's words, e.g. *"paid but seller didn't join"*. Search understands synonyms, Amharic keywords and typos.
3. **Open the article.** Read it in this order:
   - **Possible Causes**
   - **Information to Collect**: never ask for passwords, OTP codes, PINs or card numbers
   - **Troubleshooting Steps**: you can tick the checklists while you work
   - **Customer responses**: choose Amharic or English, then Recommended, Short (Telegram) or Follow-up, and press **Copy**. Use **Fill placeholders** to insert the customer's name and references.
4. **Create the ticket.** Click **Create ticket for this issue** on the article. Category, priority and the linked article are filled in for you. Add the customer's details and a description.
5. **Work the ticket** (Customer Issues → open the ticket):
   - **Internal note:** what you checked and found. Only employees see it.
   - **Log communication:** record every message, call or failed call attempt to the customer or the seller. Nothing is sent from the system, so you send messages on Telegram, email or phone yourself.
   - **Use template:** fills a template with the ticket's data. After sending it, click **I sent it — log on ticket**.
   - **Status:** move the ticket to *Under Review* while investigating, or *Waiting for Customer* / *Waiting for Seller* when you need their reply.
   - **Follow-up:** set a reminder. Due and overdue follow-ups appear on the Dashboard and under the bell icon.
   - **Attach files:** screenshots and receipts. They stay private to employees.
6. **Escalate** when the article's *Escalation Conditions* say so, or when you can't resolve it at your level. Choose the level and department and explain what you checked. The ticket stays *Escalated* until a decision is recorded, so don't promise the customer an outcome before then.
7. **Resolve.** Record the root cause, the actions taken and the final resolution, and confirm whether the customer was told. You can't resolve while an escalation is still open.
8. **Close** after the customer confirms, or with a documented reason (duplicate, no response…).
9. **Reopen** if the customer comes back about the same problem. Don't create a new ticket. The history is kept.

## Rules that always apply

- Never ask for, or write down, passwords, OTP or verification codes, PINs or full card numbers. The system warns you if you type something that looks like one.
- Never promise refunds, timelines, fees or outcomes. Amber **Policy Pending Approval** boxes mark rules management hasn't decided yet. For those, collect evidence and escalate to Senior Support.
- Check **Policies & Guidelines → Feature Status** before telling a customer that a feature or payment method is available. Physical products, delivery, digital products, inventory-free selling and most payment methods are **not confirmed as live**.
- Give session times as **"2:00 PM EAT (8 o'clock Ethiopian time)"**. Mixing up the two clocks is a common cause of missed sessions.
- Only mark a payment as verified after matching the **transaction reference** in the records. A screenshot alone is not proof.

## Finding your tickets

On the **Customer Issues** page:

- **Views (tabs):** My Assigned, Unassigned (team queue: click **Assign to me**), New, Under Review, Waiting…, Escalated, Overdue, Resolved, Closed.
- **Search:** ticket ID, customer name, phone, email, order or transaction ID.
- **Board view:** drag cards between status columns. Dropping a card on Resolved, Closed or Escalated opens the form that column requires.

## Improving the knowledge base

If an article is wrong or missing something, use **Report outdated** or **Suggest an edit** in the article's sidebar. Supervisors review these. Searches that return nothing are reported automatically as knowledge gaps.
